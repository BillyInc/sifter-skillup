### Adjacent authoritative source encountered, excluded from closure

The search engine also surfaced the National Commission for Colleges of Education’s official [Languages minimum-standard PDF](https://ncce.gov.ng/NCCE-Digitization/minstandard/new_cul_pdf/languages.pdf), printed pp.131–135 (accessed 2026-09-26). It contains an NCE L1 Fulfulde sequence and subject-specific course descriptions, including history/development, grammar, reading, oral/written literature, Ajami writing, linguistics, phonology, morphology/syntax, prose, translation theory/practice, composition, drama, poetry, and dialectology. This is an authoritative Fulfulde course/outcome source for the **NCE teacher-education** context. It is not a UNIMAID BA curriculum, is not issued by UNIMAID or NUC, and cannot establish either the standalone BA’s required sequence or its equivalence to the distinct BA Language and Linguistics (Fulfulde). It is therefore recorded only as an adjacent evidence lead and is **not used to close or populate row 303**.
