## Finding

The evidence supports **both** of the following, without contradiction:

1. **Row 163 is a single, exact JAMB identity/cell** named `Physics/Industrial Physics`, offered by BENIN and UNIZIK in the p. 481 Sciences table.
2. The p. 490 table separately lists `Physics and Applied Physics` (BAUCHI) and `Industrial Physics` (the 12 institutions above), with additional distinct Physics titles. These are other offerings/cells, not the components from which row 163 was reconstructed.

Therefore, the earlier hypothesis that row 163 itself was created by collapsing the p. 490 `Physics and Applied Physics` and `Industrial Physics` offerings is **retracted**. BENIN’s appearance in both the p. 481 slash-title cell and p. 490 Industrial Physics cell represents two separately named JAMB offerings in separate cells; it is not evidence that the cells should be merged. The matrix’s row-163 source-conflict status should now be read as resolved by A05 and this checkpoint. The local canonical registry has not been edited.
