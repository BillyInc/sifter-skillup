# Taxonomy specialization audit — lane D002–D056

Checkpoint 002 | 2026-10-03 | Final domain-by-domain research disposition for this lane; append-only successor to checkpoint 001.

## Scope, method, and count correction

This is a read-only taxonomy audit. It assesses parent-domain fit, student-facing specialization boundaries, label clarity, overlap, and whether flat/single-area structures are justified. It does not assess any role-to-domain relationship, infer fit from ISCO or subgroup membership, add roles, create student-item relationships, or edit canonical data. Recommendations below are proposals for the user/local taxonomy owner to reconcile.

I checked all 55 IDs D002–D056 against the current domain names/descriptions and subgroup labels in `data/career-mapping/domain_meta.json` and `domain_structure.json`, then compared them with professional, standards, accreditation, and public-sector frameworks listed in the source register below. A framework supports only the specific curricular/practice boundary named in its locator; it does not prove local labor demand or validate every taxonomy label.

Correction to checkpoint 001: the verified subgroup inventory is **168** rows, not 167. There are **4 one-subgroup domains** (D002, D003, D043, D056), **0 flat/no-subgroup domains**, and **51 domains with 2+ subgroups**. The D002–D056 audit is complete: **55/55 domains**. The earlier arithmetic error is preserved in checkpoint 001 and corrected here rather than silently rewritten.

## Decision vocabulary

- **Keep/refine**: the current conceptual boundary is supported, but wording or scope needs clarification.
- **Restructure**: subgroup set is substantially job-level/ISCO-shaped, overlapping, or cross-domain; replace with topical specialization areas after local reconciliation.
- **Flatten**: no current subgroup is a defensible, distinct student learning specialization; route directly from domain foundation to role (if separately supported) until genuine specializations are evidenced.
- **Split/add**: a single group collapses well-supported, learnably distinct areas. Suggestions are areas of study, not invented occupations.
- Confidence reflects the strength of evidence for the taxonomy disposition, not the truth of every proposed label.
