## Scope and denominator

Active mappings use the same population definition as the catalogue audit: `role_library_v3_cleaned.json` plus `_role_overlay_loader.load_overlay_roles()`, deduplicated last-write-wins on `(domain_id, role_name)`, in the inclusive range D029–D056. This gives **915 role→domain mappings**: 907 cleaned-library rows plus 8 overlay rows before key deduplication. There are 18 populated domains and 10 domains with zero current mappings: D037, D038, D039, D040, D045, D049, D050, D052, D053, D054.

**Reviewed in this checkpoint:** D029 and D030, 62/915 mappings. **Remaining:** 853/915. No subgroup/ISCO membership was used as proof of role→domain or role→specialization fit. The role-task basis is the checked-in ESCO occupation record description; each table row points to the exact ESCO occupation record URL. Role descriptions are shortened in the local cleaned library, so for close decisions the linked ESCO task profile is the source to inspect, not the truncation itself.

Dispositions mean: **Pass** = current parent-domain mapping is supported by the stated work; **Revise/reassign** = task output belongs elsewhere or current domain is too broad; **Unresolved** = domain mismatch is apparent, but this catalogue slice does not support a safe single destination. For specialization, “direct domain→role” means no existing specialization relationship is independently supported; it is not a negative judgement about the role.
