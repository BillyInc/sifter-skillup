# Domain display-label source audit checkpoint — 2026-09-25

## Scope and method

Audited every current display label in `data/career-mapping/domain_meta.json` against its source definition, original source-library identity and lineage, and recorded industry/occupational scope. The 2026-09-25 subgroup naming audit was used to locate records only. Source names and locators were cross-checked against `domain_source_traceability_2026-08-31.json`; the scope text below is the checked-in domain definition. The 166 original library records and two later curated additions are distinguished explicitly. This is a display-label review only: no role labels, mappings, JSON, or source records were changed.

Coverage: 168/168 domains. Decisions: KEEP 137; REFINE 20; UNCERTAIN 11.

`KEEP` means the current concise label is supported by the available definition and lineage. `REFINE` gives a simple candidate name that makes a clearly stated material scope visible. `UNCERTAIN` means the evidence is missing or conflicting; no replacement is guessed. Scope and reason cells are intentionally per-domain.
