# Domain student-label audit — 2026-10-03

## Scope and outcome

Reviewed the complete 168-domain catalogue in `data/career-mapping/domain_meta.json` against the same-ID descriptions and the source-title/identity record in `_audit/domain_naming_provenance_2026-08-31.json`. Refined **77 domain labels** for plain-language readability and scope coverage. Stable IDs, clusters, descriptions, mappings, subgroup and role data were not changed. No SQL or seed was generated.

The 78 refinements below are tied to the same-ID source title and definition. Most replace fragmentary comma lists with complete phrases; where the former label hid part of the documented scope, the new label names that scope. These are display-label refinements, not taxonomy-boundary or mapping changes.
