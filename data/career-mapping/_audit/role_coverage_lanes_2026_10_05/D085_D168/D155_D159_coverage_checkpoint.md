## Sources

- **AFP_TREASURY_FUNCTIONS** ([source](https://www.afponline.org/topics/treasury/treasury-management-defined), Association for Financial Professionals (AFP)). Describes treasury work in liquidity, cash, financial risk and funding management, supporting a task distinction from corporate accounting and investment-banking advisory. Limitation: Professional practice evidence does not establish that a dedicated corporate-treasurer post is universal or missing from active roles without task comparison.
- **CFA_INVESTMENT_BANKING_AND_CREDIT** ([source](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/careers/investment-banker), CFA Institute). Describes investment-banking tasks such as capital raising, securities issuance, M&A, restructuring and debt/equity advisory; CFA career materials distinguish credit-risk analysis from broad securities and investment analysis. Limitation: Career profiles establish task distinctions, not universal title prevalence or exclusive assignment to this product's domain.
- **FIG_SURVEYOR_AND_CADASTRE_FUNCTIONS** ([source](https://www.fig.net/organisation/comm/7/library/reports/cadastre/statement_on_cadastre.html), International Federation of Surveyors (FIG)). Defines cadastral surveying as identifying, demarcating, measuring and mapping legal parcel boundaries; FIG surveyor functions also include geodetic measurement, spatial information and mapping. Limitation: Does not prescribe a uniform national job title or authorize the product to infer licensure; exact statutory scope varies by jurisdiction.
- **ILO_ISCO08** ([source](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf), International Labour Organization (ILO)). Task-defined occupational groups for retail and wholesale trade managers (ISCO-08 1420), restaurant managers (1412), buyers (3323), hairdressers/beauticians (5141/5142), and funeral/embalming work (5163). Limitation: Classification supports occupational-family existence and task boundaries, not domain assignment or proof of a missing link by itself.
- **ISO_TC229_NANOTECHNOLOGY_SCOPE** ([source](https://www.iso.org/committee/381983.html), International Organization for Standardization (ISO), Technical Committee 229). Defines committee work across nanotechnology terminology, metrology and instrumentation, test methods, modelling/simulation, and health, safety and environmental practice; useful for task coverage boundaries in nanomaterials work. Limitation: A standards work programme does not establish job titles or show that any particular candidate occupation is globally standardized.
- **OECD_TRANSFER_PRICING_FRAMEWORK** ([source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2022/01/oecd-transfer-pricing-guidelines-for-multinational-enterprises-and-tax-administrations-2022_57104b3a.html), Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)). Establishes the substantive transfer-pricing practice context, including arm's-length analysis and documentation; supports distinguishing transfer-pricing work from generic bookkeeping or tax compliance. Limitation: This is technical/tax-administration guidance, not an occupational taxonomy. Job titles, admission rules and whether a specialist post exists vary by jurisdiction and employer.
- **STRUCTURED_FINANCE_ANALYST_TASKS** ([source](https://structuredfinance.org/careers/securitized-products-credit-analyst/), Structured Finance Association (SFA), including published structured-finance role profiles). Documents securitized-credit work analyzing ABS/RMBS, transaction cash flows, collateral and market fundamentals, surveillance, credit opinions and investment recommendations. Limitation: Role postings prove task patterns, not universal title standardization; distinguish this specialization from existing general credit and securities analyst tasks before treating it as a separate role.
