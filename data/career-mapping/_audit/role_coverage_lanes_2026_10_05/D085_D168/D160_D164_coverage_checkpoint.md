## Sources

- **AICI_IMAGE_CONSULTING_SCOPE** ([source](https://www.aici.org/page/Helpful_FAQs?page=Discover_the_Career), Association of Image Consultants International (AICI)). Describes image consulting services including wardrobe development/management, personal shopping, color/body analysis, communication coaching, etiquette and corporate image work. Limitation: AICI supports the profession and task scope, but personal stylist, fashion stylist and image consultant titles overlap and are not necessarily legally protected or globally uniform.
- **UITP_PUBLIC_TRANSPORT_PLANNING_OPERATIONS** ([source](https://www.uitp.org/fr/evenements/diploma-programme-managers-in-public-transport-2026-2027/), International Association of Public Transport (UITP)). Distinguishes network/service planning (demand, capacity, accessibility, route/network design and timetables) from execution and operations planning (vehicles, personnel, rostering, reliability and disruption management). Limitation: The source validates functions, not a mandatory role taxonomy; tasks may be combined in agencies/operators and overlap with transport planning/management titles.
- **UNHCR_PROTECTION_AND_RSD_FUNCTIONS** ([source](https://emergency.unhcr.org/protection/legal-framework/refugee-status-determination-rsd), United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR)). Defines refugee status determination as a legal/administrative process and documents protection assessment, legal protection and asylum-related functions. Limitation: These functions do not establish one universal job title or confer legal authority; role labels and mandate vary by jurisdiction and organization.
- **WFP_HUMANITARIAN_LOGISTICS_FUNCTIONS** ([source](https://www.wfp.org/operations/200685-aviation-services-logistics-augmentation-and-cluster-coordination-response-floods), United Nations World Food Programme (WFP), Logistics Cluster operations guidance). Documents humanitarian logistics coordination, common services, transport/cargo support and information-management functions in emergency response. Limitation: Operational examples establish work functions but not a universal occupational taxonomy or identical job structure across emergencies.
- **WIPO_PATENT_AGENT_FUNCTIONS** ([source](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/mdocs/scp/en/scp_12/scp_12_3_rev_2.pdf), World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)). Describes patent-agent/attorney functions such as advising applicants, preparing and prosecuting patent applications, representing clients before patent offices and supporting maintenance/opposition/enforcement. Limitation: WIPO explicitly cautions that patent attorney/agent titles, qualifications and powers differ by country; do not present a single global license or legal scope.
