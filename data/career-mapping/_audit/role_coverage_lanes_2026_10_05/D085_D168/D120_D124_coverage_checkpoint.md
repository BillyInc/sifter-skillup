## Sources

- **FAO_AQUACULTURE_FARM_MANAGEMENT — Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)** ([source](https://www.fao.org/fishery/docs/CDrom/FAO_Training/FAO_Training/General/x6709e/x6709e01.htm)). Farm-level management includes stocking, water quality, feeding, harvest and marketing; larger aquaculture farms require coordination of production factors, records and operating performance. Limitation: Technical task evidence supports the distinct farm-management function but does not by itself establish a title in every labour market or determine domain placement.
- **ILO_ISCO08_HEALTH_OCCUPATIONS — International Labour Organization (ILO), ISCO-08 Volume I** ([source](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf)). ISCO-08 provides task-defined occupations including medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians (3211), medical and pathology laboratory technicians (3212), and community health workers (3253). The 3253 definition covers education, referral/follow-up, basic preventive care and home visiting. Limitation: Task family definitions support coverage checks but do not establish exact titles, credentials, national scope of practice or product-domain ownership.
- **ILO_ISCO08_TRANSPORT_AGRICULTURE — International Labour Organization (ILO), ISCO-08 Volume I** ([source](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf)). ISCO-08 unit groups 6111-6113 crop and horticultural growers; 6121 livestock and dairy producers, 6122 poultry producers, 6123 apiary/sericulture workers and 6129 animal producers not elsewhere classified; 1312 aquaculture/fisheries production managers; 2132 farming/forestry/fisheries advisers; 6210 forestry and related workers; 8311 locomotive-engine drivers, 8312 railway-brake/signal/switch operators, 8331 bus/tram drivers, 8332 heavy-truck drivers, and 8342-8343 earthmoving/crane operators. Limitation: International family definitions support coverage checks, not automatic domain ownership. Local vehicle licensing, employer titles, regulatory work and overlapping production/transport tasks are not inferred.
- **WHO_GLOBAL_BLOOD_SAFETY — World Health Organization (WHO)** ([source](https://www.who.int/publications/b/82618)). Blood safety and transfusion require distinct service processes including collection, laboratory testing, processing and clinical use; these functions can be undertaken by laboratory staff without proving a separate occupation. Limitation: Service-function evidence does not define a separate occupational family or a national credential; classify as a role only if task-boundary sources justify it.
- **WHO_GLOBAL_CHW_GUIDANCE — World Health Organization (WHO)** ([source](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240118065)). WHO says CHW competencies and role scope vary by country, health system, programme and context, so global occupational coverage should not infer national cadre equivalence. Limitation: Global guidance only; it is not a source for any specific country's title, curriculum, regulatory status or entry credential.
- **WHO_HEALTH_LAB_SYSTEM — World Health Organization (WHO)** ([source](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789290232872)). Clinical laboratory networks perform diagnostic and surveillance services with defined personnel, quality and coordination functions across health-system levels. Limitation: System guidance supports the work context, not a globally standardized separate occupational title such as biomedical scientist or blood-bank technologist.
