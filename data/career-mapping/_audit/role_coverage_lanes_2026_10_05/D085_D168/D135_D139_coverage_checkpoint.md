## Sources

- **EASA_FLIGHT_TEST_ENGINEER — European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA)** ([source](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/online-publications/easy-access-rules-initial-airworthiness-and?page=34)). EASA defines flight test engineer and lead flight test engineer functions in ground or onboard flight-test operations, including test-system operation and support to pilots; it specifies competence and experience conditions for lead roles. Limitation: This is a real specialized engineering role, but qualification and authorization rules cited are EASA-specific; do not present it as a universally licensed occupation.
- **IAEA_MEDICAL_DOSIMETRY — International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), Human Health Series** ([source](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/Pub1610_web.pdf)). IAEA describes medical dosimetrists as radiation-therapy team members who calculate and design dose distributions/treatment plans in collaboration with medical physicists and radiation oncologists, under appropriate supervision. Limitation: The function is distinct, but the IAEA notes that its detailed job description was obtained from the American Association of Medical Dosimetrists; title, scope and independent recognition vary internationally.
- **IAEA_MEDICAL_RADIATION_TEAM — International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), Safety Standards SSG-46** ([source](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/nss-oui/Published%20Chunks/m_60b8e698-4f59-4c24-809a-dfb819ac5050/c_4616a381-7a46-4b09-a811-5e170b311e9e__1_0.Html)). Defines medical physicist responsibilities and distinguishes them from radiation protection officer and medical radiation technologist functions; notes globally variable names for radiographers, nuclear-medicine technologists and radiation therapists. Limitation: Role/function guidance does not establish one uniform international title, licence or local staffing model.
- **IAEA_NUCLEAR_OPERATIONS_ROLES — International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), Nuclear Energy Series** ([source](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/connect/MSNpublic/Pages/nct/downloads/Pub1659_web.pdf)). Identifies typical functions such as reactor manager, shift supervisor, reactor operator, radiation protection manager/officer, maintenance engineer/staff, nuclear safety officer and quality-assurance officer; required staffing depends on facility and national regulations. Limitation: Facility guidance does not prove uniform global job titles or establish that every function needs a separate catalogue role; compare active D135 tasks first.
- **IFMBE_CLINICAL_ENGINEERING — International Federation for Medical and Biological Engineering (IFMBE), Clinical Engineering Division** ([source](https://ced.ifmbe.org/resources/clinical-engineering-definitions/)). Defines clinical engineering work around healthcare-technology planning, evaluation, management, analysis, education, support and regulatory compliance, and notes WHO terminology variation with biomedical engineering/health-care technology management. Limitation: The source emphasizes regional terminology overlap; this supports a possible specialization/alias within biomedical engineering, not necessarily a separate role record.
- **IGDA_GAME_DISCIPLINES — International Game Developers Association (IGDA)** ([source](https://igda.org/global-industry-game-awards/)). IGDA recognizes discipline areas such as narrative design, level design, systems/gameplay design, UI/UX, engineering, audio and accessibility in game development. Limitation: Discipline and award categories demonstrate established specialisms, not that each is a distinct occupation or a missing role where related active records already exist.
