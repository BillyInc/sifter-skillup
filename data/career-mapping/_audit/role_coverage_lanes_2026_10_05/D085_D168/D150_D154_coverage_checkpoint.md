## Sources

- **AES_AUDIO_PRODUCTION_ROLE_FAMILIES — Audio Engineering Society (AES), international professional society and technical committees** ([source](https://aes.org/community/technical-council/aes-technical-committee-audio-production/)). AES audio-production materials distinguish recording engineers, mixers, sound designers and related production functions; paired with ILO ISCO-08 music and audiovisual technician groups, this supports separate creative-music and technical-audio work families. Limitation: AES role labels and education examples do not constitute a global occupational classification; studio/live/post-production titles and role boundaries vary, and emerging interactive/spatial roles need separate validation.
- **FIMS_SPORT_SCIENCE_TEAM — International Federation of Sports Medicine (FIMS)** ([source](https://www.fims.org/about/code-ethics/)). Describes interdisciplinary sports-medicine collaboration with physical therapists and sport scientists, including biochemists, biomechanists and physiologists; supports sport-science functions distinct from clinical treatment alone. Limitation: The source defines team functions, not a universally standardized sports-scientist title, credential, or exclusive domain placement; compare exact active exercise-science roles first.
- **ICOMOS_ARCHAEOLOGY_AND_SITE_MANAGEMENT — ICOMOS International Scientific Committee on Archaeological Heritage Management (ICAHM)** ([source](https://icahm.icomos.org/about-icahm/)). Explicitly identifies professional archaeologists and archaeological site managers and describes archaeological research, site/resource management and cultural-resource-management practice. Limitation: Professional-network and standards evidence; national title, legal authority and heritage-service structures vary.
- **ICOM_MUSEUM_PROFESSIONS — International Council of Museums (ICOM), International Committee for Museology (ICOFOM)** ([source](https://icom.museum/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/Museologie_Anglais_BD.pdf)). Distinguishes curatorial, conservation and registrar work, including scientific exhibition planning, specialized conservation treatment, collections movement, insurance and reserve management. Limitation: Museum staffing and role titles vary by institution and country; this supports task distinction, not a mandatory organizational chart or universal title standard.
- **ILO_ISCO08 — International Labour Organization (ILO)** ([source](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf)). Limitation: Classification supports occupational-family existence and task boundaries, not domain assignment or proof of a missing link by itself.
- **ILO_ISCO08_JUDGES — International Labour Organization (ILO), ISCO-08** ([source](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf)).
