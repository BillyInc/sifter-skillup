## Evidence approach

- ILO ISCO-08 task definitions support policy administration, agronomist and soil-science occupational scope; classification membership itself is not used as domain-fit evidence.
- UN DESA/IASIA and OECD public-governance materials corroborate public policy formulation, implementation and evaluation responsibilities.
- IFSW/IASSW global standards define social-work responsibilities and warn against collapsing different country contexts and adjacent occupations.
- FAO/IFAD sources describe agricultural research, extension/advisory functions and their farmer-facing tasks.
- IFT describes food scientist/technologist, food engineering, product development, microbiology/quality and sensory science functions.
- IMechE describes automotive product-development functions (design, test/validation, modelling, manufacture, quality, research) and explicitly cautions that job titles vary between companies.
