- The current computational checkpoint's four unmatched task-signal titles include `gameplay programmer`, `technical artist`, and `game environment artist` (plus `robotics engineer` for D140). “Unmatched” means no exact local title/alias match under the scanner's rule; it does not mean no related local record exists.

These must be inspected by record state and task description. A related or review-only record is not an active exact-title match.

- The role-coverage reviewer should decide, with task-level evidence, whether each is already substantively covered, needs a narrower established title, or is a true missing role candidate. Do not infer parent-domain fit or specialization fit from a subgroup/ISCO code.
