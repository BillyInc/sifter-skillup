## Checkpoint 2: external primary-source checks

Primary sources were checked through the official ILO ISCO-08 volume, United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD) ISIC classifications, U.S. Government Accountability Office, OECD, UNESCO, and British Esports Federation. Links below support only the adjacent claim; they do not establish local demand, licensing or Nigerian availability.

- D086: UNSD [ISIC Rev. 4 division 92](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/27/92) includes gambling facilities, lotteries and off-track betting; [class 9200](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/27/9200) includes bookmaking, casinos, lottery ticket sales and online gambling sites. ILO [ISCO-08 Volume I](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf), unit group 4212, identifies bookmakers, croupiers and related gaming workers. This independently supports the title's activity family, while the existing student exclusion remains a product-policy choice.
- D087–D089: ILO [ISCO-08 Volume I](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/%40dgreports/%40dcomm/%40publ/documents/publication/wcms_172572.pdf), groups 5141 (Hairdressers), 5142 (Beauticians and Related Workers) and 5163 (Undertakers and Embalmers), supports the core occupational identities. UNSD [ISIC class 9602](https://unstats.unst.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/en/27/9602) covers hairdressing and other beauty treatment; [division 96](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/27/96) explicitly includes funeral and related activities. The source-library title, rather than an adjacent code, is the evidence for the omitted “Hair Technology” and “Spa Services” qualifiers.
- D100–D101: ILO [ISCO-08 Volume I](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf), groups 7126, 7127, 7131 and 7132, directly validates the plumbing/pipefitting, air-conditioning/refrigeration, painting and spray-painting branches. The original library detailed records supply the additional labels “HVAC Technology” and “Surface Treatment.”
- D108: ILO [ISCO-08 Volume I](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf), group 732, divides printing into pre-press technicians (7321), printers (7322), and print finishing and binding workers (7323). UNSD [ISIC 1811](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/27/1811) covers printing, including newspapers, packaging-related print products and labels. The source-library detailed record adds print shops, reprographics and print finishing.
- D113: ILO [ISCO-08 Volume I](https://webapps.ilo.org/ilostat-files/ISCO/newdocs-08-2021/ISCO-08/ISCO-08%20EN%20Vol%201.pdf), groups 8181–8183 and 8189, describes glass/ceramics plant operations, boilers, and packing/bottling/labelling plants. This supports the current label's glass, ceramics and boiler components; the original record specifically names building materials and boiler rooms.
- D133: UNSD [ISIC Rev. 4 division 30](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/27/30) and [class 3011](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/27/3011) cover shipbuilding; [class 3030](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/27/3030) covers aircraft and spacecraft manufacture and aircraft seats. UNESCO [Behind the Screens](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/in/documentViewer.xhtml?ark=%2Fark%3A%2F48223%2Fpf000&file=%2Fin%2Frest%2FannotationSVC%2FDownloadWatermarkedAttachment%2Fattach_import_786e0e39-4dec-4e34-ad59-0140487776a7%3F_%3D392006eng.pdf&id=p%3A%3Ausmarcdef_0000392006&locale=es&multi=true&v=2.1.196) studies digital content creators across 45 countries; UK HMRC-commissioned [creator research](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/understanding-social-media-content-creators/research-to-understand-social-media-content-creators) documents monetisation methods and creator self-employment.
