# D045 — Public Administration and Policy: specialization review

**Disposition:** source-reviewed; current subgroups excluded; candidate restructuring only. No canonical taxonomy changes were made.

## What the catalogue currently says

D045 has **0 active role mappings**, 3 stored subgroup records and 0 visible subgroups. The inherited groups—Government & Elected Leadership (ISCO 11), Policy & Programme Officers (24), and Inspection & Enforcement Officers (33)—are excluded and each has zero mapped roles. The zero is a coverage gap; it is not evidence that public administration has no occupations. Nor should the count of legacy groups determine the correct number of specializations.

The current parent description combines policy and public institutions with civil registration, urban planning, customs/tax/safety inspection, elected leadership and ambassadors. Those are not automatically one practice domain. OECD describes public governance through policy formulation, implementation, communication and evaluation; UN CEPA guidance covers capable public institutions, sound policymaking, accountability and integrity; public financial management has its own distinguishable functions. None of these sources prescribes a fixed student taxonomy.

## Recommended scope and candidate areas

Keep the parent focused on the professional work of designing, coordinating, implementing and evaluating public policy and public services through public institutions. Do not make government employment alone a fit test. Diplomacy belongs with foreign affairs when the task is diplomatic representation; technical city planning, tax-law practice, clinical/teaching service delivery and safety inspection need their own domain/role checks.

Four **provisional**, not officially mandated, practice areas are proposed for later reconciliation with role-level task evidence:

1. **Public Policy Design and Evaluation** — analyze public problems, develop policy options and evaluate effects and implementation.
2. **Public Programme and Service Delivery** — coordinate and monitor government programmes and services; frontline professional work remains in its own domain unless the job is specifically administrative.
3. **Public Finance and Budget Management** — public budgeting, expenditure, investment management, procurement and financial controls; this does not absorb general accounting or commercial finance.
4. **Public Governance and Institutional Performance** — public-institution capability, integrity, accountability, coordination and administrative systems; not a catch-all for political leadership, diplomacy, courts or policing.

The candidate count is a synthesis from documented functions, not the number of old subgroups, ISCO buckets or roles. Do not expose these as confirmed student paths until global role coverage is rebuilt and each role independently passes a task-based D045 fit check.

## Computational coverage and boundaries

Public-sector data use, digital government, administrative information systems, monitoring/evaluation and evidence-informed policy are cross-cutting methods. Their presence does not establish a distinct specialization or a new job title. Keep general data science and software engineering in their own domains; a role can also have a D045 context only when its actual duties are public-administration work.

## Sources and limits

- [OECD Policy Framework on Sound Public Governance](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/policy-framework-on-sound-public-governance_c03e01b3-en.html) — policy formulation, implementation, communication and evaluation; not an occupational taxonomy.
- [OECD guidance on policy evaluation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/policy-framework-on-sound-public-governance_c03e01b3-en/full-report/component-11.html) — evaluation informs formulation, implementation and accountability; not a mandated subgroup.
- [OECD guidance on policy implementation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/policy-framework-on-sound-public-governance_c03e01b3-en/full-report/component-10.html) — implementation, monitoring and performance feedback.
- [UN CEPA / UN DESA effective-governance principles and guidance](https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/publications/category/CEPA%20Strategy%20Guidance%20Notes/topics/Principles%20of%20Effective%20Governance/sdgs/Peace%20Justice%20and%20Strong%20Institutions) — internationally framed principles and public-sector functions; voluntary guidance, not a binding global job classification.
- [World Bank: Managing Public Finances](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/financial-management-umbrella-program/brief/module-one-overview) — fiscal/expenditure management, public investment management and procurement; does not place all accounting or procurement under D045.
- [World Bank: Public Service Delivery](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/governance/brief/public-service-delivery-global-solutions-group) — delivery involves policy-makers, public servants, service providers and users; not every frontline occupation is public administration.
