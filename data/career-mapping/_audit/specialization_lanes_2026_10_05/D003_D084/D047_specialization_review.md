# D047 — Agricultural Science: specialization review

**Disposition:** source-reviewed; current scope overlaps several sector domains and the four inherited ISCO subgroup buckets are not valid specialization areas as written. No canonical data was changed.

## Current state and core problem

D047 has **21 active role mappings** and four visible subgroups:

| Current subgroup | Mappings | Audit finding |
|---|---:|---|
| Aquaculture & Forestry Managers | 2 | Combines distinct sectors and an occupational level; forestry and fisheries/aquaculture already have separate catalogue domains. |
| Agricultural & Life Scientists | 8 | Broad ISCO family, not a single coherent agricultural specialization; general life science is not automatically agricultural. |
| Farm & Aquaculture Technicians | 1 | Technician is a job level, and the label combines terrestrial and aquatic production. |
| Crop & Livestock Farm Leaders | 10 | Combines crop, livestock, vineyard and garden work; overlaps crop/horticulture, livestock and farm-operations domains. |

The parent currently mixes research and science with farm production, forestry, fisheries/aquaculture and machinery. The repository already has **D096 Agricultural Machinery and Farm Operations, D118 Crop Production and Horticulture, D119 Livestock, Poultry and Dairy Production, D120 Fisheries and Aquaculture, and D121 Forestry and Wildlife Management**. FAO’s wide agricultural subject umbrella includes related sectors, but that alone does not justify duplicating their operational learner paths under D047. The defensible distinction is: D047 for agricultural science/research/extension tasks; the specialist production or machinery domain for operational work. A research role can be connected across domains only when its actual duties support that context.

The separate D047 parent-fit review assessed all 21 current mappings (12 supported, 7 conditional, 2 unsupported), but it predates this subgroup/domain-overlap review. Those results must be reconciled before any canonical correction; they do not validate the four subgroups.

## Provisional areas if D047 is narrowed to science, research and extension

The following four areas are a **working synthesis**, not an externally mandated count and not approved student paths:

1. **Crop and Plant Science** — agronomy, plant/crop science, plant health and improvement. Operational crop growing and horticulture routes remain primarily D118.
2. **Animal and Livestock Science** — animal science, nutrition, breeding and production research. Veterinary diagnosis/treatment remains D066; routine livestock production remains primarily D119.
3. **Soil, Land and Agroecological Science** — soil processes, land resources and interactions within agricultural systems. The IUSS divisions support soil science as a coherent scientific area.
4. **Agricultural Systems, Research and Extension** — interdisciplinary farming-systems research and evidence-based advice/extension. Generic public administration, sales or education is not agricultural extension without the agriculture-specific task remit.

Agricultural biotechnology, genetics and precision/data methods cut across these fields; do not make them stand-alone subgroups unless distinct role tasks and learning paths justify that structure. Forestry and aquaculture operations stay in their specialist domains. Research connections require exact role evidence.

## Computational coverage

Agricultural data analysis, remote sensing, crop/livestock modelling, bioinformatics and precision agriculture may support several areas. They are methods, not automatic specializations or job titles. A computational agriculture route must show the agricultural task context and a verified relationship to a specific area or role.

## Source evidence and limits

- [FAO AGRIS/CARIS subject categories](https://www.fao.org/4/U1808E/U1808E01.htm) distinguish agricultural subject areas and require checking scope notes against the research subject. This is a research classification, not an occupation taxonomy.
- [FAO Agricultural Research](https://www.fao.org/research-extension-systems/agricultural-research/en) describes research and biotechnology spanning crops, livestock, forestry, fisheries/aquaculture and agro-industry; it supports cross-sector research, not duplicate operational mappings.
- [FAO Research and Extension Systems](https://www.fao.org/research-extension-systems/en/) identifies linked research, extension and advisory functions in agricultural innovation; it does not prescribe learner subgroups.
- [IUSS divisions and commissions](https://www.iuss.org/general-information/) organize soil science across space/time, properties/processes, use/management and societal/environmental roles; this supports soil science as a candidate branch, not a complete agriculture taxonomy.
- [FAO Agricultural and Farm Systems](https://www.fao.org/4/w7365e/w7365e04.htm) describes related crop, livestock, forestry, fisheries, research and production subsystems, supporting the distinction between cross-sector systems work and commodity operations.

Only after that should the final number and role membership of D047’s specialization areas be approved. Keep global categories separate from explicitly evidenced Nigeria-specific practice.
