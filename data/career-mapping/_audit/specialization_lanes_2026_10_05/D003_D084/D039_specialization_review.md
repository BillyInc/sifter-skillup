# D039 — Higher Education and Academic Research: specialization review

**Disposition:** two current subgroup rows do not define appropriate specializations, and the catalogue has **zero active role mappings**. The root description also includes school-level teaching, which conflicts with the higher-education scope. Four provisional areas are proposed, but none should become a learner-facing path until role population and scope are repaired.

## Current structure

| Current label | ISCO-derived group | Active mapped roles | Finding |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Deans & School Principals | 13 | 0 | Combines higher-education academic leadership with school principals. |
| Lecturers & Vocational Teachers | 23 | 0 | Mixes tertiary lecturers with potentially non-tertiary vocational instructors. |

## Provisional areas

1. **Higher-Education Teaching and Curriculum**
2. **Academic Research and Scholarship**
3. **Academic Leadership, Governance and Quality**
4. **Student Learning, Academic Services and Community Engagement**

These functions follow UNESCO's global description of higher-education teaching personnel as engaged in teaching, scholarship/research or educational services; UNESCO also recognizes higher-education quality assurance. OECD distinguishes academic roles from professional/administrative staff supporting teaching, research and engagement. These sources support a provisional design, not an official four-part subgroup count.

## Scope correction needed

Do not keep school principals or school-level teachers in D039 by default. Verify whether teaching is tertiary before assigning it here. Research administration and general university services also need task-based parent-domain review rather than being treated automatically as academic careers. Local degree levels, titles, accreditation and professional requirements must be explicitly labelled.

See the JSON artifact for detailed source locators, candidate boundaries and limitations. No role mappings or canonical taxonomy records were changed.
