# D010 specialization review — Business Intelligence and Analytics

**Disposition:** Recommend three student-facing areas as an internal synthesis: the trusted BI data foundation, reporting/performance intelligence, and decision-oriented business analytics. Sources support these bodies of practice but do not mandate this taxonomy/count. No role mappings were reviewed.

## Existing structure checked

The four current labels are Statistics & Computational Modelling; Business Analysis & Strategy; Data & Systems Engineering; and Statistical Operations & Data Support. Their memberships are unverified and their labels mix disciplines, functions and support levels. They were not used to determine the new set or count.

## Recommended areas

1. **BI Data Foundations and Architecture** — model, integrate, assure and organize organizational data for analytical use, including warehouses, marts, semantic structures and metadata.
2. **BI Reporting and Performance Intelligence** — governed reporting, dashboards, visual analysis and ongoing performance measurement.
3. **Business Analytics and Decision Support** — analyze business questions, drivers and alternatives, then translate evidence into decisions or process improvements.

Governance, quality, privacy/security, strategy/adoption and tools cut across areas rather than becoming default top-level specializations. Keep the boundary with D009 Data Science by purpose: D010 centers organizational reporting and business decisions; statistical science or general-purpose ML engineering may be adjacent and cannot be inferred from a tool choice.

## Evidence and limits

- **DAMA-DMBOK2R knowledge areas**: identifies Data Warehousing and Business Intelligence alongside data architecture, modeling, integration/interoperability, metadata and quality. [Source](https://dama.org/dmbok2r-infographics/)
- **ACM/AIS IS 2010**, Data/Information/Knowledge Management material: data quality, OLTP/OLAP, BI, warehousing, mining and data-mart outcomes. [PDF](https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/education/curricula-recommendations/is-2010-acm-final.pdf)
- **TDWI CBIP scope**: lists BI reporting/visualization, analytics, data management/architecture and strategy/program methods. It is broad and overlaps other domains. [Source](https://tdwi.org/cbip)
- **TDWI Analytics Maturity Model**: distinguishes BI reporting/dashboard beginnings from broader organizational analytics capability and acting on insights. [Source](https://tdwi.org/pages/assessments/adv-all-tdwi-analytics-maturity-model-assessment.aspx)
- **DAMA 2024 revision statement**: says the DMBOK framework and knowledge areas were maintained in the revision. [Source](https://dama.org/dama-dmbok-revision/)

These sources define professional knowledge and curriculum content, not a universal occupational taxonomy. This three-area set is an internal student-facing synthesis; academic sign-off is not claimed. No role IDs, ISCO groupings or role counts were used to infer fit.
