## Provisional practice areas

1. **Organizational Compliance, Ethics and Integrity** — operate systems for identifying obligations, accountability, prevention, advice and continual improvement.
2. **Corporate Governance, Risk and Internal Controls** — support accountable governance, risk oversight and controls over operations, reporting and compliance.
3. **Regulatory Policy, Affairs and Oversight** — develop/evaluate public regulatory policy or manage an organization's regulatory interface.
4. **Regulatory Supervision, Inspection and Enforcement** — risk-based monitoring, inspection and proportionate response to non-compliance.

ISO 37301, COSO, OECD and FATF support these as distinct functions, but they do not prescribe four subgroups. D038 may need to split into separate root domains after role-level reconciliation. No active roles are currently linked, so these are not yet verified student routes.

## Boundaries

General government administration, legal advice, independent audit, and sector-specific inspection should not be placed here simply because they touch regulation. Classify each role by its primary tasks. Management-system and governance concepts can be global; legal duties and regulatory powers are local and sector-specific. ISO 37301 is a management-system standard, not an individual certification.
