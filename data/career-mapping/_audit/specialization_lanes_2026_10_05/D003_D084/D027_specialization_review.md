# D027 Biomedical Science — specialization structure review

**Status:** Source-reviewed candidate set; provisional pending parent-domain role reconciliation. This is not external academic sign-off.

## Finding

D027 currently has three subgroup rows and 46 active role mappings. The three rows are occupational groups: two are rewritten ISCO families and one remains an ISCO family label. They are not evidence that Biomedical Science should have exactly three areas of specialization.

The current role map also includes environmental, agricultural, aquaculture, conservation and other biology-adjacent work. Those records must be checked against their actual tasks and compared with adjacent domains before they can be treated as Biomedical Science roles. Using laboratory methods or biological knowledge alone does not prove parent-domain fit.

## Provisional areas of specialization

The sources support the following nine-area internal synthesis, not a universally fixed global count:

1. Clinical Chemistry and Biomedical Laboratory Science
2. Haematology and Transfusion Science
3. Medical Microbiology and Virology
4. Immunology and Immunopathology
5. Cellular Pathology and Cytology
6. Molecular Diagnostics, Genetics and Genomics
7. Cell, Molecular and Human Biomedical Research
8. Pharmacology and Toxicology
9. Bioinformatics and Computational Biomedicine

These candidates separate diagnostic-laboratory disciplines from research, pharmacology and biological-data analysis. Their boundaries overlap in real practice; the data should show shared skills/items without pretending that the professional fields or country rules are identical. Generic software/data work, general ecology, agriculture, aquaculture and conservation are not included merely because they use biological methods.

## Fix disposition

- Replace the current ISCO occupational-family labels as student-facing specialization names only after taxonomy implementation can preserve the occupational/source records separately.
- Do not set subgroup membership from the current role list. First complete task-based role-to-domain review; then assign only roles whose tasks fit both the Biomedical Science parent and the specific specialization.
- Keep licensing, protected titles and clinical authority jurisdiction-specific. The cited UK IBMS material informs discipline names but is not a global licensing rule.
- Keep the nine-area set provisional: international sources recognize the component fields, but do not prescribe a single worldwide subgroup count.
