# D030 Marketing and Brand Management — specialization structure review

**Status:** Source-reviewed candidate set; pending catalogue-wide reconciliation. This is not an official fixed subgroup count.

## Finding

D030 currently contains two ISCO-derived subgroup rows, both excluded from the student view: “Administrative and Commercial Managers” and “Business and Administration Professionals.” They describe occupational families, not student-facing marketing practice areas, so D030 currently has **no visible specialization branches**.

The AMA's current marketing competency model organizes connected work around strategy and planning, content and branding, channels and technology, and data and analytics; it also places ethics and essential capabilities at the center and explicitly recognizes overlap. ESOMAR grounds market research/insights as a distinct, method-based professional practice; ISO 20671 supports a distinct brand evaluation practice; AMA and CIM career guidance recognize digital, product marketing, research and communications pathways. Together these support an internal **seven-area** student-navigation structure, but none prescribes this exact seven-subgroup taxonomy.

## Proposed student-facing areas

1. Marketing Strategy and Planning
2. Market Research and Customer Insights
3. Brand Strategy and Management
4. Product Marketing and Go-to-Market
5. Marketing Communications and Content
6. Digital Marketing and Channel Activation
7. Marketing Analytics and Performance

Treat customer experience/relationship marketing and ethics as cross-cutting learning themes for now; do not split every channel or marketing tool into its own subgroup. Keep product-market positioning and launch work within Marketing, while product roadmap/ownership stays in Product Management. Keep the shared foundation visible once and show additional branch-specific learning without duplicating common items as exclusive requirements.

## Fix and boundary disposition

- Replace the two excluded occupation-family labels as student-facing specialization categories after the catalogue-wide ID, naming and cross-domain reconciliation.
- Do not use the current ISCO codes, existing role counts or role titles to set the number of areas or infer parent-domain fit. The independent role lane must decide whether each of the 46 current role links belongs in Marketing from its actual tasks.
- Keep Sales and Business Development distinct. Show supported marketing-to-sales handoffs, but do not collapse marketing positioning/demand creation into sales account ownership or closing.
- Keep product marketing limited to positioning, market requirements, launch and adoption; product roadmap and product ownership remain in D033 Product Management.
- Keep market research/insights subject to research ethics, consent and privacy. Label advertising, consumer-protection and privacy requirements by jurisdiction.
- Treat marketing ethics and essential capabilities as shared foundations, not separate specializations. Treat customer experience/relationship marketing as cross-cutting until non-overlapping evidence supports its own subgroup.
- Do not turn SEO, social, email, paid-media or other individual channels into separate subgroups; they are use cases within channel activation.
