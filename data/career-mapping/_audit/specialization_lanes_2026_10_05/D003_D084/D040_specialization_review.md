# D040 — Vocational and Technical Training: specialization review

**Disposition:** three existing subgroup rows do not define a reliable specialization taxonomy, and there are **zero active role mappings**. Four provisional learning areas are recommended for later reconciliation. They are not yet student pathways.

## Provisional areas

1. **Vocational and Technical Instruction and Assessment**
2. **Apprenticeship and Work-Based Learning**
3. **Workplace and Continuing Professional Development**
4. **TVET Curriculum, Qualifications and Quality**

UNESCO-UNEVOC sources support vocational teacher/trainer development and TVET teaching practice. ILO Recommendation 208 and work-based-learning guidance distinguish structured apprenticeship from general workplace coaching. These are practice distinctions, not an official four-area taxonomy.

## Required boundary repair

Generic corporate coaching belongs here only when it teaches occupational/technical capabilities. School assistants do not belong here absent direct vocational-training duties. Confirm tertiary/school/provider context and task evidence before role assignment. Apprenticeship contracts, qualification frameworks and instructor credentials are local requirements and must be explicitly labelled.

There are no current mapped roles to validate; reconstruct the role population and compare it with global and Nigerian evidence before making these areas visible. See the JSON artifact for source locators, limitations and full dispositions. No canonical taxonomy records were changed.
