# D026 Public Health — specialization structure review

**Status:** Source-reviewed candidate set; provisional pending role-to-domain reconciliation. This is not external academic sign-off.

## Finding

The current inventory shows five subgroup cards and 26 active role mappings. Four cards are ISCO-derived occupational families; one, **Epidemiology & Health Data**, is a plausible practice area but combines analytic methods and surveillance work whose boundary needs to be clarified. Current member lists are inventory only, not proof of domain fit.

WHO's global essential public health functions (EPHFs) provide a better basis for an area audit: they cover surveillance, emergency management, stewardship, multisectoral planning, health protection, prevention, promotion, community participation, workforce development, equity/quality, research and access to health products. WHO's competency sources further distinguish public-health intelligence and One Health field epidemiology. They are global function/competency frameworks—not a formal subgroup taxonomy.

## Recommended provisional areas of specialization

A nine-area internal synthesis is proposed:

1. Epidemiology, Surveillance and Public Health Intelligence
2. Public Health Data, Biostatistics and Evaluation
3. Health Promotion and Disease Prevention
4. Environmental, Occupational and Food Health Protection
5. Public Health Emergency Preparedness and One Health Response
6. Community Health, Equity and Participation
7. Public Health Policy, Systems and Leadership
8. Public Health Workforce Development and Education
9. Public Health Research and Knowledge Translation

These are practice areas, not nine WHO-certified specialties. Public-health intelligence, statistical methods, One Health, and evaluation may overlap across routes; item-specific learning should explain that overlap rather than imply a separate credential. The current ISCO labels should not be carried forward as specialization names.

## Fix disposition

- Replace the four ISCO family headings as student-facing specialization labels; preserve their source records for staff audit.
- Refine “Epidemiology & Health Data” into surveillance/intelligence and data/evaluation only if the eventual role evidence supports both as distinct routes.
- Do not assign or reassign roles from current subgroup membership. Independently assess all 26 roles against the Public Health parent tasks; compare adjacent-domain candidates before adding specialization links.
- Keep the nine-area synthesis provisional and show country-specific legal authority as local context, not as the global baseline.
