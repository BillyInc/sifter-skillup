# D013 specialization review — Investment Analysis and Asset Management

**Disposition:** Recommend four learner-facing areas: Investment Research and Valuation; Portfolio Management and Asset Allocation; Private Markets Investing; Private Wealth and Client Investment Management. The count is an internal synthesis informed by current professional pathways, not a universal taxonomy. No roles were assigned.

## Existing structure checked

The two current labels—Investment & Fund Advisers and Market Traders & Brokers—are ISCO-derived occupational buckets with unverified memberships. They do not cover the investment workflow coherently and do not determine this review's count.

## Recommended areas

1. **Investment Research and Valuation** — research issuers/securities, value opportunities and communicate evidence for decisions.
2. **Portfolio Management and Asset Allocation** — allocate, construct and manage portfolios against objectives, constraints, risk and execution needs.
3. **Private Markets Investing** — evaluate, structure, manage and exit private investments and fund positions.
4. **Private Wealth and Client Investment Management** — integrate investment decisions with individual/family goals, constraints, planning and liquidity contexts.

Performance measurement, attribution and manager selection are cross-cutting in the reviewed CFA framework (and also have a focused CIPM curriculum), so this audit does not make them a fifth default area. The computational catalogue scan found 22 source-field hits for D013; these are candidate signals, not proof of a computational subgroup. Quantitative methods are attached by investment task; systematic strategy/model engineering overlaps D011 and must not be inferred from tool keywords.

## Evidence and limits

- **CFA Level III 2026** offers Portfolio Management, Private Markets and Private Wealth pathways following common core. [Source](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/candidate-resources/level-iii-exam)
- **CFA specialized pathway descriptions** distinguish public portfolio workflows, private investment/GP-LP processes, and private-client wealth management; performance measurement remains common core. [Source](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/candidate-resources/level-iii-exam/specialized-pathways)
- **CFA Equity Valuation 2026** describes analyst valuation/research activity as informing investment decisions. [Source](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/equity-valuation-applications-and-processes)
- **CFA Candidate Body of Knowledge** includes investment analysis, asset allocation, portfolio construction, alternatives, performance and manager selection. [Source](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/candidate-resources/cbok)
- **CFA CIPM curriculum** provides a focused performance-measurement and manager-selection curriculum, while the CFA Level III structure places these in a common core. [Source](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cipm/curriculum)

These are professional curricula, not an exhaustive occupation taxonomy. They support workflow distinctions but do not mandate the proposed four areas. No role IDs, ISCO membership or role counts were used to infer area fit.
