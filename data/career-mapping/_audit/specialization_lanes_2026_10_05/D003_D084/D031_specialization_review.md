# D031 Sales and Business Development — specialization structure review

**Status:** Source-reviewed candidate set; pending catalogue-wide reconciliation. This is not a universally prescribed subgroup count and does not adjudicate individual role links.

## Finding

D031 currently has four visible ISCO-derived rows across 122 role mappings: Sales & Marketing Leadership, Technical Sales Specialists, Buying & Trading, and Retail & Shop Sales. These rows mix management level, seller-side technical practice, buyer/intermediary activities, and a sales context. The current rows and role counts do not establish the correct student-facing specialization structure.

ILO's occupation/task framework distinguishes technical sales representatives, shop sales and purchasing functions by their actual work. Global professional bodies separately describe business development, strategic/key account management, technical sales, sales operations/enablement, and channel/partner practices. Taken together, the sources support a provisional six-area navigation synthesis, not a standard taxonomy with a fixed count.

## Proposed student-facing areas

1. Business Development and Partnerships
2. Strategic and Key Account Management
3. Technical and Solution Sales
4. Retail and Wholesale Sales
5. Channel and Partner Sales
6. Sales Operations and Enablement

Keep the domain foundation to knowledge and capabilities genuinely shared across these routes. Each area adds its distinct work; role-specific requirements are added only after the independent task-based role audit. Relationship management and negotiation may be shared competencies, not exclusive branches. Sales leadership is progression/responsibility, not automatically a peer specialization.

## Fix and boundary disposition

- Replace or retire the current occupation-family subgroup labels only after catalogue-wide identifier and naming reconciliation.
- Do not retain “Buying & Trading” as a confirmed D031 student area. Buyer-side procurement/sourcing should be considered under the relevant procurement/supply domain only after reviewing the exact occupation tasks.
- Do not automatically reassign brokerage, commodity trading or intermediary roles. Their domain depends on the product/market, regulatory context and actual duties; role labels alone are insufficient.
- Keep marketing demand creation and campaign ownership in Marketing; product roadmap ownership in Product Management; buying/supply management in Procurement/Supply Chain; and finance/investment trading in the applicable finance domain.
- Do not infer that all 122 role links fit D031 or any proposed area from current subgroup membership, ISCO code, or frequency. The separate role-to-domain task audit must justify each link from role tasks and domain scope.
- Treat the six areas as a student-navigation synthesis from global practice evidence, not as a fixed count required by a global authority. Local sales, consumer, brokerage, commodity-market and licensing requirements must be labelled by jurisdiction.

## Sources

- [ILO — ISCO-08](https://isco.ilo.org/en/isco-08/): job classification is task/duty based; the classification distinguishes sales and purchasing work. ISCO is an occupation taxonomy, not a student specialization standard.
- [Sales Management Association — About](https://salesmanagement.org/about/): sales operations, enablement, commercial effectiveness and sales leadership as functions supporting sales organizations.
- [Strategic Account Management Association — Who We Are](https://blog.strategicaccounts.org/about/): strategic, key and global account management as a distinct practice community and career path.
- [International Technical Sales Association](https://www.itsa-global.org/): technical-sales capability framework covering discovery, solution development, value communication, negotiation and opportunity management.
- [Business Development Association — How BDA Works](https://bda-global.org/about-bda/how-bda-works/): one association's competency framework distinguishing longer-term opportunity/partnership development from sales conversion and marketing; this is single-body evidence, not universal consensus.
- [IFPSM — Constitution](https://www.ifpsm.org/_files/ugd/496c58_ec04077d80e44e408b09a8db2d74e142.pdf?index=true): purchasing and supply management field includes purchasing, supply chain, logistics, contracts, inventory and materials planning.
