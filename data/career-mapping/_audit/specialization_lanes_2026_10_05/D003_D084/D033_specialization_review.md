# D033 Product Management — specialization structure review

**Status:** Source-reviewed candidate set; pending catalogue-wide reconciliation. This is not a universally mandated subgroup taxonomy.

## Finding

D033 currently has three ISCO-derived rows across 51 role links: Product & Programme Management (22), Business Analysis & Estimation (8), and Technical & Data Engineering (21). The first crosses into the separately named D032 Project and Programme Management area; the second can support product decisions but is not inherently product management; the third mixes product accountability with hands-on technical implementation. Current subgroup labels and role totals do not establish specialization fit.

PDMA's globally applicable Product Development and Management Body of Knowledge covers product strategy, portfolio, process, design/development, market research, culture/teams and product lifecycle. It applies across product and service industries. Those source topics support a local student-navigation synthesis, but they do not prescribe subgroups or validate D033's 51 role links.

## Proposed student-facing areas

1. Product Strategy and Portfolio Direction
2. Product Discovery and Market Insights
3. Product Planning and Lifecycle Management
4. Product Innovation and Development

Here, “product portfolio” means choices and investment across products/product lines. It is distinct from D032's project/programme portfolio governance and from managing a temporary project delivery. Product marketing, hands-on engineering and data engineering also remain distinct practices.

## Fix and boundary disposition

- Replace the three occupation-family subgroup labels only after catalogue-wide identifier and parent-domain reconciliation.
- Do not set the number of areas from the three current ISCO buckets or their role counts.
- Keep product strategy/portfolio, product discovery, product planning/lifecycle and innovation/development as candidate branches. Show common product foundations once; selected branches add their own learning.
- Keep product marketing and go-to-market communication under Marketing (D030), project/programme delivery under D032, and implementation engineering/data work in the appropriate technical domain unless role-task evidence says otherwise.
- Distinguish product portfolio investment/line decisions from organizational project/programme portfolio governance; do not silently broaden D032 or D033.
- Do not assign any of the 51 roles to a candidate area from their current subgroup, ISCO code or title. The separate role lane must assess actual task/accountability against the parent-domain scope.
- Stage-Gate, Agile, Lean and other innovation processes are contextual methods; none should be treated as required for all product roles.
