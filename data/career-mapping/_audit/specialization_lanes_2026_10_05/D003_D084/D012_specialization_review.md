# D012 specialization review — FinTech Engineering

**Disposition:** Recommend three learner-facing areas anchored in the financial system being engineered: payments, digital banking/credit, and financial-market/investment infrastructure. APIs, AI, cloud, identity, security and DLT are cross-cutting enablers, not automatic subgroups. No roles were mapped.

## Existing structure checked

The current four cards include two copied ISCO headings and two broad functional labels. Memberships are unverified. They do not determine the three-area recommendation; role fit is explicitly deferred.

## Recommended areas

1. **Digital Payments and Transaction Systems** — payment initiation, processing, routing, clearing, settlement, reconciliation and payment infrastructure connections.
2. **Digital Banking and Credit Platforms** — digital account/deposit, lending and credit-service platforms and their product integrations.
3. **Financial-Market and Investment Infrastructure** — technology for trading/brokerage, securities processing, custody-related interfaces and post-trade workflows.

APIs/open finance, cloud, AI/ML, cybersecurity, identity/consent and DLT span multiple service areas. Local licensing, consumer-protection and payment rules vary by jurisdiction and must be labelled as local context. A DLT implementation in finance does not automatically make every such pathway part of the separate Blockchain domain.

## Evidence and limits

- **FSB FinTech function taxonomy**, Figure 1: distinguishes economic functions (payments/clearing/settlement; deposits/lending; investment services; market infrastructure) from innovation/enabler technologies (mobile/web, APIs/cloud, DLT, e-trading). [PDF](https://www.fsb.org/uploads/R270617.pdf)
- **BIS CPMI mandate**: international standard-setting scope for payment systems, securities settlement and other financial-market infrastructures. [Source](https://www.bis.org/committees/cpmi/overview)
- **BIS CPMI Payment Aspects of Financial Inclusion in the FinTech Era**, §§2 and 4.3: payment-system components, front-end arrangements, back-end processing/clearing/settlement, infrastructure and platform dependencies. [PDF](https://www.bis.org/cpmi/publ/d191.pdf)
- **World Bank Digital Financial Services**: cross-country digital finance, infrastructure and open APIs, with policy/regulatory context. [Source](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/financialsector/publication/digital-financial-services)
- **World Bank DFS glossary**, API/Open Banking entries: payment, data, ecosystem-expansion, consent and identity APIs as integrations across providers/services; includes country-specific examples. [Source](https://digitalfinance.worldbank.org/glossary)

These sources support service-function distinctions, not a complete occupational taxonomy or a mandated three-area count. Product regulation varies globally; local details require explicit jurisdiction labels. No role IDs, ISCO membership or role counts were used to infer specialization fit.
