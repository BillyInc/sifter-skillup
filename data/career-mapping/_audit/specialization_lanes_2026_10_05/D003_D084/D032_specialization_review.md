# D032 Project and Programme Management — specialization structure review

**Status:** Source-reviewed candidate set; pending catalogue-wide reconciliation. The proposed count is a student-navigation synthesis, not an official fixed subgroup count.

## Finding

D032 currently has three visible subgroups across 38 role mappings: “Programme & Project Managers” (12), “Business & Process Analysts” (8), and “Project & Admin Support Officers” (18). These are ISCO-derived occupation groupings, not validated areas of specialization. The labels mix management level, analysis work, project coordination and general administration.

ISO/TC 258 separately scopes project management (ISO 21502) and programme management (ISO 21503), and its standards catalogue separately lists portfolio management and governance guidance. APM's competence framework spans project, programme, portfolio and PMO practice; APM also describes project controls as an analytical practice with specialist planners, schedulers and risk managers on larger work. This supports distinct learning branches, but not a prescribed number.

## Proposed student-facing areas

1. Project Delivery and Lifecycle Management
2. Programme Management and Benefits Realisation
3. Project Controls, Planning and Performance
4. Project Management Office (PMO) Practice and Assurance

These areas preserve the current parent title. Portfolio management is not silently added as a D032 subgroup because the title excludes it and international/professional frameworks distinguish it from project and programme management. A portfolio path should be placed after separate parent-domain reconciliation.

## Fix and boundary disposition

- Replace the three occupation-family labels only after catalogue-wide ID, naming and cross-domain reconciliation.
- Do not derive specialization count from the current three ISCO buckets or their role counts.
- Keep project delivery, programme integration/benefits, project controls and PMO as candidate branches; show shared project/programme foundations once and add the selected branch's distinct learning.
- Treat agile, predictive, iterative and hybrid as delivery approaches within project practice, not as separate student specializations by default.
- Keep portfolio management outside D032 pending a domain-boundary decision. Do not move or add roles solely because their titles mention portfolio, programme, office or manager.
- Business analysis/process improvement, organization-wide change management, procurement and general office administration are adjacent capabilities/functions. Their correct parent domain depends on actual tasks and must be checked against the separate role-domain audit.
- Do not infer that all 38 current role links fit D032 or one proposed area from ISCO membership, current subgroup membership or title. The role audit must decide each parent-domain link from tasks and evidence.
- Local public procurement, construction, employment and governance requirements must be explicitly jurisdiction-labelled; the practice structure itself is not Nigeria-specific.

## Sources

- [ISO/TC 258 — ISO 21502:2020, project management](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc258/home/projects/published/iso-21502.html): organization- and sector-agnostic project guidance, spanning predictive, iterative, adaptive, hybrid and agile approaches; explicitly separate from programme/portfolio guidance.
- [ISO/TC 258 — ISO 21503:2022, programme management](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc258/home/projects/published/iso-21503.html): programme-management scope and practices across organization types, sizes and sectors.
- [ISO/TC 258 — published standards list](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc258/home/projects/published/iso-21502.html): lists ISO 21502 project, 21503 programme, 21504 portfolio and 21505 governance guidance as distinct deliverables.
- [APM — Competence Framework](https://www.apm.org.uk/resources/find-a-resource/competence-framework/): 29 competences span project, programme, portfolio management and PMO.
- [APM — What is project controls?](https://www.apm.org.uk/resources/what-is-project-management/what-is-project-controls/): controls cover time, cost, risk, change, performance, reporting and information; specialist roles become more distinct with project scale/complexity.
- [APM — What is portfolio management?](https://www.apm.org.uk/resources/what-is-project-management/what-is-portfolio-management/): portfolio selection, prioritisation and control align programmes/projects with strategy and delivery capacity.
- [PMI — What are Project Management Standards?](https://www.pmi.org/standards/about): consensus-based standards catalogue identifies separate ISO project, programme, portfolio and governance standards.
