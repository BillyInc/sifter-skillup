# D028 General Management — specialization structure review

**Status:** Source-reviewed provisional candidate set; pending role and cross-domain reconciliation. This is not external academic sign-off.

## Finding

D028 has three current subgroup cards and 45 active role mappings. The cards currently mix executive seniority/founder status, a broad administrative-and-commercial manager family, and business analyst/consultant occupations. Those are not a consistent set of student-facing areas of specialization.

The wider catalogue already separates accounting/finance, marketing, project and programme management, product management, supply chain, logistics, procurement, construction, hospitality and industrial operations. A manager title alone must not pull a function-specific or sector-specific role into General Management. The role-to-domain lane must independently compare all 45 tasks with D028's parent scope.

## Provisional areas of specialization

International management frameworks support the practice themes below, but do not prescribe a universal General Management subgroup count. This four-area organization is an internal synthesis for student navigation:

1. Strategy, Leadership and Organizational Governance
2. Business Operations and Process Management
3. Organizational Performance, Quality and Improvement
4. Management Analysis and Advisory

EFQM distinguishes organizational direction, execution and results; ISO 9001 addresses sector-neutral leadership, processes, performance evaluation and continual improvement; CMI provides a management capability framework (UK context); ILO ISCO classifies jobs/occupations rather than defining a learner's specializations. These sources support boundaries and learning themes, not one official four-part taxonomy.

## Fix disposition

- Do not retain current ISCO-derived groups as validated specialization labels. Preserve their occupational-source provenance separately from student-facing area taxonomy.
- Do not assign roles to candidate areas from present subgroup membership. First disposition all 45 role-to-domain links from their actual tasks, then compare surviving roles with the specialization boundaries.
- Keep financial, marketing, project, product, procurement, supply-chain, logistics and sector-operations work in its relevant specialist domain unless task evidence supports a cross-functional General Management route.
- Treat quality-system standards as organizational frameworks, not individual certifications.

## Sources

- [ILO — ISCO-08](https://isco.ilo.org/en/isco-08/): international occupation classification; group definitions describe job scope and task-based classification.
- [EFQM — The EFQM Model](https://efqm.org/the-efqm-model/): Direction, Execution and Results dimensions for organizational management and performance.
- [ISO — ISO 9001:2026](https://committee.iso.org/standard/9001): sector-neutral quality-management system framework covering leadership, operations, evaluation and improvement.
- [CMI — Professional Standard](https://www.managers.org.uk/education-and-learning/professional-standards/professional-standard/): management capabilities across personal effectiveness, interpersonal excellence and organizational performance; UK professional context.
- [ISO — ISO 21502:2020](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc258/home/projects/published/iso-21502.html): cross-sector project-management guidance, supporting separation from General Management.
