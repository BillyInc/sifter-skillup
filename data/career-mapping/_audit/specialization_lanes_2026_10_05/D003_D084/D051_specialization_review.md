The current subgroups are Administrative and Commercial Managers (1 role), Hospitality/Retail/Other Services Managers (1), Business and Administration Professionals (1), and Business and Administration Associate Professionals (5). The present domain description spans real-property transactions, management, mortgage finance and conveyancing, but should not imply that generic capital-markets brokerage, business valuation, insurance or cultural-institution operations are Real Estate. Conveyancing and mortgage licensing depend on jurisdiction; avoid presenting local credentials as global requirements.

## Provisional areas of specialization

RICS pathway competencies and international valuation standards support these candidate areas. They do **not** decree Sifter’s exact subgroup count:

1. **Property Agency, Transactions and Leasing** — property-specific acquisition/disposal, agency, leasing and due diligence.
2. **Real Estate Valuation and Appraisal** — valuation of land, buildings and property interests; keep development appraisal distinct from valuation.
3. **Property and Facilities Management** — property operations, landlord/tenant work, building services and occupier/owner needs.
4. **Real Estate Development and Planning** — site/development appraisal, feasibility, planning constraints and project delivery.
5. **Real Estate Finance and Investment** — property-specific funding, mortgages, investment analysis, cash flows and portfolios.

Commercial, residential and rural are market/property contexts for testing these paths; do not create separate subgroups unless they produce distinct supported learning routes. Conveyancing may be attached to transaction work only with jurisdiction-specific legal context.

## Computational relationships

Property analytics, financial models, GIS/spatial information, valuation tools and property-management systems can support particular tasks. They should be attached to the relevant transaction, valuation, development, investment or management path—not promoted to a generic computational subgroup. The exact tool-to-task relationship still needs evidence.

## Sources and limits

- [RICS sector pathways](https://www.rics.org/join-rics/sector-pathways) and [Commercial Real Estate pathway guide](https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/join-rics/COMMERCIAL-Real-Estate_January-2026.pdf) distinguish competencies such as management, leasing, purchase/sale, valuation and development appraisal.
- [RICS Property Finance and Investment pathway](https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/join-rics/property_finance_investment_pathway_guide_chartered_rics.pdf) says pathways are global while markets vary by country; it supports a distinct property finance/investment route, not a universal mortgage licence.
- [International Valuation Standards Council](https://ivsc.org/standards/) separates real-property interests and development property from business and intangible-asset valuation.
- [RICS purchase/sale competency](https://ww3.rics.org/uk/en/journals/property-journal/how-to-attain-the-purchase-and-sale-competency-.html) grounds property transaction tasks; [RICS valuation competency guidance](https://www.rics.org/content/rics/uk/en/journals/property-journal/apc-5-valuation-methods.html) distinguishes development appraisal from valuation.
