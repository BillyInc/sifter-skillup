# D096 — Agricultural Machinery and Farm Operations

Global specialization recommendation reconciliation (2026-10-06). This is an internal recommendation, not canonical taxonomy or external academic sign-off.

- **Candidate areas:** 2
- **Evidence URLs:** 3 unique; 3 source records screened as global/international
- **Canonical changes / role assignments:** none / none
- **Geographic scope:** global phase only; Nigerian-specific evidence intentionally deferred

## Recommendation

Replace the three ISCO-derived records with two student-facing areas: Agricultural Machinery Operation and Mechanization Services; and Agricultural Machinery Maintenance and Repair. These represent distinct but connected work: operating equipment for field/harvest/post-harvest tasks and maintaining/repairing farm power, machinery and implements. Do not create a separate subgroup for every crop operation or machine type. Exclude forestry equipment from the student-facing D096 specializations pending the independent role→domain audit and keep farm production/agronomy outside this machinery domain unless the parent scope is deliberately revised with evidence.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Agricultural Machinery Operation and Mechanization Services.** Safely select and operate agricultural power sources, tractors, implements and equipment for field, harvest and relevant post-harvest operations, including mechanization services for farms.
  - Source claim: FAO's training manual covers tractors, power take-off, implements and field operations; FAO's hire-service guidance documents mechanized services for land preparation, planting, spraying, harvest, irrigation and post-harvest operations.
- **Agricultural Machinery Maintenance and Repair.** Inspect, maintain, diagnose, overhaul and repair tractors, agricultural power sources, implements and other farm machinery, including replacing parts and returning equipment to safe working condition.
  - Source claim: FAO's manual devotes a module to maintenance, spare parts and replacement and its equipment course covers tractor-system maintenance and proper repair service provision across agricultural machines.

## Source evidence and limits

- [Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations — Training of trainers manual on the operation, maintenance and repair of farm machinery](https://www.fao.org/family-farming/detail/en/c/1512764/) — Overview and four modules: tractors/power take-off; tractor implements and field operations; harvest/post-harvest/storage equipment; maintenance, spare parts and replacement. **Supports:** Farm mechanization includes two distinguishable work areas: operating equipment across field and post-harvest operations, and maintaining/repairing machinery and its power sources. **Limit:** Global FAO technical/training resource with adaptable examples; not an academic accreditation or universal occupational taxonomy/count. Country and machine requirements require local adaptation. Geographic scope: international, context-adapted. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations — Hire Services for Sustainable Agricultural Mechanization](https://www.fao.org/sustainable-agricultural-mechanization/hire-services-as-a-business/hire-services/en/) — Hire-service scope lists land preparation, planting, spraying, threshing, shelling, transport, irrigation/pumping, drying, winnowing, grading and storage; describes providers and equipment service enterprises. **Supports:** Mechanized agricultural field/post-harvest operations and provision of machinery services form a coherent practice family beyond merely driving a tractor. **Limit:** Practice and business models vary by local farm systems, finance and equipment access; the page does not prescribe a learner subgroup taxonomy or number. Geographic scope: international guidance with local adaptation. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, FAO eLearning Academy — Operation and maintenance of agricultural equipment for hire service businesses](https://elearning.fao.org/course/view.php?id=780) — Course outline and learning topics: proper operation, maintenance and repair; tractor systems; seeders, planters, sprayers, post-harvest equipment and pumps; audience includes farmers, cooperatives, mechanization service providers and specialists. **Supports:** Operation/service provision and maintenance/repair are separately describable competency sets within agricultural mechanization, though they can be combined in smallholder or multi-role contexts. **Limit:** A course curriculum is supporting practice evidence, not global accreditation or a claim that every learner must earn its digital badge. Local adaptation is required. Geographic scope: international course for context-adapted use. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.

## Open limits / reconciliation notes

- The two-area set is a source-based synthesis, not a globally ratified taxonomy or academic accreditation decision.
- FAO references are globally oriented technical guidance but explicitly require adaptation to equipment, farming systems, resources and local conditions.
- The current D096 root name/description spans machinery and farm operations; later domain-name/scope review should determine whether to simplify it to Agricultural Mechanization and Farm Machinery or retain a broader farm-operations scope with additional evidence.
- Forestry role placement is unresolved until task-based D096 versus D121 role→domain decisions are reconciled; this subgroup review does not move those roles.

No roles were assigned to these areas. Current subgroup counts, ISCO grouping, role frequency and computational keyword hits were not used to determine candidate count. Nigeria-specific context is explicitly out of scope in this global phase.
