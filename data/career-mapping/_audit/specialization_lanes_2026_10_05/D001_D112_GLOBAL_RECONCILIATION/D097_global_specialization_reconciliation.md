# D097 — Welding, Fabrication and Blacksmithing

Global specialization recommendation reconciliation (2026-10-06). This is an internal recommendation, not canonical taxonomy or external academic sign-off.

- **Candidate areas:** 3
- **Evidence URLs:** 6 unique; 3 source records screened as global/international
- **Canonical changes / role assignments:** none / none
- **Geographic scope:** global phase only; Nigerian-specific evidence intentionally deferred

## Recommendation

Replace the generic excluded ISCO bucket with three candidate areas: Welding and Joining; Metal Fabrication and Structural Assembly; and Forging and Blacksmithing. The proposed set separates controlled joining, preparation/assembly of metal products and structures, and hot-forging/hand- or machine-shaped metal craft. Treat processes/materials (MIG/MAG, TIG, arc, cutting, sheet metal, structural steel, aluminum, etc.) as themes/competencies within the appropriate area, not as separate subgroup cards. This is a source-based synthesis, not a globally mandated count.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Welding and Joining.** Join metal parts using suitable manual, mechanized or automated welding and related joining processes, following drawings, procedures, safety controls and quality requirements.
  - Source claim: WorldSkills' international Welding standard defines welding as controlled joining of metals and alloys, with preparation/assembly, drawing interpretation, process selection, safety and quality; IIW provides globally harmonized welding personnel qualifications.
- **Metal Fabrication and Structural Assembly.** Interpret fabrication drawings, cut and form metal stock, prepare and fit components, and assemble metal products or structures using appropriate joining methods.
  - Source claim: WorldSkills' welding standard includes fabrication drawings and preparation/assembly, while IIW/ISO 3834 identifies welding fabrication as a lifecycle managed from design to delivery, supporting a broader fabrication/assembly focus around but not reducible to welding.
- **Forging and Blacksmithing.** Heat, shape, forge-join and finish metal by hand or machine to make, repair or conserve tools, components and bespoke metalwork.
  - Source claim: Skills England's Blacksmith standard describes hot forging and small-batch/bespoke or heritage work; the Austrian AMS apprenticeship and Ontario trade standard independently corroborate a named blacksmith/forging practice outside England.

## Source evidence and limits

- [WorldSkills International — WorldSkills Occupational Standards: Welding (2024)](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1672/) — Occupation description and standards sections on preparation/assembly, welding processes, positions, materials, fabrication drawings/symbols, safety and quality. **Supports:** Welding and joining are a distinct skilled practice involving controlled joining processes, material and procedure selection, interpretation of fabrication drawings, and quality/safety requirements. **Limit:** International WorldSkills benchmark for a competition-defined occupation; technical processes and qualification/regulatory requirements vary by material, sector and jurisdiction. It does not prescribe the full domain taxonomy. Geographic scope: international reference. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [International Institute of Welding — Qualification and Certification of Personnel](https://iiwelding.org/iiw-qualification-and-certification-of-personnel/) — Personnel framework lists International Welder, welding practitioners, inspectors, technologists, engineers and mechanised/orbital/robotised welding personnel; training/certification delivered via authorized national bodies under harmonized rules. **Supports:** Welding work has recognized levels and technical emphases, including manual/automated joining and inspection/coordination; these are competency/role distinctions and do not all require separate student-facing specializations. **Limit:** IIW qualification systems demonstrate defined pathways but not a universal legal requirement for every welding job; delivery and accepted codes depend on local authorization and sector requirements. Geographic scope: international scheme through national authorized bodies. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [International Institute of Welding (IIW) / ISO 3834 framework — Certification of the welding fabrication process - ISO 3834](https://iiwelding.org/iiw-iso-3834/) — Overview describes management of welding-fabrication activities from design to delivery, manufacturer capability and staff competence. **Supports:** Welding is embedded in a broader fabrication lifecycle with design, production controls and delivery/quality management; this supports making fabrication a distinct but related learning area rather than treating all metalwork as weld execution. **Limit:** A quality framework is not itself an occupational taxonomy and does not prove every fabricator welds or every welder performs design/management. Certification applicability depends on organization, standard and jurisdiction. Geographic scope: international framework. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [Skills England, Department for Education — Blacksmith, ST0378 v1.1](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeships/st0378-v1-1) — Occupational profile: design, shape and join metal by hot forging/other processes for small-batch/bespoke production and heritage conservation; duty areas include forging, thermal welding/cutting, machining, bench work, fabrication, fitting and tool maintenance. **Supports:** Blacksmithing/forging is a distinct metal-shaping craft and practice, including hot forming and bespoke/heritage work, not merely a synonym for welding or structural fabrication. **Limit:** England-specific apprenticeship standard; its apprenticeship level, duration and qualification rules are not global. The occupational profile corroborates task boundaries only. Geographic scope: England/United Kingdom. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.
- [Austrian Public Employment Service (AMS) Berufsinformationssystem — Blacksmith / Forging Technician Apprenticeship](https://bis.ams.or.at/bis/lehrberuf-ausdruck/5314?language=en) — Official apprenticeship profile and occupational classification for SchmiedetechnikerIn (forging technician/blacksmith), with related apprenticeship fields. **Supports:** A separate public occupational/apprenticeship record in Austria corroborates blacksmithing/forging as an established named metalworking practice outside the UK example. **Limit:** Austrian national apprenticeship/classification record; details, route and legal recognition are Austria-specific. It corroborates the practice name, not an international qualification or subgroup count. Geographic scope: Austria. **Scope screen:** geographic_scope_not_established.
- [Skilled Trades Ontario — Blacksmith (600P) Trade Standard / Program Summary](https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/wp-content/uploads/1970/01/Blacksmith-600P-EN-SOT-V100-2000.pdf) — Scope of practice and apprenticeship program summary: forging and repairing metal parts for farm machinery and industrial/domestic equipment. **Supports:** A Canadian provincial trade standard provides independent cross-region corroboration of blacksmithing/forging as a distinct trade practice that can overlap with repair and fabrication. **Limit:** Ontario trade regulation and apprenticeship hours are provincial and not globally applicable. Used only as comparative task-scope evidence. Geographic scope: Ontario, Canada. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.

## Open limits / reconciliation notes

- The three-area structure is an internal synthesis, not a globally ratified taxonomy or academic accreditation decision.
- WorldSkills and IIW provide international references; blacksmith evidence comes from national/provincial standards and is used comparatively to confirm an established craft, not to universalize local apprenticeship rules.
- The domain name explicitly includes blacksmithing; domain-level review should later assess whether this established craft remains within one broad metalwork domain or deserves a separate parent domain based on the full catalogue and student progression needs.
- The current canonical role set likely includes machine operators and assemblers outside the parent scope; the separate role→domain lane must reconcile each one before path implementation.
