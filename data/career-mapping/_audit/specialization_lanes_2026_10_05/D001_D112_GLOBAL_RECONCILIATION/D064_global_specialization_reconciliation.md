## Recommendation

Replace the four occupational buckets with eight candidate student-facing areas: Clinical Psychology; Counselling Psychology and Psychotherapy; Educational, School and Developmental Psychology; Health and Community Psychology; Forensic Psychology; Work and Organizational Psychology; Sport and Exercise Psychology; and Psychological Research and Assessment. Keep clinical neuropsychology as a clearly named advanced theme/pathway under Clinical Psychology pending full curriculum/role-boundary review, rather than assuming a separate card is globally appropriate. This eight-area proposal synthesizes areas that recur across official US, UK and Australian professional recognition structures; the bodies do not prescribe a common international count or identical title boundaries.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Clinical Psychology.** Assess and support people experiencing significant psychological distress or mental-health conditions using evidence-informed psychological assessment, formulation and intervention.
- **Counselling Psychology and Psychotherapy.** Apply psychological theory and therapeutic relationships to support people with life challenges, wellbeing, relationships, adjustment and distress, using appropriate assessment and evidence-informed interventions.
- **Educational, School and Developmental Psychology.** Understand development and learning, assess educational and developmental needs, and support children, young people, families, educators and learning systems.
- **Health and Community Psychology.** Apply psychology to health behaviour, prevention, healthcare settings, population wellbeing and community-level influences on mental and physical health.
- **Forensic Psychology.** Apply psychological knowledge to questions arising in legal, justice, correctional and related settings, using appropriate assessment, evidence and ethical safeguards.
- **Work and Organizational Psychology.** Use psychological science to understand people at work and improve selection, learning, performance, wellbeing, teams and organizational systems.
- **Sport and Exercise Psychology.** Apply psychology to performance, participation, motivation, wellbeing and behaviour in sport and physical activity contexts.
- **Psychological Research and Assessment.** Design and conduct psychological research; measure behaviour and psychological constructs; evaluate evidence and communicate findings responsibly.

## Open limits / reconciliation notes

- APA evidence is United States-specific; professional recognition and accredited pathways are not globally transferable.
- The protected practitioner psychologist titles and regulator evidence are United Kingdom-specific; UK legal title protection does not define practice globally.
- Psychology Board of Australia endorsement areas are Australia-specific, and the source cautions that an endorsement is not a separate specialist register; these categories cannot be copied as universal legal categories.
- Cross-region recurrence shows established study/practice areas, not a globally standardized specialization taxonomy or common training sequence.
- The eight-area set is a synthesis. The proposed health/community grouping and nested neuropsychology should be reconsidered with curriculum and full-catalogue evidence.
- Counselling/psychotherapy scope, registration, title protections, assessment authority and clinical practice rules vary by jurisdiction.
