## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Chemical Process-Plant Operations.** Monitor and control chemical production processes and plant equipment, following process, safety and quality procedures.
- **Rubber and Plastics Processing.** Operate processes that compound, mould, extrude or otherwise convert polymers, rubber and plastics into products.
