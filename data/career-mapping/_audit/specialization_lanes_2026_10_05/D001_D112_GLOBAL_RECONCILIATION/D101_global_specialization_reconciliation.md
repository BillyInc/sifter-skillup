## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Building Painting and Decorative Finishes.** Prepare and finish interior/exterior building surfaces with paint, wallpaper, texture and decorative coatings.
- **Industrial Protective Coatings and Surface Preparation.** Prepare industrial substrates and apply/inspect protective coating systems for corrosion and durability.
