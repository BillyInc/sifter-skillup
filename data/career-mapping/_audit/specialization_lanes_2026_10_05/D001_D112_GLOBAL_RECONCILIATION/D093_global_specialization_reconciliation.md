# D093 — Electrical Installation, Wiring and Maintenance

Global specialization recommendation reconciliation (2026-10-06). This is an internal recommendation, not canonical taxonomy or external academic sign-off.

- **Candidate areas:** 2
- **Evidence URLs:** 6 unique; 2 source records screened as global/international
- **Canonical changes / role assignments:** none / none
- **Geographic scope:** global phase only; Nigerian-specific evidence intentionally deferred

## Recommendation

Replace the excluded ISCO bucket with two candidate areas: Building and Commercial Electrical Installation; and Electrical Power Networks and Distribution. The international WorldSkills standard describes broad installation/maintenance practice across building and industrial environments, while CIGRE's global technical structure and separate network occupational standards distinguish transmission/distribution assets, cables, overhead lines and substations. This is a source-grounded synthesis, not an externally mandated two-part taxonomy. Keep industrial automation, electronics, and vehicle/marine/rail electrical systems outside the proposed specialization set unless role-task/domain-scope review proves a specific D093 fit; their presence in the legacy parent description or role library alone is not sufficient.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Building and Commercial Electrical Installation.** Install, test, commission, maintain and repair electrical wiring and systems in residential, commercial and comparable built environments.
  - Source claim: The WorldSkills Electrical Installations occupational standard describes wiring, switchboards, lighting, controls, verification, commissioning and maintenance across residential/commercial and related installation contexts; Skills England's installation-electrician standard corroborates the broad premises/electrical-systems profile.
- **Electrical Power Networks and Distribution.** Work with electricity transmission and distribution infrastructure, including network cables, overhead lines, substations, protection, operation and asset maintenance.
  - Source claim: CIGRE's international committees separately organize T&D equipment, cables, lines, substations, protection and network operations; Skills England standards for power-network engineers, distribution cable jointers and overhead linespersons provide explicit task-based corroboration.

## Source evidence and limits

- [WorldSkills International — WorldSkills Occupational Standards: Electrical Installations (2024)](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1677/) — Occupation description; sections on installation (35%), testing/reporting/commissioning (25%), maintenance/fault finding/repair (15%); work settings include commercial, residential, agricultural and industrial, and installation examples include building automation, solar/wind generation and EV charging. **Supports:** A coherent electrician installation-and-maintenance practice spans building and commercial installation contexts, commissioning, inspection, fault-finding and newer customer-side systems. **Limit:** International occupational reference for WorldSkills competition standards, not a binding global curriculum or endorsement of a particular subgroup label/count. It does not establish that all vehicle, marine, rail or industrial-automation roles fit D093. Geographic scope: international reference. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [Skills England, Department for Education — Installation and Maintenance Electrician, ST0152 v1.2](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeships/st0152) — Occupation summary and duties: electrical installation, initial verification/testing, commissioning and maintenance in industrial, commercial and residential settings, including low-carbon technologies; includes electrical maintenance of automated production systems. **Supports:** A national occupational standard corroborates the broad installation-and-maintenance practice area and shows renewables/automation as work content within it, not automatically separate specializations. **Limit:** This is a UK apprenticeship standard and its safety rules/qualifications are UK-specific; it is corroborating evidence only, not a global credential or rule. Geographic scope: United Kingdom. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.
- [CIGRE, International Council on Large Electric Systems — Our 16 Study Committees and Domains of Work](https://www.cigre.org/GB/knowledge-programme/our-16-study-committees-and-domains-of-work) — Study Committees A3 transmission/distribution equipment, B1 insulated cables, B2 overhead lines, B3 substations/electrical installations, B5 protection/automation, C2 system operation/control, and C6 active distribution systems/distributed resources. **Supports:** Power-network and distribution practice is a technically organized domain with distinct cable, overhead-line, substation, protection, operations and active-distribution areas, distinct from ordinary premises electrical installation. **Limit:** CIGRE is an international professional technical organization; its committee structure is technical scope, not an academic curriculum or prescribed learner subgroup count. Geographic scope: international. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [Skills England, Department for Education — Electrical Power Networks Engineer, ST0475 v1.0](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeship-standards/st0475-v1-0) — Occupational profile and duties for network asset design/planning/control/project/operations engineering, electricity-network construction, operation, maintenance and repair; employer-defined specialist options include asset management, planning, design, control, project and operational delivery. **Supports:** Power-network engineering and operation are a distinct practice context from premises installation and maintenance, corroborating a separate power-networks specialization at D093's current broad scope boundary. **Limit:** UK apprenticeship standard; its regulatory requirements and professional-registration route are national. The source supports a practice distinction, not a global subgroup count. Geographic scope: United Kingdom. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.

Geographic scope: United Kingdom. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.

Geographic scope: United Kingdom. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.
