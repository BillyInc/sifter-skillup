## Recommendation

Two candidate areas capture design/product development and hands-on manufacture, but remain provisional: the international Jewellery WSOS integrates design interpretation and manufacture in one occupational standard, while the English standard enumerates nine technical functions. Those sources establish breadth, not a universal two-route learner taxonomy.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Jewellery Design and Digital Product Development.** Develop jewellery concepts and technical designs, including sketches or CAD/CAM models, specifications and production-ready details.
- **Jewellery Making, Setting and Finishing.** Form, cast, assemble, set stones, engrave/enamel where relevant, polish, repair and finish jewellery or precious-metal objects.
