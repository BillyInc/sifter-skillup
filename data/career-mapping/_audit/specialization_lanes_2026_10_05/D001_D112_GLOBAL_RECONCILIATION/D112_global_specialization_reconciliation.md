## Recommendation

Two primary process areas are supported. Paper converting/finishing remains a topic or downstream process under pulp/paper until a distinct curriculum/industry route demonstrates independent learner needs; do not display it as a confirmed third specialization. Source-gap disposition recorded as `two_primary_process_families_supported_converting_not_separately_established`.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Timber and Engineered-Wood Processing.** Operate industrial processes that convert logs and wood inputs into sawn timber, veneer, panels, pellets and related products.
- **Pulp and Paper Manufacture.** Operate pulping, bleaching, stock preparation and paper-machine processes to make pulp and paper.
- **Paper Converting and Finishing (not a separate area yet).** Convert paper stock into finished products through cutting, folding, coating or related finishing operations.
