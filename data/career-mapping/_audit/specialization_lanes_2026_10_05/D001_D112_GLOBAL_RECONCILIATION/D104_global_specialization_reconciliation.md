## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Pottery and Ceramic Making.** Form, fire, decorate and finish clay or ceramic objects for functional or decorative use.
- **Glass Forming and Glasswork.** Form, blow, shape, fabricate, engrave or finish glass objects and components in craft/workshop contexts.
