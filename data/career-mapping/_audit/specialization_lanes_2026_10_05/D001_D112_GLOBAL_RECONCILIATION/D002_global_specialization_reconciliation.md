# D002 — Data Engineering and Architecture

Global specialization recommendation reconciliation (2026-10-06). This is an internal recommendation, not canonical taxonomy or external academic sign-off.

- **Candidate areas:** 4
- **Evidence URLs:** 3 unique; 3 source records screened as global/international
- **Canonical changes / role assignments:** none / none
- **Geographic scope:** global phase only; Nigerian-specific evidence intentionally deferred

## Recommendation

Replace the single excluded occupational bucket with four candidate areas: Data Architecture and Modeling; Data Engineering and Integration; Database and Data Platform Operations; and Data Quality and Metadata Management. These are an internal student-facing synthesis of recognized global data-management knowledge areas, not a DAMA/ACM-mandated taxonomy. Keep business analytics/visualization in D010, data science in D009, cloud platform operations in D005, and cybersecurity practice in D004 unless separately evidenced role tasks justify a contextual relationship.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Data Architecture and Modeling.** Design how organizational data is structured, related and represented so systems can store, exchange and use it consistently.
  - Source claim: DAMA distinguishes Data Architecture from Data Modeling and Design; ACM/AIS IS2020 includes relational database design, non-relational models and enterprise-architecture competencies. Combined here for a concise student-facing design specialization.
- **Data Engineering and Integration.** Build dependable ways to collect, transform and move data between systems, making it ready for consistent use by applications and teams.
  - Source claim: DAMA identifies Data Integration and Interoperability as a distinct knowledge area; ACM's 2023 Version Beta data-management proposal includes data-engineering/infrastructure work and database/data-lifecycle foundations.
- **Database and Data Platform Operations.** Implement and operate databases, warehouses and other data platforms, maintaining dependable storage, access, performance and service operation.
  - Source claim: DAMA identifies Data Storage and Operations and Data Warehousing and Business Intelligence; ACM/AIS IS2020 teaches database programming, security, concurrency and non-relational models. The D002 boundary covers platform construction and operation, not downstream BI analysis.
- **Data Quality and Metadata Management.** Assess and improve whether managed data is accurate, consistent and traceable, and maintain the descriptions and lineage that help others find and interpret it.
  - Source claim: DAMA treats Data Quality Management and Metadata Management as distinct knowledge areas; paired here provisionally as the trust and discoverability specialization. This pairing is a catalogue synthesis, not the framework's own grouping.

## Source evidence and limits

DAMA's overview says the framework is a consensus-based foundation, not an exhaustive catalog or prescriptive standard. **Supports:** A global data-management professional framework distinguishes architecture/modeling, storage/operations, integration, warehousing, metadata and quality as established knowledge areas relevant to D002's stated scope. **Limit:** DMBOK knowledge areas are not automatically student-facing specializations or a mandatory subgroup count; the proposed D002 set is an internal synthesis and must preserve boundaries with D004 Cybersecurity, D005 Cloud Computing, D009 Data Science and D010 Business Intelligence. Geographic scope: international/global professional framework. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [Association for Computing Machinery and Association for Information Systems — IS 2020: A Competency Model for Undergraduate Programs in Information Systems](https://is2020.hosting2.acm.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/IS2020-Competencies.pdf) — Data/Information competency realm, pp. 2-3: database querying and design, programming database systems, database security, concurrency trade-offs and non-relational models; IT infrastructure realm, p. 4, includes infrastructure planning and enterprise architecture concepts. **Supports:** An independent academic competency framework corroborates database design/management and architecture/infrastructure as teachable competency areas relevant to a data-systems domain. **Limit:** This undergraduate information-systems model does not prescribe a Data Engineering subgroup taxonomy, establish an exact count, or validate any occupation's placement. Geographic scope: international curriculum guidance. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.

Its proposed role examples do not validate repository role mappings. Geographic scope: international academic guidance. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.

## Open limits / reconciliation notes

- DAMA is a professional framework and ACM/AIS IS2020 is an information-systems curriculum model; neither establishes a universally mandated specialization count or these exact labels.
- Data warehousing overlaps implementation, storage operations and business intelligence. This recommendation limits D002 to designing/operating the data platform and leaves analysis, semantic reporting and visualization to D010.
- Data quality and metadata are distinct DAMA knowledge areas. They are grouped here only as a provisional learner-facing trust/discoverability specialization; split them only if broader curriculum evidence and the downstream content justify two clear branches.
- The current D002 role set contains broad ICT and analytics titles. The separate role audit may conclude that some records do not belong under D002; role volume must not determine this subgroup count.
- External academic sign-off has not been obtained. Canonical implementation remains pending independent specialization identifiers and generator/API support.

No roles were assigned to these areas. Current subgroup counts, ISCO grouping, role frequency and computational keyword hits were not used to determine candidate count. Nigeria-specific context is explicitly out of scope in this global phase.
