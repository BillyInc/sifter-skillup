## Source additions and claim limits

- **D070:** [UNESCO Institute for Statistics 2025 Framework for Cultural Statistics](https://www.uis.unesco.org/en/methods-and-tools/framework-cultural-statistics) distinguishes performing arts from audiovisual and separately identifies music/design/festivals. This resolves the category boundary only; the framework is statistical, not a learning taxonomy.
- **D071:** [Qatar University sport-management BSc](https://www.qu.edu.qa/en-us/Colleges/css/Pages/sport%20management.aspx) covers governance, marketing/sponsorship, event/facility management, finance and development; [University of Hertfordshire](https://www.herts.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate/sport-business-management) covers governance/finance, events, marketing and sport development; [George Washington University](https://business.gwu.edu/academics/programs/masters/ms-in-sport-management) covers commercial, event, analytics and operations dimensions. Their modules corroborate recurring subject areas across regions; they do not prescribe a universal taxonomy or prove the exact four groups.
- **D075:** [Textile Exchange/Apparel Alliance Version 2 Supply Chain Taxonomy](https://www.textileexchange.org/knowledge-center/reports/supply-chain-taxonomy-for-the-textile-apparel-and-fashion-industry/) maps value-chain tiers/processes from raw materials through finished products/distribution across textile, clothing, leather and footwear; [UNEP's global roadmap](https://www.unep.org/resources/publication/sustainability-and-circularity-textile-value-chain-global-roadmap) covers apparel/footwear plus home, technical, medical and automotive textiles; [GRI's sector-standard project](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/standards-development/sector-standard-project-for-textiles-and-apparel/) scopes production, manufacturing and retail. These are global value-chain/reporting sources, not academic subgroup standards.
