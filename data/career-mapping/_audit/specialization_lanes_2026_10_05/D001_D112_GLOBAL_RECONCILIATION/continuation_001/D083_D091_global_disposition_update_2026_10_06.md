## Source limits

- UNSD ISIC is an activity classification, not an academic curriculum or subgroup standard. It is used for D083 service-boundary checks only.
- UN Tourism's destination-management work establishes practice themes, not student-facing area counts or local authority.
- IFAC IES are global accountancy education standards aimed at professional accountancy organizations; they do not standardize payroll/bookkeeping tax rules or create a universal right to practise.
- WorldSkills is an international vocational benchmark; its Beauty Therapy standard is not a clinical license or a prescriptive taxonomy. CIDESCO is an international professional-education body, not a universal regulator. WHO's practitioner-regulation guidance supports careful scope governance but does not specify beauty-therapy law.
- IAABC practice/competency standards are international professional guidance for animal behavior consulting; they are not a universal license or a full animal-care taxonomy. EAZA is a European zoo/aquarium framework, not a global pet-services standard.
- UNECE road-instruction material is a recommendation implemented through competent authorities; IMO STCW is an international convention for seafarer standards. Neither resolves stored role-to-domain records.
- No cited source prescribes any count in this table. Counts are bounded internal recommendations for later local reconciliation.
