## Evidence additions and limitations

- **D063:** [American Economic Association JEL codes](https://topcat.aeaweb.org/econlit/jelCodes.php?view=jel) organizes economics scholarship into quantitative, macro/monetary, international, financial, public, labor/demographic, industrial, development, environment/agriculture and regional fields. It supports subject breadth only. It is explicitly a literature classification and does not prescribe learner taxonomy.
- **D064:** [EFPA EuroPsy](https://www.efpa.eu/about-europsy/) describes advanced specialist certificates (including psychotherapy, work/organisational, sport and clinical neuropsychology) that require further education and supervised practice; availability depends on national committees. This supports specialization as advanced practice but not globally uniform titles/counts.
- **D065:** [Newcastle programme specification 2026–27](https://teaching.ncl.ac.uk/docs/regsdocs2026/documents/-S4040_4181_2627%20vFinal.pdf) offers staged pathways in conference interpreting, translation/localisation, translation/interpreting, translation studies and interpreting/diplomacy. [Middlebury curriculum](https://www.middlebury.edu/institute/academics/joint-degrees/translation-interpretation-localization-management/curriculum) distinguishes translation, interpretation and conference interpretation degrees while teaching localization management and terminology as curriculum components. These are curricular examples, not global accreditation.
- **D066:** [WOAH Veterinary Education Core Curriculum](https://www.woah.org/app/uploads/2021/03/veterinary-education-core-curriculum.pdf) explicitly does not recommend one global veterinary curriculum; its competencies support broad coverage dimensions, not subgroup count. [WOAH workforce development](https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-offer/improving-veterinary-services/pvs-pathway/veterinary-workforce-development/) distinguishes animal health, veterinary public health and laboratory diagnosis for paraprofessional education, which is not directly a veterinarian specialty list.
