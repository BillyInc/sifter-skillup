## Newly identified report-metadata issue

The earlier D057, D058 and D060 reports cite international or multi-region bodies and curricula, but their headers say `0 source records screened as global/international`. Their body also marks sources `geographic_scope_not_established`, which means the source did not define the taxonomy's geographic scope—not that it was not reviewed. This is a source-screening metadata/reporting inconsistency. The original lane files are preserved; this supplemental report records the newly screened cross-region materials explicitly.

## Source notes and limits

### D057 — Digital Health and Health Technology (5 sources screened)

- [IMIA, BMHI education recommendations](https://imia-medinfo.org/wp/imia-recommendations-bmhi-education/), purpose/framework: international guidance for courses, course tracks and programs; its knowledge areas are curriculum content, not specialization labels.
- [Oxford MSc Applied Digital Health handbook](https://www.ox.ac.uk/sites/files/oxford/media_wysiwyg/MSc%20ADH%20Handbook%2022_23.pdf), course overview/modules: integrates clinical informatics, surveillance, data, remote monitoring, implementation and health-system topics. One programme cannot prescribe a global count.
- [University of Sydney, Master of Digital Health and Data Science](https://www.sydney.edu.au/handbooks/medicine-health-pg/coursework/digital-health-data-science/unit-of-study-table.html), degree structure: shared core, separate data-science/digital-health electives and capstone. Elective categories are not subgroup proof.
- [UCL, Health Informatics MSc](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/study/prospective-students/graduate/courses/health-informatics-msc?year=2025), modules: common informatics/data core with options in learning health systems, machine learning, digital transformation, personal informatics and evaluation. One institutional menu is not a universal taxonomy.
- [University of Leeds, MSc Health Informatics with Data Science](https://catalogue.leeds.ac.uk/Programme/202425?code=MSC-HI%2FDS-FT), programme catalogue: combines informatics, human factors, governance, AI/ML, analytics/visualization and project work. It does not cover every health-technology engineering practice.

The sources reinforce the interdisciplinary health-informatics foundation but do not resolve the compound root's inclusion of health-technology engineering/device development. That parent-scope question should be resolved before subgroup labels/counts.

### D058 — Education Technology and Learning Experience Design (5 sources screened)

- [NTU/NIE Singapore, MA Instructional Design and Technology](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/business/education/graduate-programme/master-of-arts-%28instructional-design-and-technology%29), programme overview/course descriptors: explicitly presents two professional contexts (corporate training/industry and formal institutions) over shared design/technology coursework. This does not prove employer context should define the taxonomy.
- [University of South Florida, MS Learning Design and Technology](https://www.usf.edu/education/for-students/graduate/master-of-science/learning-design-and-technology.aspx), programme overview: shared core with selectable e-learning design/development, learning analytics, game-based learning and AI/design emphases. Concentrations at one university do not imply four global subgroups.
- [AECT, Educational Technology definition](https://aect.org/aect/about/aect-definition): integrated scope spans design, management, implementation and evaluation; a field definition is not a taxonomy.
- [Simon Fraser University, Educational Technology and Learning Design](https://www.sfu.ca/education/programs/graduate-studies/masters/etld.html): integrates learning-technology design, planned use and evaluation; one Canadian programme is corroborative only.
- [University of Missouri, Learning Technologies and Design MS](https://catalog.missouri.edu/collegeofeducation/learningtechnologiesdesign/ms-learning-technologies-design/): separates design-focused and development-focused coursework. This supports testing two broad paths but not a universal count.

The two-route proposal is simpler than the former four buckets and preserves design-versus-technology-development emphasis. Whether these are sufficiently distinct and cumulative, rather than one integrated field with optional pathways, remains a local reconciliation question.

### D060 — Banking and Credit Services (5 sources screened)

- [GBEStB global standards](https://www.globalbankingstandards.org/globalstandards): separate Credit & Lending and Treasury Operations education standards, while expressly noting no definitive global answer for all banker knowledge/skills; treasury standard is a first draft.
- [Hong Kong Institute of Bankers training outline](https://www.hkib.org/page/74): names Credit Management, Treasury Management, Operations Management and Fintech Management streams. It is jurisdictional evidence, not a universal taxonomy.
- [State Bank of India training unit](https://sbi.bank.in/web/strategic-training-unit/sbicrm-gurgaon): offers separate credit, treasury and international-banking training. This is institutional corroboration, not a universal taxonomy.
- [University of Leeds Banking and Finance MSc](https://courses.leeds.ac.uk/K258/banking_and_finance_msc): shared commercial/international banking curriculum with optional banking/intermediation, digital-finance and risk topics. Academic modules are not automatically specializations.
- [BIS CPMI/IOSCO, financial market infrastructures](https://www.bis.org/committees/cpmi/pfmi/overview): defines payment systems and settlement infrastructures as technical practice areas. It does not establish that these belong inside D060 rather than an adjacent parent.

Three candidate functional areas are advanced, but ownership of payments, distinctness of deposit/account operations, and additive learning beyond the domain foundation remain open. Do not turn bank-wide risk, governance, compliance or digital tools into subgroup nodes just because standards treat them separately.
