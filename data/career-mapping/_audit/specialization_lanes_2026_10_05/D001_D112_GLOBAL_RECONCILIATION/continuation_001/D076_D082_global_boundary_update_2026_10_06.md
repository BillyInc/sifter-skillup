## D078 evidence added

- [Linköping University, Computational Social Science MSc](https://liu.se/en/education/program/f7mcd): dedicated program uses discrete-choice models, social network analysis, agent-based simulation and machine learning for social phenomena. Supports a distinct interdisciplinary route; one Swedish program does not mandate a global taxonomy.
- [UC3M, Master in Computational Social Science](https://www.uc3m.es/master/computational-social-science): dedicated program in predictive modeling, text analysis and network science applied to social science. Confirms the route is not only a digital-sociology topic; one Spanish curriculum is not an accreditation standard.
- [UC San Diego, Computational Social Science curriculum](https://css.ucsd.edu/masters/curriculum.html): combines mathematics, programming and statistics with social-science theory and applied project work. Independent US curricular exemplar; not a universal subgroup prescription.

Existing D078 global sources remain relevant: UNESCO ISCED describes social-science fields but does not prescribe taxonomy; ISA WG10 Digital Sociology is a research community, not a count standard. Computational/digital learning should remain cross-cutting and optional outside its focused path.
