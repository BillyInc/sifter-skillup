# D062 — Trading

Global specialization recommendation reconciliation (2026-10-06). This is an internal recommendation, not canonical taxonomy or external academic sign-off.

- **Candidate areas:** 2
- **Evidence URLs:** 5 unique; 0 source records screened as global/international
- **Canonical changes / role assignments:** none / none
- **Geographic scope:** global phase only; Nigerian-specific evidence intentionally deferred

## Recommendation

Provisionally replace the two occupational buckets with two function-based candidate areas: Trading, Dealing and Market Making; and Brokerage and Market Intermediation. Treat asset classes (equities, fixed income, derivatives, FX, money markets, commodities/energy) and trading styles (agency, proprietary, flow) as themes or contexts under those areas, not separate subgroup cards by default. Final taxonomy depends on resolving the parent-domain boundary.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Trading, Dealing and Market Making.** Analyze market conditions, manage orders and positions, provide liquidity or execute trades, and assess execution quality across financial markets.
- **Brokerage and Market Intermediation.** Connect market participants to trading venues and counterparties, handle client or agency orders, support execution and market access, and coordinate the relevant transaction lifecycle.

## Open limits / reconciliation notes

- CFA Institute professional curriculum/career descriptions inform investment practice but are not an academic accreditation taxonomy; trade execution often sits in portfolio-management curricula.
- ACI is an international financial-markets professional association; its dealing syllabus supports market contexts but does not establish a universal license or a separate D062 subgroup.
- FIA educational material concerns derivatives/futures market structure; it is one market segment and does not define all trading.
- No role-to-domain or role-to-specialization status is granted here; no domain counts or role-frequency patterns are used as fit evidence.
- The final recommendation remains provisional until the parent domain is reconciled against D011 and D013.
