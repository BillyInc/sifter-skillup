## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Textile and Fibre Crafts.** Make textile goods by hand or simple equipment, including weaving, knitting, embroidery and related fibre techniques.
- **Basketry and Plant-Fibre Crafts.** Make baskets, wickerwork and related objects from plant fibres and similar flexible materials.
