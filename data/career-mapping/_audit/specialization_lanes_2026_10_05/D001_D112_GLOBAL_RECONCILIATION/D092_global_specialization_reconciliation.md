# D092 — Culinary Arts, Catering and Food Service

Global specialization recommendation reconciliation (2026-10-06). This is an internal recommendation, not canonical taxonomy or external academic sign-off.

- **Candidate areas:** 4
- **Evidence URLs:** 7 unique; 4 source records screened as global/international
- **Canonical changes / role assignments:** none / none
- **Geographic scope:** global phase only; Nigerian-specific evidence intentionally deferred

## Recommendation

Replace the three ISCO-derived buckets with four candidate areas: Culinary Production and Kitchen Operations; Baking, Pastry and Confectionery; Food and Beverage Service; and Catering and Foodservice Operations Management. This is a compact internal synthesis of international occupational standards and comparative curricula, not a count prescribed by WorldSkills or an academic accreditor. Clarify the parent description to include management of commercial and institutional foodservice systems; do not infer that every catering role belongs in the management area.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Culinary Production and Kitchen Operations.** Prepare and cook meals and coordinate safe, efficient kitchen production and workflow.
  - Source claim: WorldSkills Cooking describes professional preparation, cooking and work organization across restaurant, hotel, welfare, healthcare, transport, canteen and quick-service contexts.
- **Baking, Pastry and Confectionery.** Produce bakery goods, breads, pastries, desserts, cakes and confectionery using specialist methods and equipment.
  - Source claim: WorldSkills maintains separate Bakery and Pâtisserie/Confectionery standards; this recommendation combines the related craft family at top level to avoid excessive fragmentation.
- **Food and Beverage Service.** Serve food and drinks to guests, including dining-room, bar, beverage and banquet service.
  - Source claim: WorldSkills Restaurant Service defines a guest-facing competency profile covering service styles, beverages, bar work and banqueting, separate from kitchen production.
- **Catering and Foodservice Operations Management.** Plan and manage commercial or institutional foodservice systems, including menus, procurement, staffing, production/delivery, cost, quality and safety.
  - Source claim: UPM covers institutional menus, delivery systems, logistics, cost and quality; Porto covers meal-production systems and collective catering; UAB covers department structures, job profiles, cost control and catering operations.

## Source evidence and limits

Geographic scope: international reference. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [WorldSkills International — WorldSkills Occupational Standards: Bakery (2024)](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1667/) — Occupation description and bakery competency sections covering bread and bakery production, processes, equipment, quality and work organization. **Supports:** Bakery production is represented as a distinct technical competency area rather than merely a generic cook task. **Limit:** The standard supports a bakery/pastry family but does not require bakery and pastry to be separate top-level specializations; this review deliberately combines them. Geographic scope: international reference. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [WorldSkills International — WorldSkills Occupational Standards: Pâtisserie and Confectionery (2024)](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1711/) — Occupation description and WSOS sections for pastry/confectionery production, hot/cold desserts, cakes, finishing, presentation, hygiene and commercial portion/cost control. **Supports:** Pastry/confectionery is a specialist production practice that is distinguishable from general savory cooking and overlaps sufficiently with bakery to be grouped in one learner-facing area. **Limit:** The standard is an international occupational reference, not a universal academic subgroup list or role-mapping decision. Geographic scope: international reference. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [WorldSkills International — WorldSkills Occupational Standards: Restaurant Service (2024)](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1699/) — Occupation description and WSOS competencies for front-of-house food/drink service, guest interaction, beverage/wine knowledge, bar service, banqueting and service styles. **Supports:** Guest-facing food-and-beverage service has a distinct competency profile from cooking and kitchen production. **Limit:** An international occupational standard, not an academic accreditation taxonomy and not evidence for any individual role-to-domain mapping. Geographic scope: international reference. **Scope screen:** global_or_international_source.
- [Universiti Putra Malaysia, Faculty of Food Science and Technology — Bachelor of Science in Food Service Management with Honours: Undergraduate Course Synopsis](https://food.upm.edu.my/academic/undergraduate_course_synopsis/bachelor_of_science_in_food_service_management_with_honours-1823) — Course FSM4706, Institutional Food Service Management: menu management, food delivery systems, logistics/information systems, operational cost, quantity and quality controls; adjacent Beverage Management course FSM4707. **Supports:** Institutional foodservice management includes systems-level planning and control that is distinct from hands-on cooking and front-of-house service competencies. **Limit:** A Malaysian university curriculum, used as comparative academic evidence only; its course structure is not a global mandate or Nigerian requirement. Geographic scope: institution-specific, Malaysia. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.

Geographic scope: institution-specific, Portugal. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.
- [Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona — Catering Management I](https://guies.uab.cat/guies_docents/public/portal/html/2025/assignatura/103731/en) — Course objectives: food-and-beverage department structure and job profiles, cost control, catering-company management models, and hotel food-service operations. **Supports:** Catering management is a distinct systems/management curriculum area and should not be collapsed into the front-of-house service area merely because both concern food service. **Limit:** A Spanish university course guide; supports comparative curriculum distinction, not a global subgroup count or any role's fit to D092. Geographic scope: institution-specific, Spain. **Scope screen:** country_or_institution_specific_corroboration.
