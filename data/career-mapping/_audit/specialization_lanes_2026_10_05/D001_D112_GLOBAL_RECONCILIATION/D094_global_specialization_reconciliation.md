# D094 — Electronics and ICT Equipment Servicing, Smart Systems Installation

Global specialization recommendation reconciliation (2026-10-06). This is an internal recommendation, not canonical taxonomy or external academic sign-off.

- **Candidate areas:** 4
- **Evidence URLs:** 5 unique; 1 source records screened as global/international
- **Canonical changes / role assignments:** none / none
- **Geographic scope:** global phase only; Nigerian-specific evidence intentionally deferred

## Recommendation

Replace the two excluded ISCO buckets with four candidate areas: Electronic Hardware Design, Assembly and Embedded Systems; Electronics Installation, Testing and Maintenance; ICT and Communications Equipment Servicing; and Smart-Building and Connected-Systems Integration. This is a compact synthesis of WorldSkills occupational standards, ESCO task descriptions and the KNX/CEDIA smart-systems practice context. It is not an externally mandated taxonomy or approval of any role mapping. Clarify the root description so it describes the scope rather than implying that only repair-workshop servicing belongs.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Electronic Hardware Design, Assembly and Embedded Systems.** Design, prototype, assemble and test electronic circuits and embedded devices, including PCB work, microcontrollers and sensor interfaces.
  - Source claim: WorldSkills International's Electronics occupational standard identifies prototype circuit design, PCB layout, assembly, embedded-system programming and MCU interfacing as distinct specialist electronics work.
- **Electronics Installation, Testing and Maintenance.** Install and commission electronic equipment, test it against specifications, diagnose faults, and maintain or repair equipment and systems.
  - Source claim: The WorldSkills Electronics standard names installation/commissioning, service and maintenance, measurement/testing and repair as electronics specialist work areas.
- **ICT and Communications Equipment Servicing.** Install, troubleshoot, repair and maintain computer, data-communications and telecommunications equipment, cabling and related hardware.
  - Source claim: ESCO's ICT installers and servicers classification describes installation, repair and maintenance of telecommunications/data equipment, cables, antennae and computers; ESCO's telecommunications technician profile lists installation, measurement, troubleshooting and repair tasks.
- **Smart-Building and Connected-Systems Integration.** Install, configure and commission connected building or home systems that coordinate functions such as lighting, heating controls, shading and security.
  - Source claim: KNX Association describes installer work integrating lighting, heating, shading and security across building/home projects; CEDIA's Integrated Systems Technician credential describes a cross-system smart-home installation and maintenance technician role.

## Open limits / reconciliation notes

- The four-area structure is a cross-source synthesis, not a globally ratified taxonomy or academic accreditation decision.
- WorldSkills is an international occupational-skills standard; ESCO is a European classification whose occupation descriptions are corroborative rather than globally binding.
- KNX describes its own open smart-building ecosystem, and CEDIA is an industry credential body; neither makes a vendor/platform credential universally required.
- Some current role records may belong in adjacent domains when actual tasks are reviewed; this specialization recommendation does not validate them.
- Canonical implementation is pending completion and reconciliation of all domain specialization reviews and a stable specialization identifier/schema.
