## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Leather Processing and Finishing.** Prepare hides/skins and process, tan and finish leather material.
- **Footwear Design, Construction and Repair.** Develop patterns and construct, fit, repair or finish footwear, including specialist footwear where evidence supports it.
- **Leather Goods, Saddlery and Accessories.** Cut, form, stitch, assemble and finish bags, saddlery and other leather accessories.
