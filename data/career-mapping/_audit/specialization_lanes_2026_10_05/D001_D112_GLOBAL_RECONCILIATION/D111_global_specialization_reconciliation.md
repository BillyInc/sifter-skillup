## Recommendation

Three textile process areas are supported by machinery standards evidence. Keep garment-machine production as a conditional fourth candidate because the ISO machinery committee's sewing-machine liaison and sector OSH code do not alone demonstrate an independent learner route within this parent; seek an occupational curriculum before canonicalizing it. Source-gap disposition recorded as `three_textile_process_families_supported_garment_machine_branch_needs_parent_boundary_review`.

## Candidate areas and boundaries

- **Fibre Preparation and Spinning.** Prepare fibres and operate machinery that twists/spins fibres into yarn.
- **Fabric Formation.** Operate weaving, knitting and related machinery that forms fabrics from yarn or fibre.
- **Textile Colouration, Printing and Finishing.** Operate machinery and processes for textile dyeing, printing, finishing and surface treatment.
- **Garment-Machine Production (parent-boundary candidate).** Operate sewing/assembly machinery for garment production, distinct from garment design and hand tailoring.
