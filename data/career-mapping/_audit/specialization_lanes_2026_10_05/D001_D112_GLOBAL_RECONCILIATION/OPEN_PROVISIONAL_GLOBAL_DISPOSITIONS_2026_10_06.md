## Why counts remain internal rather than “official”

The sources answer different questions: global competency frameworks, international professional practice, scholarly classifications, standards, and individual curricula. None defines a universally binding subgroup count for this product's 168-domain taxonomy. The defensible result is therefore an explicit internal synthesis—or a flat domain when evidence cannot distinguish additive learner paths—not a false claim that an international body prescribed the count.

## Primary and authoritative evidence added/confirmed in this pass

- [WHO, *Competency frameworks and standards for digital health: a landscape analysis* (2026)](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240119765), overview: maps competency clusters across workforce frameworks and emphasizes context/adaptation. **Limit:** workforce competencies, not a digital-health product-builder specialization taxonomy.
- [IMIA recommendations on BMHI education](https://imia-medinfo.org/wp/imia-recommendations-bmhi-education/): purpose is international guidance for courses, tracks and dedicated programmes. **Limit:** guides education design; does not mandate the D057 subgroup count.
- [Yale MS in Public Health, Health Informatics](https://ysph.yale.edu/school-of-public-health/graduate-programs/master-of-science-in-public-health-msph/health-informatics/): curriculum combines population/public-health informatics, computational methods, clinical databases and decision support. **Limit:** one US programme; comparative evidence only.
- [University of Michigan Master of Health Informatics curriculum](https://www.si.umich.edu/programs/degree-programs/master-health-informatics/master-health-informatics-curriculum): one programme exposes informal interests such as big data/AI and management/leadership. **Limit:** institution-specific options, not a global taxonomy.
- [Global Banking Education Standards Board, global standards](https://www.globalbankingstandards.org/globalstandards): states that definitive internationally agreed answers on banker knowledge/skills do not yet exist; lists credit/lending and a treasury-operations first draft. **Limit:** standards support topics and education, not exact subgroup count.
- [BIS/CPMI-IOSCO, Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures](https://www.bis.org/publications/fsi-summary-principles-financial-market-infrastructures-executive-summary): defines payment systems, securities depositories/settlement, central counterparties and trade repositories as FMIs. **Limit:** infrastructure oversight scope, not a banking-worker specialization taxonomy.
- [CFA Institute, Sales and Trading Professionals](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/careers/sales-and-trading): differentiates sales, trading, structuring, research, agency/principal/flow trading and asset-class contexts. **Limit:** professional career guidance, not an academic/accreditation count.
- [American Economic Association, Graduate Degrees in Economics](https://www.aeaweb.org/resources/students/grad-prep/overview): describes graduate core study followed by field specialization classified through JEL. **Limit:** JEL classifies scholarship; fields and offerings differ by programme.
- [EFPA, EuroPsy 2025 regulations and specialist practice](https://www.efpa.eu/apply-to-europsy-certificate/): distinguishes common practice fields from advanced specialist certificates and notes national committee/legal context. **Limit:** European framework; not global title law or complete worldwide inventory.
- [International Federation of Translators](https://en.fit-ift.org/): represents translator, interpreter and terminologist associations working across literary, technical, public-service, court, conference, media and academic settings. **Limit:** global professional federation description, not an education taxonomy/count.
- [WOAH, Veterinary Education Core Curriculum](https://www.woah.org/en/document/veterinary_education_core_curriculum/): model core curriculum and Day-1 competencies. **Limit:** the source explicitly does not recommend a single global curriculum or prescribe specialist subgroup counts.
