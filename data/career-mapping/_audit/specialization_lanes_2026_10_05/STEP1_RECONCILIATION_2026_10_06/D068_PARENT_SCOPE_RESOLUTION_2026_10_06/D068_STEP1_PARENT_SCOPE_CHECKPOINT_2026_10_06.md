# D068 Step 1 Parent-Scope Resolution — 2026-10-06

## Outcome

The D068 parent-scope hold is resolved at the recommendation level. The recorded parent is **Allied Health and Rehabilitation Therapies**; its own description covers human-health rehabilitation, biomedical laboratory diagnostics, and medical imaging. These are distinct areas of study/practice, but the broad allied-health umbrella can present them as three separate routes after a limited shared health-sciences foundation. The foundation must not be represented as professional qualification across all three branches.

Recommended student-facing areas:

1. **Rehabilitation and Functional Health Services** — interventions focused on functioning, participation, and independence; a multidisciplinary field.
2. **Biomedical Laboratory Diagnostics** — patient-specimen testing, analytical methods, quality assurance, validation, and interpretation supporting diagnosis and monitoring.
3. **Medical Imaging and Radiography** — diagnostic-image practice, modality-specific work, image quality, and radiation protection; radiotherapy is not included solely because it uses medical radiation.

The three-area count is an internal taxonomy decision, not a count prescribed by WHO, IFBLS, or IAEA. It is the smallest set separating the three different practice/learning bases expressly named in D068’s recorded description. Further splitting hearing/communication, prosthetics/orthotics, or assistive technology into top-level D068 cards is not justified by this evidence pass; WHO treats these as contributors within multidisciplinary rehabilitation. These can remain topics within the rehabilitation branch pending later structured enrichment.

## Evidence and limitations

- WHO describes rehabilitation as a distinct function-focused, multidisciplinary field, lists its contributing professions, and provides an adaptable competency framework for professions and specializations. This supports one coherent rehabilitation branch, not the inclusion of laboratory and imaging in rehabilitation itself. [WHO Rehabilitation Workforce](https://www.who.int/teams/noncommunicable-diseases/sensory-functions-disability-and-rehabilitation/rehabilitation/workforce), [WHO Rehabilitation Competency Framework](https://www.who.int/teams/noncommunicable-diseases/sensory-functions-disability-and-rehabilitation/rehabilitation-competency-framework).
- IFBLS describes biomedical laboratory science as a distinct body of education and competence centered on specimen analysis, methods, quality assurance, validation, and clinical interpretation. It does not prescribe D068’s count or universal national scope. [IFBLS Core Competence](https://www.ifbls.org/index.php/statements/core-competence-core-curriculum).
- IAEA SSG-46, paragraphs 2.91–2.93 and 2.126–2.127, distinguishes diagnostic radiology from radiation therapy and nuclear medicine and notes specialty structures vary by State. This supports an imaging branch while requiring jurisdiction-specific credentials to remain separate. [IAEA SSG-46](https://gnssn.iaea.org/CSN/Trainings%20and%20Workshops/Inspectors%20Training%202019%20Tirana/Useful%20references/IAEA%20SSG-46%20RP%20in%20Medicine.pdf).
- WHO’s health-worker classification says its groupings are occupational codes for classification and comparability. The stored “Health Professionals” and “Health Associate Professionals” labels are therefore not accepted as specialization areas. [WHO Classifying Health Workers](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/classifying-health-workers).

The tracked D068 metadata does not mention **animal rehabilitation**; it describes human allied health. Animal rehabilitation is excluded from this D068 recommendation rather than silently added.
