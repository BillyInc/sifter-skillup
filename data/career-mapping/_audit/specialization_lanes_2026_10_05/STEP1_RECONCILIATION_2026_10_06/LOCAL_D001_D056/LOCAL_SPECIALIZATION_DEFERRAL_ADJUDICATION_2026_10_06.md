# Local specialization deferral adjudication — D001–D056

Date: 2026-10-06  
Purpose: finish global area-of-specialization decisions that were previously held only because role-to-domain or role-to-specialization work was unfinished.

## Decision rule

An unfinished role audit is not a reason to postpone deciding what a domain's areas of specialization are. This pass determines only whether the proposed areas describe coherent learning/practice branches within the current domain scope. It does **not** decide which individual roles belong to a domain or area, change canonical data, add Nigerian context, or claim external academic approval. Where a root-domain boundary genuinely affects an area, the current root definition is used as the working boundary and the boundary limitation is recorded for the later catalogue-wide review.

## Candidate-set conflict: D054 Oil and Gas Operations

Use the seven technical practice areas in `D053_D056_global_specialization_review_2026_10_06.json` as the reconciled internal recommendation: Subsurface Exploration and Reservoir Development; Drilling and Well Construction; Well Completions and Intervention; Production and Field Operations; Petroleum Facilities, Projects and Construction; Midstream Gas Processing, Transport and Storage; Refining and Petroleum Processing.

The competing three-part upstream/midstream/downstream framing is retained as a **value-chain crosswalk**, not treated as a conflicting specialization taxonomy. It expresses a coarser organizational/industry segmentation; SPE's technical communities evidence finer distinct technical learning/practice boundaries. The checked-in D054 parent description spans the value chain, so the seven-area set fits the current scope. HSE, asset integrity, sustainability and digital methods remain cross-cutting unless later evidence establishes separate learning routes. This reconciles the candidate disagreement without using D054's role inventory.

## Evidence and limits for D021–D024

- D021: the existing UIA Charter sources address architectural education and distinguish architecture from urban design/planning and related built-environment themes; SME/SME-adjacent evidence previously captured for D022 is not used to justify D021. The proposed three-way split is an internal synthesis of building design, spatial planning and landscape/public-realm design, not a prescribed global count. Exclusions above prevent surveying and inspection from being silently treated as specializations.
- D022: the local source pass captured the Society for Mining, Metallurgy & Exploration's divisions and reference scope. Its material distinguishes exploration/mining, mineral/metallurgical processing, and environment/safety practice, but its association divisions do not prescribe a student taxonomy. The four-area synthesis separates resource discovery, mine development/operation, and processing while keeping petroleum in D054.
- D023: ABMS's current specialty/subspecialty directory and UEMS's European specialty structure show recognizable physician specialty branches organized by breadth, organ system, patient population and treatment type. WHO's international health-worker classification distinguishes specialist doctors from other health-worker groups. These sources support family-level grouping, but ABMS/UEMS are not global regulators and WHO's classification is not a student taxonomy.
- D024: ICN's 2025 global nursing definition supplies the profession-wide boundary. WHO's regional specialist-practice guide gives examples including critical care, women's, community, mental and school health and explicitly reports no agreement on a universal nursing-specialty definition; the NMC's four fields are a UK-specific corroborating example only. The six areas are therefore an internal synthesis; no global count or credential equivalence is claimed.
