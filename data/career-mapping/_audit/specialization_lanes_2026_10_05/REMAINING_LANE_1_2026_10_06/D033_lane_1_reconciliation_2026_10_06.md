## Candidate areas

- **Product Strategy and Portfolio Direction** — Product portfolio means product/line investment decisions; organizational project/programme portfolio selection and governance remains distinct in D032.
- **Product Discovery and Market Insights** — Research methods overlap with marketing research and user research; place ownership by the decision being informed and actual role tasks, not tool choice alone.
- **Product Planning and Lifecycle Management** — A roadmap is a planning artifact, not a guarantee of delivery authority; project management coordinates temporary delivery work, while product managers retain product-outcome decisions where that is the actual role.
- **Product Innovation and Development** — The product manager coordinates decisions and outcomes but is not thereby the engineer/designer doing implementation; methods such as Stage-Gate, Agile and Lean are optional/contextual approaches.
