## Residuals and limits

- This is an internal evidence-based recommendation, not academic accreditation or external sign-off.
- The FSB function taxonomy is from 2017 and is used for high-level distinctions, not as a current exhaustive technology inventory.
- World Bank and BIS sources cover policy, infrastructure and financial inclusion as well as technology; none is a full FinTech engineering curriculum.
- Local product regulation, licensing and open-finance rules must be clearly jurisdiction-labelled; the taxonomy itself is global.
- Blockchain is an enabling technology with an adjacent domain (D056); its use in a payment or market product does not automatically make the pathway a D056 specialization.
- No roles, ISCO memberships, role-to-domain fit or role-to-specialization links were evaluated.
- FinTech Engineering is the engineering of technology-enabled financial products and infrastructures, not every finance occupation and not every technology used by finance. Specializations should be anchored in the financial service/system being engineered.
- The candidate set remains noncanonical and requires local reconciliation.
