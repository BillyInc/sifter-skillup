## Candidate areas

- **Business, Corporate and Financial Law** — Separate buyer-side procurement from legal contract advice; separate tax, securities, insolvency and competition law when the catalogue has enough distinct role/task evidence.
- **Dispute Resolution** — Distinguish legal advocacy/advice from neutral dispute-resolution services and court administration; do not imply all routes authorize practice in every jurisdiction.
- **Criminal Justice** — Criminal justice services, law-enforcement investigation and legal representation are different accountabilities; classify roles by their actual primary tasks.
- **Public, Constitutional and Human Rights Law** — International principles can be taught globally, but constitutional rules, administrative remedies and enforceable rights vary by jurisdiction.
- **Employment, Family and Immigration Law** — These are distinct practice subjects grouped here only as a provisional people-and-status learning family; split them if role-path evidence supports separate routes. Local statutes and permissions must be identified.
- **Intellectual Property, Technology and Data Law** — Separate technical cybersecurity/data operations from legal advice about rights, liability and compliance; privacy/data rules are jurisdiction-specific.
- **Property, Construction and Infrastructure Law** — Property transactions, construction-project claims and engineering/project delivery are different work; jurisdictional property, planning and construction laws must be localized.
