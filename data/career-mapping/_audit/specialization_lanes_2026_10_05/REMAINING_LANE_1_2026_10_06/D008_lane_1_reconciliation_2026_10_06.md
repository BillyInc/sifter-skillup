## Candidate areas

- **Telecommunications Access Networks** — Engineer the network segment that connects users, devices or local networks to service-provider networks, including radio-access systems and fixed-access technologies; work with access capacity, coverage, interfaces and service entry points.
- **Core and IP Network Engineering** — Design and engineer provider/core and IP networks, including routing, forwarding, addressing, interconnection, traffic engineering and service/core functions that connect access networks to services.
- **Transport and Optical Networks** — Engineer the high-capacity transmission systems that carry traffic between access, core, data-centre and service-network locations, including optical and carrier-grade packet transport, topology, capacity and resilience.
- **Network Operations and Service Assurance** — Operate and manage live communications networks: monitor service and network performance, handle faults and incidents, manage configuration and changes, assess quality/reliability, and maintain operational/security controls.
