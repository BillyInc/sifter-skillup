# D072 — Geospatial Science and Geographic Information Systems

**Status:** Provisional five-area synthesis; parent scope should be clarified. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

FIG describes an international surveying/geomatics field spanning geodesy, mapping, hydrography, cadastre and spatial-information management; its technical commissions distinguish spatial-information management and hydrographic surveying. IHO publishes separate hydrographic survey standards, while OGC standards focus on interoperable access, processing and exchange of geospatial information. Together these support several coherent practice areas, but none prescribes five student-facing groups.

Recommend provisionally: Geodesy and Positioning; Land Surveying and Cadastre; Hydrography and Marine Geospatial Information; Remote Sensing and Photogrammetry; GIS, Spatial Data and Geospatial Analysis. This is an internal synthesis, not a global academic taxonomy or professional sign-off.

**Parent boundary:** the existing description includes geology, hydrology and oceanography. These are earth-science subjects that use geospatial methods, not automatically specializations within a geospatial domain. Narrow the parent to spatial measurement, mapping and spatial-information practice, or split the geoscience disciplines into their own domains.
