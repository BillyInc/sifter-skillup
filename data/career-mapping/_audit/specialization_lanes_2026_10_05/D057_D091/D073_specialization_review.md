# D073 — Libraries, Archives and Information Management

**Status:** Provisional four-area synthesis; parent boundary remains open. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

IFLA maintains international library standards and service guidance; the International Council on Archives describes archival access and preservation responsibilities; ISO 15489 defines records-management principles across formats and technological environments. These support distinct but related information practices; they do not prescribe a four-group learner taxonomy.

Recommend provisionally: Library and Information Services; Archives and Records Management; Knowledge and Organizational Information Management; Digital Information, Data Curation and Preservation.

**Parent boundary:** enterprise data architecture/software engineering and museum object conservation/curatorship should not be included solely because they handle information or collections. Privacy, records-retention and archival law vary by jurisdiction. These candidate areas are an internal synthesis, not external academic sign-off.
