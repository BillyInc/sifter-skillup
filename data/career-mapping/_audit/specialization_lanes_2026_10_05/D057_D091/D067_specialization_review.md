# D067 — Dentistry: specialization review

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** Source-reviewed, eight-area internal synthesis; specialty recognition remains jurisdiction-specific.  
**Scope:** Areas of specialization only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

The former three visible groups are ISCO occupation families, not dentistry specializations. Recommend eight broad learner-facing areas: Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery; Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics; Pediatric and Special Care Dentistry; Endodontics; Periodontology; Prosthodontics and Implant Dentistry; Oral Medicine, Diagnosis and Orofacial Pain; and Dental Public Health.

This is not the ADA's U.S.-specific twelve-specialty list copied globally. FDI's 2025 policy supports distinct advanced specialties while recognizing cross-country variation. ADEE supports a shared undergraduate foundation; these paths should be presented as later specialization, not as undergraduate licensure or job eligibility. Oral pathology, imaging, pain, implant dentistry and special-care practice may be separately recognized in some jurisdictions; preserve those local labels and requirements where applicable.

Source locators, alternatives and limits are in [D067_specialization_review.json](D067_specialization_review.json). No role fit is inferred from current occupational groups.
