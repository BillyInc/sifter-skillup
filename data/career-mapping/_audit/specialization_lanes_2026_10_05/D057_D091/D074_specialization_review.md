# D074 — Occupational Health and Safety

**Status:** Provisional four-area synthesis; jurisdictional scope remains explicit. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

ILO occupational-safety instruments distinguish national OSH governance from workplace prevention and management. ISO 45001 describes an organizational OH&S management system. The sources support multiple kinds of practice but do not mandate this exact count.

Recommend provisionally: Workplace Safety and Risk Control; Occupational Hygiene and Exposure Prevention; Occupational Health and Worker Wellbeing; OH&S Management Systems, Assurance and Incident Learning.

**Boundaries:** statutory inspection/enforcement powers are country-specific. Clinical occupational-health practice requires the relevant professional qualification and local authorization. ISO 45001 is a management-system standard; it is not an individual certification. Sector-specific hazards are contextual routes unless independently justified.
