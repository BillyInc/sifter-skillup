# D068 — Allied Health and Rehabilitation Therapies: specialization review

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** Source-reviewed; parent-scope split recommended. **Recommended visible subgroup count under the current parent: zero, pending scope repair.**  
**Scope:** Specialization structure only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

The two stored records are ISCO categories, not specializations. The domain description combines rehabilitation/function-focused services with biomedical laboratory diagnosis and medical imaging. WHO describes rehabilitation itself as multidisciplinary; IFBLS separately defines a distinctive laboratory-science competency base. Flattening physiotherapy, audiology, lab science and radiography beneath one foundation would suggest a common learning path that the evidence does not establish.

Therefore, do not publish subgroup cards under the current D068 parent. First review whether the catalogue should separate coherent roots such as rehabilitation/function, biomedical diagnostics and medical imaging. Those are candidate parent boundaries, not approved subgroup assignments. The zero is not a claim that these professions have no areas of specialization.

Source locators, competing options and limitations are in [D068_specialization_review.json](D068_specialization_review.json). WHO's scope is international but country compositions vary; ASHA policy is explicitly U.S.-specific. No role is assigned to a proposed branch.
