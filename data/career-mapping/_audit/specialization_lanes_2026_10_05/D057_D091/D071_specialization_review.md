# D071 — Sports Management: specialization review

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** Provisional four-area synthesis; parent-domain boundaries remain open.  
**Scope:** Area-of-specialization review only. No role assignment or canonical taxonomy edit.

## Recommendation

The current domain description mixes management with coaching, officiating, scouting and athlete-performance work. Based on the domain title and cross-disciplinary sport-management evidence, provisionally use four areas:

1. Sport Organization Governance and Administration
2. Sport Marketing and Commercial Partnerships
3. Sport Events, Facilities and Venue Operations
4. Sport Development and Participation

Treat law, finance, analytics, communications and ethics as connected themes unless verified learning evidence shows they need independent specialization cards. Coaching, officiating, athlete performance science and clinical sports care require separate role-to-domain decisions; mentioning them in the current description does not make them management specializations.

NASSM sources identify recurring field knowledge and research areas, but are North American and do not prescribe a global taxonomy. UNESCO supplies international policy framing on governance, safety and development, not an exact curriculum. Four is therefore an internal synthesis, not an official global count.
