# D079 — Customs and International Trade

**Status:** Provisional three-area synthesis; public customs/private trade boundary remains open. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

WCO PICARD standards provide a competency framework for customs-related academic programmes and job profiles. WCO's Harmonized System supports a distinct technical classification route; WTO's Trade Facilitation Agreement addresses movement, release and clearance of goods and cooperation among border authorities. These frameworks support the candidate areas but not a fixed subgroup count.

Propose: Customs Administration and Border Compliance; Tariff Classification, Customs Valuation and Origin; Trade Facilitation and International Trade Operations. Separate immigration/migration control for its own scope review, and route general freight, warehousing and distribution to logistics unless trade-specific learning additions justify inclusion. Customs authority, tariffs and procedures are jurisdiction-specific. Details and source limits: [D079_specialization_review.json](D079_specialization_review.json).
