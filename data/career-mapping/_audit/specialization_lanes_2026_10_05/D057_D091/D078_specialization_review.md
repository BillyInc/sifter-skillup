# D078 — Social Sciences

**Status:** Provisional seven-area synthesis; computational social science is an evidenced cross-cutting specialization candidate. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

UNESCO's international education classifications identify distinct social/behavioural disciplines including sociology, anthropology, economics, political science, psychology, demography and human/social geography. The International Sociological Association has an international Digital Sociology working group focused on research at the intersection of digital technologies and society. This supports computational/digital social inquiry as a real route, but not a programming requirement for every social-science learner or role.

Propose: Sociology and Social Anthropology; Economics and Applied Economic Analysis; Politics, Governance and Public Policy; Social Behaviour and Interaction; Human Geography and Population Studies; Social Research and Evaluation; Computational and Digital Social Science. Social Behaviour and Interaction is the bounded social-science area formerly labelled Psychology and Social Behaviour; clinical psychology, counselling, psychotherapy and related practice remain in the separate D064 Psychology and Counselling domain. Keep method routes distinct from discipline foundations, and review neighboring fields such as law, journalism and practice-led social work separately. The seven-area count is an internal synthesis, not prescribed by UNESCO or ISA. Details: [D078_specialization_review.json](D078_specialization_review.json).
