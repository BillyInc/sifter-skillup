# D066 — Veterinary Science: specialization taxonomy review

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** Provisional; count and neighboring-domain ownership require reconciliation.  
**Scope:** Domain-level taxonomy only. No roles or canonical data changed.

## Finding

The current records—Animal & Feed Scientists; Veterinarians & Animal Therapists; Veterinary Nurses & Technicians—mix a science family with occupation and qualification levels. They are not suitable learner-facing areas of specialization.

Eight candidate areas:

1. Companion Animal Medicine and Surgery
2. Equine Medicine and Surgery
3. Livestock, Poultry and Aquatic Animal Health and Production
4. Veterinary Public Health, Food Safety and Epidemiology
5. Veterinary Clinical Disciplines and Diagnostics
6. Wildlife, Zoo and Conservation Medicine
7. Animal Welfare, Behaviour and Clinical Nutrition
8. Laboratory Animal Science and Veterinary Research

WOAH’s international veterinary-education and public-health materials support veterinary work across animal health/welfare, animal production, zoonoses, food safety, public veterinary services and research. RCVS groups recognized specialties into production animal medicine, companion/competitive animals and cross-species disciplines, with examples spanning equine, small animals, poultry, aquatic, wildlife/zoo, public health, epidemiology, pathology, imaging, welfare and nutrition. RCVS is UK-specific; its regulated title rules are not generalized.

## Boundaries and open points

- Keep species/context areas distinct from cross-species disciplines. For example, clinical nutrition or imaging may connect to several species routes instead of being copied as exclusive requirements under each.
- The proposed eight is an internal synthesis. Equine might be nested under companion/competitive animals in a more compact view; welfare/behaviour/nutrition could be split; public health/epidemiology could separate if the catalogue has distinct learning sets.
- Wildlife/zoo care must remain distinct from general conservation biology; production-animal veterinary medicine must remain distinct from general farming/feed science.
- The computational scan reports three quantitative/mathematical hits but does not establish a standalone computational veterinary specialization. Teach computational methods where veterinary epidemiology, surveillance, bioinformatics or diagnostics sources support them.
- Species-specific protected titles, permits, inspection powers and postgraduate routes must be labelled by country.
