# D084 — Payroll and Bookkeeping

**Status:** Provisional two-area synthesis; statutory details are jurisdiction-specific. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

Payroll and bookkeeping are separate learning areas: payroll concerns employee pay/benefit data and calculations; bookkeeping concerns business transactions, ledgers, reconciliations and trial balances. ILO task descriptions corroborate the distinction but are not used to assign subgroup membership. AAT's bookkeeping progression and CIPP's payroll competency framework provide professional learning evidence; CIPP is explicitly UK-specific.

Propose: Payroll and Benefits Administration; Bookkeeping and Financial Recordkeeping. Tax, deductions and employment law need jurisdiction-specific additions. Audit/assurance, advanced accounting and insurance processing must not be implied by either area. [D084 review evidence](D084_specialization_review.json).
