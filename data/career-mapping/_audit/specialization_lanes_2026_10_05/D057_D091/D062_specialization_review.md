# D062 — Trading: specialization review

**Disposition:** Provisionally recommend two function-based areas: **Trading, Dealing and Market Making** and **Brokerage and Market Intermediation**. A parent-domain scope issue remains open, so this is not ready for canonical implementation.

## Current taxonomy and root-scope problem

The two current groups—Investment & Finance Analysts and Market Traders & Brokers—are broad occupational buckets, not verified learning specializations. More importantly, the D062 description includes securities analysis, investment management and financial planning, which overlap D013 Investment Analysis and Asset Management; quantitative pricing/model work overlaps D011 Quantitative Finance and Financial Engineering. The specialization recommendation assumes D062 is narrowed to market transactions/dealing and intermediation. Resolve that parent boundary before implementation.

## Proposed areas and coverage

1. **Trading, Dealing and Market Making** — analyze market conditions, manage orders and positions, provide liquidity or execute trades, and assess execution quality.
2. **Brokerage and Market Intermediation** — connect participants to venues/counterparties, handle client/agency orders, support market access and coordinate the transaction lifecycle.

CFA Institute describes trading/execution across equities, fixed income, derivatives and FX, and separately describes sales and trading functions. ACI’s international dealing syllabus covers FX, money/capital/commodities markets, rates, derivatives and treasury dealing. FIA’s futures education distinguishes traders, brokers, exchanges and clearinghouses. Those sources support function-based learning areas, but none mandates this exact two-area count. [CFA trade strategy and execution](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/trade-strategy-execution), [CFA sales and trading](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/careers/sales-and-trading), [ACI dealing syllabus](https://acifma.com/sites/default/files/inline-files/ACI%20Dealing%20Certificate%20New%20Version%20Syllabus%208%20Jul%202024_Final.pdf), [FIA futures-market basics](https://www.fia.org/markets-academy/articles/futures-market-basics).

FX/money-market dealing remains a strong specialist learning context, but not a separate third card on present evidence: it can be nested within trading/dealing. Equities, bonds, derivatives, FX, commodities/energy and styles such as proprietary, agency and flow are themes unless comparative evidence demonstrates separate complete learner pathways. Portfolio construction, investment research, financial planning, underwriting securities and quantitative model development are not presumed D062 areas.

FIA covers futures markets, not all financial trading. The proposed count remains **provisional** pending root-scope reconciliation against D011/D013.
