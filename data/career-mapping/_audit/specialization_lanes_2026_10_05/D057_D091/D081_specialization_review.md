# D081 — Office Administration and Secretarial Services

**Status:** Provisional four-area synthesis; adjacent functions remain bounded. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

IAAP's Certified Administrative Professional body of knowledge includes communication, office/records management, information distribution, event/project management and additional functional domains. ISO 15489 independently supports a substantive records-management practice. IAAP is international, but its CAP examination framework is not a universal license or globally binding curriculum.

Propose: Executive and Administrative Support; Office Operations and Business Coordination; Office Records and Information Management; Meetings, Events and Project Coordination. Treat HR, payroll, finance, sector-specific medical administration and generic data entry as separate/contextual paths unless distinct learning requirements support inclusion. The four-area count is an internal synthesis, not copied as a global standard. [D081 review evidence](D081_specialization_review.json).
