# D082 — Customer Service and Contact Centre Operations

**Status:** Two provisional core areas; parent-scope split recommended. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

ISO 18295 distinguishes requirements for contact-centre service organizations from expectations for the client organizations that use them. ISO 10002 provides organization-wide complaints-handling guidance. This supports a conservative core split: Customer Contact and Service Resolution; Contact-Centre Operations and Quality.

Move emergency dispatch, medical administration and hospitality reception/concierge to separate domain or path reviews. Their shared customer interaction does not erase their emergency protocols, health-information handling or hospitality-specific learning. Complaints handling may be a theme across the two areas rather than a separate group without evidence of an independent learning contract. [D082 review evidence](D082_specialization_review.json).
