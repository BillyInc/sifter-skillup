# D075 — Fashion and Apparel

**Status:** Provisional four-area synthesis. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

UNESCO's international design/fashion sector framing includes clothing, textiles and accessories, supporting a scope broader than garment sewing alone. It does not define an academic subgroup count. The UNESCO fashion value-chain study is explicitly Africa-focused and is used only as regional evidence, not a global default.

Recommend provisionally: Fashion and Apparel Design; Textile Materials and Design; Garment Engineering and Production; Fashion Product, Merchandising and Supply Chain.

Product types, techniques and cultural traditions should be presented as contextual routes within these areas unless a distinct cumulative learning scope is demonstrated.
