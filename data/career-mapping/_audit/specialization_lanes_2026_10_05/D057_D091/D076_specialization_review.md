# D076 — Maritime and Naval Operations

**Status:** Parent-scope split recommended; four provisional civilian maritime areas. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

The current domain combines commercial vessel work, fishing vessels, marine engineering, underwater trades and naval service. IMO's STCW convention establishes an international framework for seafarer training and certification, while STCW-F separately covers specified fishing-vessel personnel. WTO/UNCTAD sources support a related ports, shipping and trade-logistics route. None establishes a fixed learner taxonomy.

For the civilian maritime scope, propose: Deck, Navigation and Vessel Operations; Marine Engineering and Vessel Systems; Ports, Shipping and Maritime Logistics; Fishing Vessel Operations. Treat naval/military operations as a distinct defence context, not a default civilian specialization. Hold diving and underwater construction for parent-domain review. National licensing and flag-state requirements must be labelled by jurisdiction. Full evidence and limitations: [D076_specialization_review.json](D076_specialization_review.json).
