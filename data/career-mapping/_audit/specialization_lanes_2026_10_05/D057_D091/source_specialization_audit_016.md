## Internal synthesis versus formal credential

An *area of specialization* in this catalogue is a learner-facing grouping of coherent subject matter/practice and its additional learning. It is not automatically a formal specialty, professional license, certification, protected job title or complete industry taxonomy. Where a body formally recognizes a specialty, the audit states that body's jurisdiction and source. Otherwise the lane calls the proposed grouping an internal synthesis and does not display the source as granting credentials. None of the sources establishes an exact global subgroup count for these seven domains.
