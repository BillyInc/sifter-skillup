# D070 — Performing Arts and Entertainment: specialization review

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** Provisional six-area synthesis; recorded-performance boundary remains open.  
**Scope:** Areas of specialization only. No role assignments or canonical taxonomy edits.

## Recommendation

The current four visible records are occupational/ISCO groupings, not student-facing specializations. Recommend six provisional areas:

1. Theatre and Dramatic Performance
2. Music Performance and Creation
3. Dance and Choreography
4. Opera and Music Theatre
5. Circus and Physical Performance
6. Live Performance Production and Technical Theatre

UNESCO identifies theatre, dance, opera, circus and other live performance; its heritage guidance also treats music, dance and theatre as distinct but sometimes integrated practices. OISTAT independently supports technical theatre as a professional practice. None prescribes a six-area learner taxonomy: six is an internal synthesis, not an external standard.

## Important boundaries

- **Recorded performance:** the parent description includes studios and broadcasters. This review does not establish that film acting or broadcast production belongs in D070; reconcile the neighboring media-domain boundary before implementation.
- **Opera/music theatre and circus:** retain as separate areas only if they have meaningful learning additions beyond music/theatre or physical-performance routes; otherwise present as cross-disciplinary pathways.
- **Technical production:** lighting, sound, scenography, costumes, props and stage management are themes/routes under live performance production unless separate learning contracts justify separate areas.
- **Cultural variation:** preserve local and traditional forms in context; do not make a single tradition the universal template.
- **Computational:** digital production tools support tasks but are not evidence, by themselves, for a computational specialization.

Evidence and claim limits are recorded in [D070_specialization_review.json](D070_specialization_review.json). The decision is not external academic sign-off. Role fit must later be checked separately against both the parent domain and any specialization.
