# D083 — Travel and Tourism

**Status:** Provisional four-area synthesis; adjacent transport/hospitality boundaries remain open. **Scope:** specialization areas only; no role assignments or canonical edits.

UN Statistics distinguishes travel agency, tour operator, reservation and visitor-information activities. UN Tourism describes destination planning/management and multiple tourism service sectors. These support separate learning routes but do not prescribe four student-facing groups.

Propose: Travel Planning and Reservation Services; Tour Operations and Visitor Guiding; Destination Development and Tourism Management; Passenger Travel Services. Do not absorb hotel operations, airline/rail/marine operations or park/conservation management merely because tourists use those services. Product types such as cultural, eco or adventure tourism are contexts unless their independent learning additions are shown. [D083 review evidence](D083_specialization_review.json).
