# D108 — Printing Technology, Pre-press and Print Finishing: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** candidate_set_reviewed_with_explicit_residuals

**Parent scope basis:** No substantive parent-domain description is currently stored. The area set therefore remains conditional on defining the root scope before canonical implementation.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Pre-press and Print-File Preparation** — Prepare and verify digital/physical files, colour, imposition, proofs and plates or other production inputs before printing.
2. **Press Operations and Print Production** — Set up and operate print processes, control materials/colour/quality and produce printed output.
3. **Post-press, Binding and Finishing** — Bind, cut, fold, laminate, finish and inspect printed products after press.

## Decision

The three-stage production model is strongly supported for the print-production scope. Keep graphic design and content creation distinct: design may supply print-ready work but is not itself pre-press/press/post-press production. The three stages form an operational workflow, not a required sequence of qualifications.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- Do not absorb general graphic design, marketing content, or publishing editorial work.
- Digital printing changes equipment/workflow but does not erase file preparation, press/output production, and post-press finishing functions.
- Specific press technologies and bindery methods are themes/tools, not separate areas absent a distinct curriculum route.

## Evidence and claim limits

- [WorldSkills International: Graphic Design Technology WSOS 2024](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1701/) — Print/output constraints and preparation tasks in the occupational standard. **Supports:** Supports a boundary between visual design and production-ready output; used only as adjacent evidence. **Limit:** Graphic-design competition is not a print-operator standard and cannot alone establish D108's stages. **Scope:** International/global benchmark.
- [Skills England: Print operative – Pre-Press Operative OCC0962B](https://occupational-maps.skillsengland.education.gov.uk/maps/occupation/OCC0962B) — Occupation option and duties for pre-press operative. **Supports:** Supports a distinct pre-press work stage. **Limit:** England-specific occupational map; not global qualification guidance. **Scope:** England, UK.
- [Skills England: Print operative – Post-Press Operative OCC0962A](https://occupational-maps.skillsengland.education.gov.uk/maps/occupation/OCC0962A) — Occupation profile explicitly describes print production as pre-press, press and post-press stages. **Supports:** Directly corroborates the three-stage print-production structure. **Limit:** England-specific occupational map; not a global taxonomy or credential requirement. **Scope:** England, UK.
- [Skills England: Print Technician ST0309](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeship-standards/st0309-v1-0) — Occupational standard and duties relating to print production technology. **Supports:** Corroborates technical print production scope. **Limit:** England-specific; use its process evidence, not local credential status, as global requirement. **Scope:** England, UK.
