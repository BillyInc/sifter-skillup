Seven records mix executive rank, management, teaching, ICT and several justice occupations. Criminology as research, criminal-justice institutions, law-enforcement investigation and corrections are not one interchangeable specialization ladder.

## Parent scope and disposition

The parent combines a social-science field (criminology), the justice-system lifecycle and corrections. Keep research/policy and justice-service practice paths distinct. Courts/legal advocacy, policing, forensic science and clinical rehabilitation overlap but have separate professional scope; do not imply a criminology route licenses policing, legal practice or clinical treatment.

The American Society of Criminology's standing divisions include Corrections & Sentencing, Policing, Cybercrime, Victimology and policy/research themes; UNODC criminal-justice materials treat policing, prison management, rehabilitation and reform as distinct institutional practices. These support the four candidate areas as navigation branches, not a universal justice-system curriculum. Existing seven groupings should be rebuilt from tasks and evidence, not retained by ISCO.

## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Criminology Research and Crime Policy** — Study crime, victimization, social context, prevention and justice-system outcomes; translate evidence into policy and evaluation. Boundary: Research/policy path; criminologist is not automatically a police investigator or therapist.
- **Policing and Criminal Investigation** — Public-safety and investigation practices including lawful evidence gathering, interviewing and case development. Boundary: Police powers, academy requirements and detective appointments are jurisdiction-specific; forensic lab science remains D143.
- **Corrections, Rehabilitation and Re-entry** — Custodial systems, prison operations, community supervision, rehabilitation and reintegration, within rights-based frameworks. Boundary: Clinical treatment and social-work interventions require separate professional qualifications and are not implied by correctional employment.
- **Justice-System Administration and Reform** — Policy, institutional performance, alternatives to custody, sentencing/justice reform and coordination among justice institutions. Boundary: Legal representation and judicial authority remain law-domain roles, not criminology specialties.

## Boundary audit

- Omissions / over-splitting: Do not make rank, executive level, teaching or ICT into criminology specializations. Do not absorb courtroom legal practice, forensic laboratory examination, clinical psychology or social work without explicit cross-domain scope and role-specific qualifications.
- Computational signal: Crime statistics, spatial analysis, digital evidence, cybercrime research and justice-system data are computational methods. Cybercrime is a substantive criminology research/practice area in ASC, but its technical response roles may belong to cybersecurity or digital forensics.
- Jurisdiction note: Police powers, court processes, sentencing, correctional systems, juvenile justice, probation qualifications and data privacy are jurisdiction-specific. UN standards provide international principles, not local authorization.

## Sources and limits

- [American Society of Criminology (ASC): ASC Divisions](https://asc41.org/divisions/) — Lists professional research divisions including Corrections & Sentencing, Policing, Cybercrime, Victimology, Public Opinion & Policy and qualitative/developmental criminology. Supports: Professional association evidence for distinct criminology research and justice practice themes. Limitation: US scholarly association division list is neither global nor a complete learner specialization standard.
- [United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC): Handbook on Dynamic Security and Prison Intelligence](https://www.unodc.org/documents/justice-and-prison-reform/CPCJCATALOGUE/Justice_Section_Publications_September_2020.pdf) — UNODC handbook catalogue describes prison security, staff-prisoner interactions and prison intelligence as specialized correctional-management functions. Supports: Supports corrections as a distinct practice area and its boundary from criminology research and police investigation. Limitation: UN guidance is broad and implementation depends on member-state law, institutions and resources.
- [United Nations: Maintain International Peace and Security / justice and security work](https://www.un.org/en/our-work/maintain-international-peace-and-security) — UN describes preventive diplomacy/peace and security but its linked justice institutional materials must be interpreted within separate institutional mandates. Supports: Only contextual evidence of justice/security adjacency; it is not relied on as the primary taxonomy basis. Limitation: Not a direct criminal-justice specialization standard.
