## Parent scope and disposition

Retain personal image/style consulting and styling services, but distinguish client-facing image consultation/personal shopping from editorial/commercial styling and retail visual merchandising. Hair/makeup work may be part of a complete styling service but often has its own craft training and role identity. Clarify that brand/retail merchandising is not personal styling.

AICI's international association materials identify wardrobe consultation, color consultation, personal shopping, clothing/uniform design, hair/makeup styling, image branding and related client services. That supports a coherent image-consulting branch but also shows the domain spans retail display and media styling. Four candidate routes are a navigation synthesis; visual merchandising and production costume need parent-boundary validation. Existing six subgroup records are not evidence of six specialties.

## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Personal Styling and Wardrobe Consulting** — Assess client needs, build wardrobe strategies and provide personal clothing/style recommendations and related image consultation. Boundary: Client advisory path; AICI service examples include wardrobe and color consultations and personal shopping.
- **Editorial, Commercial and Media Styling** — Select and coordinate clothing/looks for editorial, advertising, media, public appearances or production briefs. Boundary: Costume design/wardrobe for screen and stage has separate production workflows and may belong to D042 or costume/fashion domains.
- **Personal Shopping and Retail Client Advisory** — Source, select and procure clothing/accessories for clients or provide personal shopping services. Boundary: Distinguish advisory service from retail sales, buying/merchandising and store management.
- **Visual Merchandising and Fashion Presentation** — Plan store/product presentation and visual displays that shape a retail customer experience. Boundary: This is a retail/brand presentation practice, not simply personal image consulting; retain under this parent only if root includes fashion presentation.

## Boundary audit

- Omissions / over-splitting: Do not use broad science, teaching, hospitality management or sales occupations as specialty labels. Do not split by client demographics, body type, color season, fashion genre or product category. Keep makeup artistry, hairdressing, costume design and visual merchandising separate where training/task boundaries require it.
- Computational signal: Digital wardrobe tools, styling platforms, virtual try-on, retail visual analytics and brand/content tools may support particular routes; none is a shared foundation requirement across all stylists or image consultants.
- Jurisdiction note: Image-consultant and stylist titles are generally not globally licensed; cosmetics safety, consumer law, employment/production rules and cultural dress norms vary by jurisdiction and context. AICI is an international professional association, not a regulator.
