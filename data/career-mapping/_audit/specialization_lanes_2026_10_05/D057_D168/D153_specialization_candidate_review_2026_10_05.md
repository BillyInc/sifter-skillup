## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Music Production and Arrangement** — Shape musical material and production decisions for recorded releases, including arrangement and collaboration with performers/composers. Boundary: Songwriting/composition and performer careers may be separately owned; producer duties vary by project and market.
- **Recording and Studio Engineering** — Capture sound, plan sessions, operate recording systems and manage technical audio quality in studio or location contexts. Boundary: Distinguish studio recording from live sound reinforcement and broadcast engineering.
- **Mixing and Mastering** — Balance, process and finalize multitrack recordings for technical translation and release delivery. Boundary: Mixing and mastering are commonly distinct tasks but should only be separate learner areas if the learning links and evidence justify two cards.
- **Live Music Sound Production** — Reinforce and manage sound for live music performance, including system setup, mixing and event sound workflow. Boundary: Event production, concert touring and venue operation require separate safety/production context; game/film audio belongs to adjacent domains.

## Boundary audit

- Omissions / over-splitting: Do not classify every musician, conductor, music director or composer as a producer. Do not automatically include film/game audio, podcasting, broadcast engineering, concert logistics or instrument performance. Avoid separate subgroup for each genre, recording format, DAW or audio technology.
- Computational signal: Digital audio workstations, signal processing, plugin/software workflows, spatial/immersive formats and metadata/delivery systems are central tools and methods; the computational signal is technical production, not general software engineering.
- Jurisdiction note: Copyright, royalties, recording contracts, broadcast loudness/delivery rules, safety and union/venue practices vary by jurisdiction and distribution platform. AES is a global professional society, not an occupational licensing authority.
