# D110 — Chemical, Rubber and Plastics Plant Operations: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** source_gap_closed_candidate_boundary_or_count_remains_provisional

**Parent scope basis:** Chemical and rubber processing involves running plants that convert raw materials into products such as soap, lime and other chemicals. Operators control distillation units, kilns, blenders and soap-making machines, adjust process settings, troubleshoot faults and follow strict safety rules with protective equipment. Common employers are chemical plants, soap factories and other process industries.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Chemical Process-Plant Operations** — Monitor and control chemical production processes and plant equipment, following process, safety and quality procedures.
2. **Rubber and Plastics Processing** — Operate processes that compound, mould, extrude or otherwise convert polymers, rubber and plastics into products.

## Decision

Two candidate areas fit the two distinct process/material families; no third area is justified. Keep the count provisional because ISCO and an Australian training package are not global learner-taxonomy authorities. Source-gap disposition recorded as `two_process_families_corroborated_area_count_candidate_not_prescribed`.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- Laboratory chemistry and chemical analysis are not plant operations.
- Chemical-engineering process design is distinct from operator-level plant work.
- Rubber/plastics are grouped for now; split only if curriculum and occupational sources show distinct learner routes in this parent.
