# D147: Industrial Process Automation (SCADA and PLC Systems) — specialization audit

Status: `source_reviewed_root_too_narrow_and_groups_mixed`. This is a source-grounded candidate review, not canonical taxonomy approval or academic-body sign-off.

## Existing structure

Current subgroup-record count: **5**. Current records: Automation & Control Engineers (4 roles); Teaching professionals (1 role); Information and communications technology professionals (1 role); Plant & Robot Operators (8 roles); Electrical and electronic trades workers (1 role)

The groups mix one relevant engineering practice label with generic teaching/ICT, plant operators and electrical trades. The root's SCADA/PLC framing is too technology-specific for broader industrial automation and process-control practice.

## Scope and disposition

Parent boundary: Define the parent as design, integration, operation support and lifecycle engineering of industrial automation and control systems (IACS/OT). Include process and discrete manufacturing contexts, instrumentation, control logic, supervisory systems and safety/security interfaces. Operators, educators and electricians may be adjacent users/support roles, not engineering specializations by default.

ISA defines automation broadly as creating/applying technology to monitor and control production and service delivery, spanning measurement/control, sensors, robotics, integration and cybersecurity. ISA/IEC 62443 treats IACS security as a lifecycle concern. These sources support a broader parent and four technical practice candidates; they do not support teaching, generic ICT or operator families as specializations. Cross-sector engineering may be global while local process-sector variants must be explicitly labelled.

## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Instrumentation and Process Measurement** — Sensors, field instruments, measurement, calibration, signal conditioning and measurement systems used in industrial processes. Boundary: Instrumentation is a foundation for control but has distinct device/measurement learning outcomes.
- **Process Control and SCADA/DCS** — Control strategies, PLC/DCS/SCADA, control-room systems, alarms, telemetry and process-system integration. Boundary: SCADA/PLC are technologies within the practice route, not the complete domain definition.
- **Manufacturing Automation and Robotics Integration** — Automation of production cells/lines, motion/control integration, industrial robotics and manufacturing-system coordination. Boundary: Robotics design belongs to D140; this branch concerns integration into industrial production.
- **Industrial Functional Safety and OT Cybersecurity** — Safety lifecycle and secure operation of connected industrial automation/control systems. Boundary: Safety engineering and cybersecurity may be separately governed disciplines; show them only as supported specialist routes and never imply a standard/certification is universally mandatory.

## Boundary audit

- Omissions / over-splitting: Do not make PLC brands, protocol stacks or specific plant sectors separate subgroup labels. Do not collapse process control with discrete manufacturing automation; retain separate depth where the learner tasks diverge. Keep plant operators distinct from system design/engineering paths.
- Computational signal: PLC/DCS logic, SCADA data, control algorithms, HMI, simulation, systems integration and OT cybersecurity are computational/digital methods central to this domain. They are distributed by pathway, not all common foundation items.
- Jurisdiction note: Process-safety law, electrical licensing, functional-safety duties and cybersecurity requirements vary by sector and jurisdiction. ISA/IEC 62443 is an international standard family; citing it does not make it a certification or universally required credential.

## Sources and limitations

- [International Society of Automation (ISA): What is Automation?](https://www.isa.org/about-isa/what-is-automation) — Defines automation as technology to monitor/control production and delivery, and enumerates robotics, telemetry, cybersecurity, measurement/control, sensors and system integration. Supports: Primary professional association source for a broader root scope than SCADA/PLC alone and for cross-cutting application families. Limitation: Professional association definition, not a formal global curriculum taxonomy.
- [ISA / International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC): ISA/IEC 62443 Series of Standards](https://www.isa.org/standards-and-publications/isa-standards/isa-iec-62443-series-of-standards) — Describes lifecycle requirements/processes for industrial automation and control system security across industries and bridging operations technology and IT. Supports: Evidence that OT cybersecurity is a significant automation-system practice with lifecycle-specific learning. Limitation: Standards guidance is not a student specialization mandate or a certification requirement for every automation role.

Evidence supports candidate discipline/practice distinctions, not a universal subgroup count. Existing role-group membership is not used as proof of fit. No role-to-specialization assignment is made.
