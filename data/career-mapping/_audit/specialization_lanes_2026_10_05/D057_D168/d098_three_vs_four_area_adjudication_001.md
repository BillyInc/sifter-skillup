## Candidate boundaries

- **Precision Machining and Toolmaking** — Precision part/tool/die/fixture production; combine machining and toolmaking because the learner routes share precision production foundations and source evidence does not require separate areas.
- **Precision Instruments and Optical Devices** — Build, service, test or repair precision measuring/optical instruments; metrology methods used within instrument work remain connected but are not collapsed into this route.
- **Dimensional Metrology and Measurement Systems** — Measurement strategy, GD&T, calibration/traceability, uncertainty, CMM/optical measurement and validated results as a standalone learning route; generic QA is not enough.
- **Horology (Clock and Watchmaking)** — Making, servicing, repairing and restoring timepieces; explicitly within the current parent description, not held as a parent-scope anomaly.

## Evidence and limitations

- [University of North Carolina at Charlotte: Graduate Certificate in Precision Metrology](https://metrologycertificate.charlotte.edu/program-description/) — Program description and four-course/12-credit requirement in dimensional metrology theory and industrial application. **Supports:** Formal independent metrology curriculum with coherent learning outcomes. **Limit:** Institutional U.S. graduate certificate; proves route existence, not global credential requirement or D098 parent fit.
- [Piedmont Technical College: Precision Metrology Quickskills Certificate](https://catalog.ptc.edu/academic-programs/industrial-technology/machine-tool-technology-curricula/precision-metrology-quickskills-certificate/) — Curriculum includes GD&T, advanced dimensional metrology and CMM operation/programming; learning outcomes specify appropriate instrument use and CMM proficiency. **Supports:** A distinct applied manufacturing metrology route exists apart from general machining study, though it builds on prior shop knowledge. **Limit:** Institutional U.S. certificate; states prerequisites/target audience and is not universal.
- [U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology: Laboratory Metrology Training](https://www.nist.gov/pml/weights-and-measures/laboratory-metrology/lab-metrology-training) — Course listing: fundamentals, uncertainty, calibration method validation, traceability, calibration intervals and laboratory practice. **Supports:** Shows coherent metrology learning topics and specialized practice. **Limit:** U.S. national metrology institute training, with jurisdictional weights-and-measures context; not D098 taxonomy authority.
- [Coventry University / National Physical Laboratory: Dimensional Measurement (NPL) Level 1 and Specialist Metrology Courses](https://www.coventry.ac.uk/research/areas-of-research/centre-for-manufacturing-and-materials/our-research/research-group-metrology/metrology-courses/) — Dimensional measurement courses and five-day Metrology Masterclass covering principles, assessment, systems improvement, equipment, sensors and methods. **Supports:** Independent structured learning route for dimensional metrology. **Limit:** UK university/CPD offering; corroborates route existence, not a globally required credential or local role assignment.
