## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Tax Compliance and Advisory** — Interpret applicable tax law, support accurate tax filings and advise taxpayers on tax consequences and compliance obligations. Boundary: Tax regimes and reserved practice rights differ by country; no global license or one universal tax syllabus.
- **International Tax and Transfer Pricing** — Analyze cross-border related-party transactions and apply arm's-length principles, documentation and related tax rules. Boundary: OECD Guidelines are a consensus framework but domestic implementation varies; transfer pricing is not generic financial analysis.
- **Tax Disputes and Controversy** — Support review, audit response, administrative proceedings, competent-authority processes and resolution of tax disputes. Boundary: Legal representation rights and procedure vary; distinguish technical tax advice from attorney-only work.
- **Tax Policy and Administration** — Design, interpret or administer tax systems, compliance and revenue policy within public institutions or policy organizations. Boundary: Public revenue authority roles are jurisdiction-specific and should be labelled by country when shown.

## Boundary audit

- Omissions / over-splitting: Do not include budget analysts, financial controllers or auditors merely because they read accounts. Avoid one subgroup per tax type, country, treaty article or industry. Do not imply a qualification in this domain authorizes legal practice or tax representation in any jurisdiction.
- Computational signal: Tax data analytics, entity/transaction databases, benchmarking, documentation platforms and reporting automation support this field. Computation is a tool/use-case family and does not erase the legal/jurisdiction-specific foundation.
- Jurisdiction note: Tax law, credentials, filing rights, professional-body membership and dispute procedures are jurisdiction-specific. OECD transfer-pricing guidance is an international consensus framework, not itself a universal statute or certification.

## Sources and limits

Supports: Directly supports transfer pricing as a distinct international tax practice and cautions that countries implement rules differently. Limitation: OECD framework does not replace domestic law, local credentials or competent-authority procedure.
