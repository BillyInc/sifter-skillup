# D146: Building Services Engineering (Mechanical and Electrical Systems) — specialization audit

Status: `source_reviewed_existing_occupation_buckets_not_specialization_structure`. This is a source-grounded candidate review, not canonical taxonomy approval or academic-body sign-off.

## Existing structure

Current subgroup-record count: **5**. Current records: Production and specialised services managers (2 roles); Science and engineering professionals (4 roles); Science and engineering associate professionals (3 roles); Business and administration associate professionals (1 role); Building and related trades workers, excluding electricians (2 roles)

Five groups are occupational levels/families, not the engineering areas. The root's mechanical/electrical title and description also mention fire protection and smart building controls, so the intended system scope is broader than two engineering branches.

## Scope and disposition

Parent boundary: Retain coordinated engineering of building systems/services for safe, functional, efficient indoor environments. Include design/integration and commissioning engineering; distinguish construction trades, facility operations, architecture and general project management. CIBSE's technical guidance indicates relevant system families beyond only mechanical and electrical.

CIBSE's guidance organizes building-services topics across HVAC, transportation in buildings, fire safety, energy efficiency, plumbing/public health, controls, electricity and sustainability. Five candidate learner areas consolidate these into coherent system families. This is a navigation synthesis, not a CIBSE-mandated subgroup count; building transport/lifts and acoustics may need inclusion or a cross-link depending on parent scope.

## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (HVAC)** — Thermal comfort, ventilation, refrigeration, heating, cooling, air distribution and indoor environmental performance. Boundary: Includes the building environmental systems described by CIBSE; do not conflate with general mechanical plant design.
- **Electrical, Lighting and Building Power** — Electrical services, power distribution, lighting/daylight systems and their integration in buildings. Boundary: Power networks and general electrical trades overlap but are not identical to building-services design.
- **Public Health, Water and Plumbing Systems** — Water supply, drainage, sanitation and public-health engineering systems within buildings. Boundary: Do not label this as clinical public health; this is built-environment engineering.
- **Fire, Life Safety and Protective Systems** — Fire detection/protection and engineered building systems addressing occupant safety and relevant building risks. Boundary: Security system operations and statutory fire-safety approvals are related but may have different ownership/regulatory requirements.
- **Building Controls, Energy and Systems Integration** — Building automation/control, metering, energy performance, intelligent-building integration and commissioning across services. Boundary: Controls cut across mechanical/electrical systems; present as a distinct deepening route only where curriculum and tasks justify it, not as a universal separate discipline.

## Boundary audit

- Omissions / over-splitting: Do not retain manager/associate/trade group labels as specialization cards. Do not create a subgroup per building type or per equipment brand; consider lifts, acoustics, communications/security and maintenance as nested/cross-cutting pathways after confirming curriculum fit.
- Computational signal: Building controls, energy modeling, building information modeling, simulation, commissioning analytics and smart-building systems are computational/digital practice threads; they are not universal requirements for every building-services pathway.
- Jurisdiction note: Building codes, fire rules, energy standards, protected engineering titles and electrical authorizations vary by jurisdiction. CIBSE is a UK professional institution with internationally used guidance, not a global building regulator.

## Sources and limitations

Supports: Primary professional-body source for broad service-system families and omitted areas beyond mechanical/electrical alone. Limitation: CIBSE is UK-based and its guide taxonomy is guidance organization, not a globally binding subgroup standard.
- [CIBSE: What is Building Services Engineering?](https://www.cibse.org/training/professional-development/careers-in-building-services/what-is-building-services/) — Describes services supporting building occupants, including lighting, heating/ventilation, lifts, acoustics, plumbing, power, energy management, security/safety and fire detection/protection. Supports: Corroborates parent breadth and candidate boundaries. Limitation: Professional careers overview; does not prescribe a universal student taxonomy.
