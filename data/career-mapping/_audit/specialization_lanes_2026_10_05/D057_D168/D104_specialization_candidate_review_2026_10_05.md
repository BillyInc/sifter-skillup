# D104 — Pottery, Ceramics and Glasswork: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** source_gap_closed_candidate_boundary_or_count_remains_provisional

**Parent scope basis:** Pottery, ceramics and glass covers the craft of forming clay, porcelain and glass into useful and decorative objects. Practitioners such as ceramic painters, glass-blowers, pottery casters, brick moulders and engravers mould, cast, blow, paint and engrave their pieces, often working from their own sketches. They typically work in potteries, glassworks, brickyards and craft studios.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Pottery and Ceramic Making** — Form, fire, decorate and finish clay or ceramic objects for functional or decorative use.
2. **Glass Forming and Glasswork** — Form, blow, shape, fabricate, engrave or finish glass objects and components in craft/workshop contexts.

## Decision

Two material-practice candidates are supported; exact student taxonomy/count remains provisional because the strongest global evidence distinguishes occupational/material classes rather than prescribing curricula. Keep industrial glass/ceramics plant operation separate unless the parent is broadened from craft production. Source-gap disposition recorded as `two_material_practice_families_supported_candidate_count_internal`.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- Ceramic tile installation is construction work (D099), not pottery/ceramic making.
- Industrial glass/ceramic plant operation is not conflated with studio craft.
- The two-area synthesis is not an externally approved universal taxonomy.
