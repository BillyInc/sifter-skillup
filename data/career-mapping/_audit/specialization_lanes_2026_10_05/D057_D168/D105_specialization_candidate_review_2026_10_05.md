# D105 — Handicrafts, Basketry and Textile Crafts: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** source_gap_closed_candidate_boundary_or_count_remains_provisional

**Parent scope basis:** Handicraft, basketry and textile craft is about making goods by hand from fibres, yarn, wicker and similar materials. Weavers, knitters, carpet makers, wicker furniture makers, brush makers, toymakers and artisan papermakers shape these materials using hand techniques and simple equipment they maintain themselves. Work is usually based in small workshops, craft centres and cottage industries.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Textile and Fibre Crafts** — Make textile goods by hand or simple equipment, including weaving, knitting, embroidery and related fibre techniques.
2. **Basketry and Plant-Fibre Crafts** — Make baskets, wickerwork and related objects from plant fibres and similar flexible materials.

## Decision

Retain two candidate material families. Evidence does not justify the third 'other material-based crafts' candidate; the category is too broad and risks swallowing D102, D103, D104 and D107. Count is provisional because trade classifications/product groupings do not define a learning taxonomy. Source-gap disposition recorded as `two_distinct_material_families_supported_other_materials_not_admitted_without_parent_evidence`.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- Exclude leather goods (D107), furniture/wood products (D102), ceramics/glass objects (D104), and industrial textile-machine operation (D111).
- Carpet making may be textile craft; do not create a separate subgroup without an independent route.
- UNCTAD product groups and ISCO categories are corroboration only, not specialization standards.
