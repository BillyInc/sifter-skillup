# D111 — Textile and Garment Machine Operations: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** source_gap_closed_candidate_boundary_or_count_remains_provisional

**Parent scope basis:** Textile and garment manufacturing runs the machinery that spins fibre into yarn and turns yarn into fabric and clothing. Spinning and twisting operators, knitting and finishing technicians and pattern-printing workers run machines, measure yarn, test fabric quality and inspect finished goods, with supervisors managing operations. Textile mills and garment factories are the usual workplaces.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Fibre Preparation and Spinning** — Prepare fibres and operate machinery that twists/spins fibres into yarn.
2. **Fabric Formation** — Operate weaving, knitting and related machinery that forms fabrics from yarn or fibre.
3. **Textile Colouration, Printing and Finishing** — Operate machinery and processes for textile dyeing, printing, finishing and surface treatment.
4. **Garment-Machine Production (parent-boundary candidate)** — Operate sewing/assembly machinery for garment production, distinct from garment design and hand tailoring.

## Decision

Three textile process areas are supported by machinery standards evidence. Keep garment-machine production as a conditional fourth candidate because the ISO machinery committee's sewing-machine liaison and sector OSH code do not alone demonstrate an independent learner route within this parent; seek an occupational curriculum before canonicalizing it. Source-gap disposition recorded as `three_textile_process_families_supported_garment_machine_branch_needs_parent_boundary_review`.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- D105 covers hand textile/fibre craft; D106 covers garment design, patternmaking and tailoring; D111 is industrial machine operation.
- Textile testing, maintenance, supervision and quality are task themes unless independent route evidence supports areas.
- Do not count sewing-machine liaison within a machinery committee as proof of a garment-production specialization.
