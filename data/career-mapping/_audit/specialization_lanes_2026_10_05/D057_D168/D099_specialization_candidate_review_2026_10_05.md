# D099 — Masonry, Bricklaying, Tiling and Plastering: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** candidate_set_reviewed_with_explicit_residuals

**Parent scope basis:** Masonry and its related trades build and finish the solid parts of buildings, such as walls, floors and ceilings. Bricklayers lay blocks, tile fitters and floor layers finish surfaces, ceiling and glass installers fit out interiors, and stone engravers carve decorative work, all under construction safety rules. Most work is with construction firms, and skills can be certified through government trade tests.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Brick and Block Masonry** — Set out and build or repair brick/block structures from plans, with accurate bonding, alignment and finish.
2. **Tiling and Architectural Surface Installation** — Prepare substrates and install wall/floor tiles and related architectural surfaces to specified layout and finish.
3. **Plastering, Rendering and Drywall Systems** — Prepare and finish wet-plastered/rendered surfaces and install/finish drywall framing and board systems.

## Decision

Three candidate areas are sufficiently distinct by installed output and task practice for an internal student-facing synthesis. Keep the exact count provisional: standards describe occupations/competition tasks, not a universal subgroup taxonomy.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- Stone carving, masonry restoration, and specialist heritage work need evidence of a distinct learner route before becoming separate areas.
- Glass installation and general concrete work are not admitted solely because they are construction finishes.
- Do not equate tiling with ceramic making (D104) or plasterboard manufacture.

- [WorldSkills International: Bricklaying WSOS 2024](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1695/) — Associated role and standards: setting out, interpreting drawings, constructing and repairing brick structures. **Supports:** Brick/block masonry is a distinct construction practice. **Limit:** WorldSkills occupational benchmark, not academic accreditation or a mandated specialization count. **Scope:** International/global benchmark.
- [WorldSkills International: Wall and Floor Tiling WSOS 2024](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1686/) — Associated role and standard sections on setting out, tile installation, drawings and finish. **Supports:** Tiling has a distinguishable substrate/layout/material installation task set. **Limit:** Competition occupational standard; does not prescribe taxonomy or licensing globally. **Scope:** International/global benchmark.
- [WorldSkills International: Plastering and Drywall Systems WSOS 2024](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1685/) — Associated role; plastering/rendering, drywall framing/board installation and finishing. **Supports:** Wet surface finishes and drywall systems have a recognizable technical practice. **Limit:** International vocational benchmark, not academic endorsement. **Scope:** International/global benchmark.
- [Skills England: Bricklayer occupational profile](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/occupations/OCC0095) — Occupation profile/duties for bricklayer. **Supports:** Independent employer-developed corroboration of bricklaying practice. **Limit:** England-specific occupational framework; not a global credential or requirement. **Scope:** England, UK.
- [Skills England: Plasterer occupational profile](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/qualifications/finder/OCC0096?view=qualifications) — Plasterer occupation/qualification profile. **Supports:** Corroborates plastering as distinct skilled work. **Limit:** England-specific and not a global taxonomy or universal requirement. **Scope:** England, UK.
