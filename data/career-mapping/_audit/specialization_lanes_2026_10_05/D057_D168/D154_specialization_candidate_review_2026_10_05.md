# D154: Exercise Science and Sports Therapy — specialization audit

Status: `source_reviewed_clinical_and_nonclinical_paths_mixed`. Source-grounded candidate review, not canonical taxonomy approval or academic-body sign-off.

## Existing structure

Current subgroup-record count: **3**. Current records: Sports Health Professionals (4 roles); Physio & Massage Assistants (2 roles); Fitness Trainers & Coaches (5 roles)

The groups combine regulated clinical professions, support occupations and fitness coaching. The parent title also combines scientific study of exercise with sports therapy, which may mean licensed physiotherapy, sports medicine, clinical exercise physiology or non-clinical performance coaching.

## Parent scope and disposition

Keep exercise science/physiology and sport performance distinct from clinical sports physiotherapy/therapy. Clinical rehabilitation is delivered by appropriately trained/licensed professionals; fitness training and strength coaching have other scopes. Decide whether the product intends an interdisciplinary sports-health umbrella or separate domains with transparent cross-links.

ACSM identifies distinct exercise-science interests and clinical exercise physiology, while World Physiotherapy's international sports-physiotherapy subgroup develops knowledge, competencies and standards for sports physiotherapy. Four candidates help separate science, coaching, clinical exercise practice and licensed sports rehabilitation. The current three subgroups are too occupationally mixed; do not infer clinical eligibility from a sports/exercise-science route.

## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Exercise Physiology and Human Performance** — Study physiological responses/adaptations to physical activity and assess performance/fitness using appropriate measurement and exercise science. Boundary: Research/science and applied assessment are not automatically clinical diagnosis or treatment.
- **Strength, Conditioning and Fitness Coaching** — Plan and coach exercise/conditioning programmes for fitness, performance and physical preparation. Boundary: Scope and credentials vary; do not present as physiotherapy or treatment for injury/disease.
- **Clinical Exercise Physiology** — Use exercise assessment/prescription in clinical or chronic-condition settings within the professional's training and local scope. Boundary: ACSM qualification examples are US-specific; clinical experience and authorization must be shown accurately and locally.
- **Sports Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation** — Prevention, assessment and rehabilitation of sport-related injury and return-to-sport within physiotherapy scope. Boundary: World Physiotherapy's specialist group supports an international professional practice area; local protected title and independent-practice requirements differ.

## Boundary audit

- Omissions / over-splitting: Do not merge physiotherapists, massage assistants, dietitians, personal trainers and coaches into one health-professional subgroup. Nutrition, sports medicine physician practice, massage therapy and coaching may require separate domain ownership; do not create a subgroup per sport or training method.
- Computational signal: Wearable-sensor analysis, exercise testing, biomechanics, training-load analytics and performance data are computational methods in science/coaching/rehabilitation. They are not common requirements across all routes and cannot authorize clinical practice.
- Jurisdiction note: Physiotherapy, clinical exercise physiology, dietetics, massage, prescribing and protected health-professional titles differ by country. ACSM certifications are US-based; World Physiotherapy provides international professional collaboration, not national licensure.

## Sources and limits

- [World Physiotherapy / International Sports and Exercise Physiotherapy Association (ISEPA): International Sports and Exercise Physiotherapy Association](https://world.physio/subgroups/sports) — World Physiotherapy describes ISEPA as a worldwide federation supporting sports physiotherapists and developing international core standards, competencies, clinical guidelines and professional knowledge. Supports: Authoritative international professional evidence for sports physiotherapy as a distinct clinical practice area. Limitation: Does not override national entry-to-practice, scope or protected-title rules.
- [American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM): Special Interest Groups](https://acsm.org/altai-pages/member-hub/get-involved/special-interest-groups/) — Lists interest areas including exercise science education, team sports, strength and conditioning, youth sport/exercise science and clinical exercise physiology association. Supports: Corroborates diversity of exercise-science/performance practice and clinical exercise physiology. Limitation: US-based professional community organization; interest groups are not global licensing categories.
- [ACSM: Get Certified](https://acsm.org/certification/get-certified/) — Distinguishes exercise physiologist and clinical exercise physiologist pathways and describes degree/experience requirements for those ACSM certifications. Supports: Shows applied fitness and clinical exercise physiology require different preparation; supports an explicit clinical boundary. Limitation: ACSM certification pathways are US-specific and do not define international licensure.

Evidence supports candidate distinctions, not a universal subgroup count. Current role membership is not used as proof of fit. No role-to-specialization mapping is made.
