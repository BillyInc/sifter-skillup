# D103 — Jewellery Making and Goldsmithing: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** candidate_set_reviewed_with_explicit_residuals

**Parent scope basis:** Jewellery making and goldsmithing is the craft of shaping precious metals and stones into rings, chains and other ornaments. Goldsmiths, jewellers, engravers, stone cutters and setters create new pieces, mount and adjust stones, and repair damaged items using specialist equipment. Most practitioners work in small workshops, jewellery shops or craft studios.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Jewellery Design and Digital Product Development** — Develop jewellery concepts and technical designs, including sketches or CAD/CAM models, specifications and production-ready details.
2. **Jewellery Making, Setting and Finishing** — Form, cast, assemble, set stones, engrave/enamel where relevant, polish, repair and finish jewellery or precious-metal objects.

## Decision

Two candidate areas capture design/product development and hands-on manufacture, but remain provisional: the international Jewellery WSOS integrates design interpretation and manufacture in one occupational standard, while the English standard enumerates nine technical functions. Those sources establish breadth, not a universal two-route learner taxonomy.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- Stone setting, casting, engraving, enamelling, lapidary, mounting and polishing are themes/techniques unless curricula establish independent learner routes with clear prerequisites and outcomes.
- Precious-metal assay/sale regulation is jurisdiction-specific; gemmology and jewellery retail are not automatically part of making.
- Do not map a role merely because it carries a jeweller/goldsmith title; role fit remains separate.

## Evidence and claim limits

- [WorldSkills International: Jewellery WSOS 2024](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1681/) — Occupation description and sections on design components, precious metal, manufacture, stone setting, finishing and repair. **Supports:** Shows jewellery design interpretation and manufacturing as linked specialist practices. **Limit:** The WSOS integrates them within one competition occupation; it does not prove a distinct count or separate route. **Scope:** International/global benchmark.
- [Skills England: Jewellery, silversmithing and allied trades professional](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/occupations/OCC0439H-v1-1) — Occupation map lists the nine technical functions: CAD/CAM, casting, enamelling, engraving, lapidary, mounting, polishing/finishing, silversmithing and stone setting; T Level occupational specialism: jewellery maker. **Supports:** Corroborates a structured set of technical learning functions within jewellery manufacture. **Limit:** England-specific framework; options/functions are not evidence of nine learner-facing specializations or global credentials. **Scope:** England, UK.

**Evidence limit:** Sources establish practice tasks and boundaries; they do not prescribe universal specialization counts or endorse this catalogue.
