# D159: Credit Analysis and Structured Finance — specialization audit

Status: `source_reviewed_two_parent_practices_not_currently_separated`. Source-grounded candidate review, not canonical taxonomy approval or academic-body sign-off.

## Existing structure

Current subgroup-record count: **2**. Current records: Business and Administration Professionals (18 roles); Business and Administration Associate Professionals (9 roles)

Two generic business/administration grades conflate credit risk analysis, loan origination/advice, credit management and structured finance. The description includes mortgage brokers and credit managers but does not explain the securitization/structured-finance half of the parent.

## Parent scope and disposition

Retain analysis and structuring of credit risk and debt finance, but distinguish lender-side underwriting/portfolio risk from structured products/securitization. Credit analysis evaluates borrower/obligor risk; structured finance engineers financing claims/pools and risk allocation. Mortgage broking and lending sales are adjacent origination activities, not synonymous with analytical credit work.

CFA Institute's credit-analysis curriculum defines borrower/issuer credit assessment, while the Structured Finance Association explains securitization across asset classes and capital markets. These support separate credit-risk and structured-finance branches. Four candidates are provisional; the existing two ISCO-grade groups do not communicate specialization and the parent needs stronger role evidence for portfolio management/consumer-credit coverage.

## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Corporate and Commercial Credit Analysis** — Assess company/business-model, financial statements, cash flows, repayment capacity, collateral, covenants and default/loss risk. Boundary: CFA curriculum directly supports this task family; credit approval authority and rating decisions are institutionally governed.
- **Consumer, Mortgage and SME Credit Risk** — Assess and monitor consumer, mortgage and small-business credit portfolios using borrower data, repayment and risk criteria. Boundary: Product rules, fair-lending and data requirements are jurisdiction-specific; brokers arrange credit but do not necessarily analyze institutional risk.
- **Credit Portfolio Management and Restructuring** — Monitor aggregate exposures, concentrations, portfolio performance, problem loans, workouts and restructuring. Boundary: Keep portfolio-risk analytics distinct from individual borrower underwriting while allowing shared foundations.
- **Structured Finance and Securitization** — Structure financing from pools of assets/cash flows, tranche or allocate credit risk and assess structured products. Boundary: Structured finance includes legal, accounting and market infrastructure; security design and investor analysis are related but distinct tasks.

## Boundary audit

- Omissions / over-splitting: Do not subdivide by every borrower class, loan product or rating scale. Keep debt capital markets, corporate finance, mortgage sales and loan servicing as adjacent paths unless actual role tasks establish inclusion. Do not imply credit scores alone equal professional credit analysis.
- Computational signal: Financial statement modeling, probability-of-default/loss analysis, portfolio monitoring, structured-cash-flow modeling and data/risk platforms are computationally supported tasks. Tool choice and modeling depth differ between credit analyst, portfolio manager and structured-finance specialist.
- Jurisdiction note: Credit reporting, lending, fair-treatment, collateral/enforcement and securitization rules differ by jurisdiction. CFA/SFA materials inform practice but are not legal or regulatory authorization.

## Sources and limits

- [CFA Institute: Credit Analysis for Corporate Issuers](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/credit-analysis-for-corporate-issuers) — CFA curriculum covers borrower business model, qualitative/quantitative default and loss risk, financial statements, cash-flow forecasts, ratios, seniority and collateral. Supports: Primary professional curriculum evidence for corporate credit-analysis tasks. Limitation: CFA curriculum is not a complete global job map and does not set lending permissions.
- [Structured Finance Association (SFA): About Securitization](https://structuredfinance.org/about-securitization/) — Explains securitization/structured finance and identifies asset pools such as auto, residential mortgage, credit-card, student-loan and commercial-real-estate debt. Supports: Professional-industry source distinguishes structured finance from individual borrower credit analysis and illustrates the product domain. Limitation: SFA is a US-based trade association and its market figures/products are not a universal taxonomy.
