# D100 — Plumbing, Pipefitting and Building Climate Systems: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** candidate_set_reviewed_with_explicit_residuals

**Parent scope basis:** No substantive parent-domain description is currently stored. The area set therefore remains conditional on defining the root scope before canonical implementation.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Plumbing, Water Supply and Drainage** — Install, test, maintain and repair water supply, sanitary, rainwater and drainage systems.
2. **Heating and Hot-Water Systems** — Install, commission, service and maintain building heating and hot-water systems, including relevant heat-pump applications.
3. **Refrigeration, Air Conditioning and Ventilation** — Install, commission, service and maintain refrigeration, air-conditioning and ventilation systems.

## Decision

Three learner-facing practice areas are supported as a provisional synthesis; pipefitting is a work method/context across the branches, not a separate area by itself. Keep refrigeration/AC distinct from plumbing/heating while presenting ventilation as part of building climate systems unless stronger evidence supports a separate route.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- Industrial process pipefitting is not automatically building plumbing; include only when the parent scope explicitly covers that route.
- Gas, refrigerant handling and electrical licensing are jurisdiction-specific; label by jurisdiction and never present as universal credentials.
- Building-services design engineering is not the same as installation/service practice.

## Evidence and claim limits

- [WorldSkills International: Plumbing and Heating WSOS 2024](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1687/) — Installation of pipework, fixtures and appliances; water, wastewater, heating, heat-pump and solar-thermal tasks. **Supports:** Plumbing and heating work contains distinguishable but integrated building-system tasks. **Limit:** International vocational benchmark, not a mandated subgroup count. **Scope:** International/global benchmark.
- [WorldSkills International: Refrigeration and Air Conditioning WSOS 2024](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1674/) — Associated occupation and WSOS task sections for refrigeration/air-conditioning systems. **Supports:** Refrigeration and AC form a specialist building-climate systems practice distinct from conventional plumbing installation. **Limit:** Occupational benchmark; not global licensure guidance. **Scope:** International/global benchmark.
- [Skills England: Plumbing and Domestic Heating Technician ST0303](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeship-standards/st0303-v1-1) — Occupation summary, duties and options: plumbing/domestic heating, gas appliances, heat pumps/solar thermal, non-domestic plumbing. **Supports:** Corroborates plumbing and heating learning routes and option-specific branches. **Limit:** England-specific occupational standard; local law/certification must not be universalized. **Scope:** England, UK.
