# D109 — Metal Processing, Foundry and Casting Operations: specialization candidate review

**Disposition:** source_gap_closed_candidate_boundary_or_count_remains_provisional

**Parent scope basis:** Metal processing and foundry work is about shaping and finishing metal in industrial plants. Operators run casting, coating, drawing, grinding and abrasive blasting machines, load and remove workpieces, reject faulty ones and keep equipment running to quality standards. They are employed mainly by foundries, steel plants and metal manufacturing factories.

## Candidate student-facing areas

1. **Primary Metal Processing** — Operate industrial processes that prepare or produce metal stock from raw/intermediate material.
2. **Foundry, Moulding and Metal Casting** — Prepare moulds/cores and operate melting, pouring, casting and foundry processes.
3. **Metal Forming and Finishing** — Shape, draw, grind, coat or finish metal products in industrial plant processes.

## Decision

Three process families are candidate areas, but the evidence is not yet adequate to approve the three-way learner taxonomy: foundry/casting is well-defined, while primary processing and the breadth of forming/finishing require further curriculum/industry corroboration. Do not silently collapse the weakly evidenced branches into a confirmed count. Source-gap disposition recorded as `casting_and_process_families_evidenced_primary_processing_and_area_granularity_open`.

## Parent boundaries and exclusions

- Welding and structural fabrication are excluded (D097) unless the task is plant metal-forming within D109.
- Metal finishing/coating is included only as industrial plant processing, not building surface painting (D101).
- ILO's regional manufacturing competency model is regional corroboration, not global curriculum authority.
