## Candidate areas of specialization

- **Glass, Ceramics and Refractory Materials** — Material formulation, forming, thermal processing and testing of glass, ceramic and refractory products. Boundary: Materials/process route; excludes installing glass or tile in buildings.
- **Cement, Concrete and Mineral Building Materials** — Cement manufacture, concrete/material performance, aggregates and mineral-based construction products. Boundary: Material production/testing; excludes general construction delivery and structural engineering.
- **Industrial Boiler and Steam-Plant Operations** — Safe operation, inspection/maintenance coordination and process control of industrial power boilers and auxiliary steam-plant equipment. Boundary: Boiler operations only; nuclear, marine, locomotive and domestic/heating boiler rules have distinct scope and jurisdictional requirements.
