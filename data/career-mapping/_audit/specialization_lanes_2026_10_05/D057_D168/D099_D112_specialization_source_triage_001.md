# D099-D112 specialization source triage 001

Date: 2026-10-05  
Scope: source-screen and candidate-boundary triage for the 14 domains with no prior review artifact. This is not an academic sign-off, canonical taxonomy change, role mapping, or a claim that the source search is exhaustive.

## Method and evidence limits

The parent domain title and scope define the boundary. Proposed areas are distinguished by the main material/work output or production stage, not by current ISCO subgroup counts or role membership. WorldSkills International Occupational Standards (WSOS) are comparative, international vocational benchmarks; they help establish recognizable practices, not a globally mandated subgroup count. ILO ISCO-08 is a global occupation classification, useful to expose material/process families and classification collisions, but it is not itself a specialization taxonomy. Skills England documents are UK-specific standards and corroboration only; they do not set global entry requirements. No role-to-domain or role-to-specialization fit is decided here.

## Cross-catalogue safeguards

1. **No ISCO-to-specialization shortcut:** ISCO membership identifies occupational classification, not a learner-facing area. The current subgroup records are checked only to spot possible category contamination.
2. **No role inference:** A proposed specialization neither validates nor assigns any role. Role-to-domain fit remains independent, and role-to-specialization is deferred until the specialization set is fixed.
3. **Global scope:** WorldSkills/ILO evidence is international in scope but not an accrediting authority for a universal taxonomy. UK Skills England examples are explicitly national corroboration only.
4. **Count remains an internal synthesis:** Where sources identify practices but do not prescribe taxonomy, the proposed number is provisional; unresolved boundaries stay visible.
5. **No canonical files edited:** This triage changes only this lane-owned review artifact.
