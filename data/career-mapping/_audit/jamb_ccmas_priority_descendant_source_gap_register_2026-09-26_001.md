## Review boundary and next use

- This is a priority register, not a complete row-by-row acceptance audit or a repair authorization. It synthesizes what the cited checkpoints explicitly confirm; candidates and open evidence are not promoted to defects here.
- The checkpoints do not consistently enumerate each affected subgroup and role. Where a finding gives subgroup/role counts, those are copied from the checkpoint; elsewhere descendants are identified only by domain/path family, and the referenced checkpoints explicitly leave role-level validation open. Do not represent those unenumerated roles as individually confirmed defects.
- Exact JAMB awarder-cell evidence remains open for many rows across all three ranges. Parsed institution arrays and programme-name indexes are leads, not verified offering cells. Resolve the exact faculty/title/provider occurrence before applying or inheriting a curriculum scope.
- All three assigned ranges have saved checkpoints, but many tranche reports explicitly state they are not fully closed to the complete source + course/outcome + domain/subgroup/role standard. This synthesis does not change that status.
