## Task and relationship notes captured

- Experience design produces interaction flows, spatial layouts, content and prototype/evaluation criteria. Those become implementation inputs for XR application developers; iterative user testing feeds changes back into the design.
- XR software combines the application/engine layer with runtime APIs such as OpenXR or WebXR. Runtime availability, extensions, input profiles and device capabilities constrain what the application can implement; one API does not erase platform differences.
- Tracking and scene understanding produce poses, planes, anchors or scene representations consumed by placement and interaction logic. Tracking uncertainty, environmental feature quality, privacy permissions and latency constrain downstream experience behavior.
- Asset standards such as glTF/USD support scene/content transfer and composition; they do not guarantee that a target runtime supports every material, interaction, physics behavior or extension.
- Human-factors and accessibility evaluation spans design, implementation and hardware. Comfort, motion, alternative input/output, privacy, safety and accessibility findings feed iterative redesign; compliance status must be tied to the relevant published standard, platform and jurisdiction.
- Future device directions (transparent microdisplays, multifocal waveguides, smaller/lower-power optics, scene-aware spatial computing) remain research or roadmap evidence. Do not present a prototype or target specification as a generally available capability.
- Learning has several distinct forms: university courses teach cross-area foundations and evaluation; vendor learning paths teach implementation in a specific engine; Unity's VR Developer certification assesses a bounded Unity workflow. These are not interchangeable, and no universal XR professional license is established by this source batch.
- The final counter-search added two dependencies: XR software verification must test 6DOF/input, runtime/device compatibility, performance, privacy and safety; streamed XR moves rendering to a server and requires a paired compatible client, making network quality and latency part of the experience contract.
- Evaluation is multi-method: task-based usability, think-aloud/interviews and construct-matched questionnaires can complement presence, workload and sickness measures. Reviews find heterogeneous constructs/instruments and no universal evaluation protocol; choose measures by the question and document validity limits.
- IEEE P3791 and P7030 remain active projects, not published standards. IRIS-XR and multifocal waveguide work are research-stage; their aims belong in emerging/future horizons with those maturity labels.
- Nigerian context is not a no-hit: ABU reported discussion of a proposed VR learning facility; OAU describes an animation lab that supports XR media; Imisi 3D describes a Lagos XR creation lab; and Insightful3d lists a local XR developer vacancy. These establish specific examples of local activity, not a nationwide labor-market estimate or universal local curriculum. Hardware-engineering and XR human-factors labor-market subclaims remain unsupported by this local search scope.
