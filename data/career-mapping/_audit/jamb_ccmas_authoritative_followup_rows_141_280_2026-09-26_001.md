## Disposition for this tranche

- **Official JAMB offering-cell verified:** rows 145 (printed p. 632), 146–147 (printed p. 641), 149 (p. 502), 150 (p. 504); row 142 has only the visible beginning of its institution cell (p. 623), so only ACHIEVERS/BABCOCK are confirmed from this extract. Row 144's official p. 640 requirement text names ILORIN for this programme but does not verify its full cell. Rows 141, 143 and 148 remain institution-cell-unverified. All are Sciences-faculty contexts where the table header is visible.
- **Confirmed source-data discrepancy:** row 147's official awarder cell includes UNN while the parsed occurrence inventory omits it.
- **Confirmed curriculum-scope/control findings:** row 145 has no Economics courses selected; row 146 does include Mathematics courses (three) and is not a zero-Mathematics case. Row 143's Molecular Biology facet and row 147's Computer facet require adequacy decisions, not title-driven unions.
- **Confirmed link evidence defects:** row 141's D009 evidence course set does not match the ten-course saved scope; row 142, 143 and 146 active-link evidence uses narrow/substantially different course anchors from their actual saved scopes; rows 149–150 D141 Quantum Computing links are supported only by Seminar/Research Project titles. These are evidence integrity defects; other probable overreach candidates remain unconfirmed until course outcomes and destination tasks are directly compared.
- Rows 145–147 and 149–150 JAMB table cells were source-verified in official IBASS indexed extracts; rows 141, 143 and 148, and the remainder of row 142's cell, still need exact source-locator resolution. This is not closure of rows 141–150 or of the assigned 141–280 range.
