### 127 — forensic anthropology [optional], Criminologist

The U.S. [National Institute of Justice overview](https://nij.ojp.gov/topics/forensics/overview-forensic-anthropology) describes skeletal remains analysis for identification assistance. The [AAFS Academy Standards Board Standard 148](https://www.aafs.org/asb-standard/standard-personal-identification-forensic-anthropology) sets approaches to personal identification. These are useful global explanatory/technical references but not Nigerian casework standards. A possible expert output is a documented scientific opinion with method, uncertainty and chain of custody; no evidence shows the assigned criminologist performs it. No Nigeria-specific protocol was verified. Prerequisites: advanced osteology, recovery/documentation, validation and evidence handling. DNA, odontology and fingerprints are distinct complementary evidence streams, not automatic substitutes. No identification/cause-of-death certainty from skeletal indicators alone. Free entry-level practical training and exact role/source identity remain unresolved.

### 131 — genealogy [essential], Genealogist

The [Board for Certification of Genealogists](https://bcgcertification.org/ethics-standards/) provides professional U.S. standards including the Genealogical Proof Standard. [NARA Start Your Genealogy Research](https://www.archives.gov/research/genealogy/start-research) is a free, task-specific set of tutorials/classes and source-search strategies; it explicitly concerns U.S. records and advises using other repositories. Nigeria's [National Archives portal](https://nationalarchives.gov.ng/) gives local repository context, but searchable holdings and relevant family/vital-record access were not verified. Output: evidence-linked family history/proof argument with citations, conflict resolution and uncertainty—not an unsourced tree. Prerequisites: archives, source criticism, chronology, privacy. Complements: information sourcing, data inspection and history. Oral accounts/DNA can complement but cannot alone prove lineage. Nigeria-specific methodology and exact role task remain open.
