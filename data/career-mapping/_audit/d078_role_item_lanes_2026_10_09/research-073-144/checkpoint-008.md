### 143 — human physiology [optional], Thanatology Researcher

The [OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology 2e §1.5](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/1-5-homeostasis) is free, with learning objectives on homeostasis and negative/positive feedback. It explains how the body maintains internal conditions; it is educational biology, not clinical instruction. Nigeria context: [NOU OER catalogue](https://oer.nou.edu.ng/resources?page=8) lists NSS203 Human Physiology and NSC104 Human Physiology I; the [NSS203 course guide](https://oer.nou.edu.ng/sites/default/files/OER_Files/NSS203_cg.pdf) is local courseware, but a nursing curriculum does not prove a Thanatology Researcher task. A bounded possible output is a cited description of a biological process relevant to death/life-course research, if task evidence establishes relevance. Prerequisites: biology, organ systems and homeostasis. Complements: genomics, ethics and data quality. Anatomy (structure), physiology (function), and pathology/clinical medicine are distinct. Hard boundary: no D064 clinical, counselling, diagnostic, treatment, patient-facing or medical-authority claims; physiology alone does not establish social meaning or cause of death. Exact concept/task and role-specific Nigeria evidence remain unresolved.

### 144 — inspect data [essential], Genealogist

The [UK Data Service Learning Hub](https://ukdataservice.ac.uk/learning-hub/) offers free interactive modules for finding good-quality secondary data, understanding it and beginning analysis; its [module overview](https://ukdataservice.ac.uk/2021/11/23/introducing-our-data-skills-modules-free-interactive-training-from-the-uk-data-service/) describes the learning sequence. These teach data literacy in UK service/dataset context, not genealogy. [NARA genealogy tutorials](https://www.archives.gov/research/genealogy/start-research) support record discovery and source citation in U.S. federal holdings. The UK National Archives' [recording results video guide](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/video-guides/recording-research-results/) stresses backing conclusions with original sources. Nigeria's [National Archives portal](https://nationalarchives.gov.ng/) is a local starting point, but holdings/searchability/access were not verified. Candidate output: inspection log/catalogue of provenance, fields, missingness, inconsistencies, record context and decisions; genealogical conclusions should cite and corroborate source records. Prerequisites: provenance, record structure, source comparison and living-person privacy. Complements: information sourcing, genealogy and data quality. Index searches aid discovery but do not replace underlying records; absence from a database is not historical absence. Exact ESCO, task locator, Nigeria access conventions and one module demonstrating archival inspection remain unresolved.
