### 133 — geographic information systems [essential], Geographer

The [UN Integrated Geospatial Information Framework](https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/en/united-nations-integrated-geospatial-information-framework-un-igif), second edition, Parts 1–3, sets global strategy and country implementation pathways for geospatial information. It is not a software manual. Nigeria's [OSGoF mandate](https://osgof.gov.ng/introduction/) includes geospatial documentation and national metadata; its services include maps, coordinates and cadastral surveys. This is institutional/data context, not proof of this role's specific task. For free practical learning, the [QGIS Training Manual v3.44](https://docs.qgis.org/3.44/en/docs/training_manual/index.html) has exercises across GIS basics, vectors, rasters and mapping; it is not a credential or Nigerian coordinate standard. Plausible output: spatial layers with coordinate reference, metadata, analysis and caveats. Prerequisites: spatial concepts, formats, metadata, cartography/statistics. Complements: cartography, digital processing, geology. GIS is not synonymous with GPS/cartography. Scale, datum, uncertainty and licenses matter. Exact concept, role task and a plain-language independent explainer remain open.

### 135 — geological time scale [optional], Geographer

The [International Commission on Stratigraphy chart](https://stratigraphy.org/chart) is the formal reference; use the version/date printed on the chart because boundary ages can change. The [USGS major divisions explainer](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/geotime/divisions.html) is accessible explanatory material. Nigeria context: [NGSA geological mapping](https://ngsa.gov.ng/geological-mapping/) documents national rock-distribution mapping but does not prove this Geographer's task or a local unit interpretation. Possible output: place a geological unit/event in the time hierarchy and state evidence/uncertainty. Prerequisites: stratigraphy and relative/absolute dating. Complements: geology and GIS. Geological and human historical time are distinct; chart alone cannot date a local rock. Free USGS explainer plus formal chart; Nigeria-specific practice exercise and exact role task remain open.

These are normative/global frameworks, not a universal score. No Nigerian institution outcome is asserted. A scoped assessment should define criteria and produce evidence-linked findings, not a vague country label. Prerequisites: institutional analysis, measurement and context. Complements: policy, implementation, ethics. Governance is not identical to democracy/public administration. No Nigerian application evidence or hands-on course/task locator found.
