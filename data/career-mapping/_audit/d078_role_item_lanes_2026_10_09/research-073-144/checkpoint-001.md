### 73. bookkeeping regulations — optional — `D078:role:tax-policy-analyst`

- Concept: the phrase plausibly means jurisdiction-specific rules for accounting records, but the exact ESCO skill/knowledge node and whether the intended concept exists are unresolved. Do not conflate bookkeeping, financial reporting, audit and tax law.
- Task/output hypothesis: only for a verified tax-policy task concerning compliance costs or evidence quality, map record duties, responsible entity, retention/reporting rule and authority into a jurisdiction/date-bounded note. The task family itself does not name this duty.
- Prerequisites/complements: accounting-cycle basics, entity/jurisdiction/date; tax administration, financial reporting standards and records management. A tax-outcomes study can proceed without claiming bookkeeping rules; compliance determinations need an appropriate accountant/legal authority.
- Global evidence: [IFRS Standards jurisdiction profiles](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/jurisdictions/pdf-profiles/nigeria-ifrs-profile.pdf), Nigeria profile/adoption tables, support the separate distinction between financial-reporting standards and recordkeeping. Limits: IFRS does not define bookkeeping procedures.
- Nigeria context: the IFRS profile says standards apply to specified public-interest entities and records phased SME adoption. This does not establish a general bookkeeping rule. Current Nigerian recordkeeping law and exact entity applicability remain unresolved.
- Learning: no free, task-specific link verified. **Open gates:** ESCO node; explicit role-task evidence; Nigerian primary rule; suitable free course.

### 74. budgetary principles — optional — `D078:role:tax-policy-analyst`

- Concept: public-budgeting versus private-budgeting sense and ESCO identity/type/version remain unresolved.
- Task/output hypothesis: review a fiscal proposal against completeness, authorization, credible assumptions, fiscal risks and reporting; output a cited review matrix. Prerequisites: public revenue/expenditure, budget cycle, macro-fiscal assumptions. Complements fiscal transparency, execution monitoring and tax-revenue analysis; for a firm budget, use private-sector methods instead.
- Global evidence: [IMF Fiscal Transparency Code overview](https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-transparency), definition and four pillars, plus [IMF Pillar II](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9781484331859/ch03.xml), Principle 2.1.1 and credibility dimension 2.4, support completeness, authorization and credible forecasts. Limits: public-sector transparency framework, not a universal role standard.
- Nigeria context: [Budget Office of the Federation](https://budgetoffice.gov.ng/index.php), About/mission and MTEF, MTSS, BIR descriptions, gives federal examples. It cannot be generalized to states/private organizations or prove this role uses the principles.
- Learning: IMF reference material is free but no learner lesson/course path was verified. **Open gates:** exact ESCO concept, role task, state-level context, lesson-level resource.

### 75. business analytics — optional — `D078:role:business-economics-researcher`

- Concept: practice/field phrase; exact ESCO concept and type unresolved. Distinguish business data analytics from business analysis and business intelligence.
- Task/output hypothesis: frame a business question, source and assess data, analyze, interpret, communicate and state uncertainty; output a reproducible chart/table and decision note. Prerequisites: question framing, business context, data quality and descriptive statistics. Complements economics, data processing and conditional forecasting. Alternatives include qualitative case work or accounting analysis when structured data cannot answer the question.
- Global evidence: [IIBA Business Data Analytics](https://www.iiba.org/career-resources/business-analysis-specialization/business-data-analytics/), “What is Business Data Analytics?” and six practice domains, supports the workflow and its outputs. [IBM explainer](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/business-analytics), definition and BA/BI distinction, is an accessible independent explanation but has vendor framing. Neither establishes causality or a role requirement.
- Nigeria context: no Nigeria-specific business-economics analytics practice or requirement verified in this batch. Do not replace missing local evidence with global professional-body descriptions.
- Credential: IIBA-CBDA is an issuer-defined voluntary certification; exam/eligibility details and relevance to the occurrence were not checked. Free IIBA paper is an explanation, not a verified hands-on course. **Open gates:** ESCO identity; role task; Nigerian example; free lesson; credential details if relevant.

### 76. cadastral taxation — essential — `D078:role:tax-policy-analyst`

- Concept: ambiguous composite, possibly property taxation using parcel/cadastre data, cadastral valuation or cadastral survey. ESCO identity/type unresolved.
- Task/output hypothesis: for a verified property-tax study, link parcel attributes/boundaries and valuation basis to taxable units, assess register coverage and create a provenance-labelled tax-base map/table. Prerequisites: land/tenure, valuation, cadastral/GIS data and local tax law. Complements GIS, land administration and tax-incidence analysis. Administrative tax-register analysis can proceed without performing surveys.
- Global evidence: [HMRC property-value guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/annual-tax-on-enveloped-dwellings-pre-return-banding-checks), valuation dates and basis, supports that tax calculations may depend on valuation. Limits: UK ATED example only; not transferable law.
- Nigeria context: [OSGOF cadastral and legal surveys](https://osgof.gov.ng/cadastral-legal-surveys/), functions i–v, establishes federal cadastral surveys and cadastral GIS as local geospatial context. It does not establish property-tax rules or state valuation practice.
- Learning: QGIS [training manual](https://docs.qgis.org/3.44/en/docs/training_manual/index.html), map/data lessons, supports only GIS basics, not the composite label. **Open gates:** exact concept; current state/local tax law; role task; combined topic-specific learning resource.

### 78. cartography — optional — `D078:role:geographer`

- Concept: cartographic practice/discipline; exact ESCO URI/type/version unresolved.

A table/plot can be preferable where geography is irrelevant.

Limits: the guide is not a full production standard; QGIS instruction is not professional survey authorization.
- Nigeria context: [NPC Enumeration Area Demarcation](https://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/EAD), EAD/cartography description, and [OSGOF](https://osgof.gov.ng/cadastral-legal-surveys/), cadastral GIS functions, show bounded census and land-record uses. They do not prove this geographer’s task or national occupational prevalence.
- Learning status: candidate; check current access, version and cartographic-design lesson fit. **Open gates:** exact ESCO node and role task.

- Concept: material ambiguity. [Cornell Wex civil law](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/civil_law), definition, explains both private-rights law and a codified legal-system tradition; it is U.S.-oriented. Exact intended sense/ESCO node unresolved.
- Task/output hypothesis: only if economic advice analyzes a legal mechanism, identify jurisdiction/date and primary instrument, then write a bounded note on how rights/obligations may affect incentives. Prerequisites: jurisdiction, legal hierarchy, economic question and primary-source checking. Complements institutional/regulatory analysis. Legal interpretation should be referred to qualified counsel; civil law is not synonymous with all non-criminal law in every context.
- Nigeria context: no local legal source linked to this occurrence was verified; no Nigerian rule asserted. Nigeria’s federal/state/customary/religious legal dimensions require case-specific identification.
- Learning: Cornell Wex is a free reference, not Nigerian law or a task-specific course. **Open gates:** intended ESCO sense/type, task source, primary Nigerian authority if applicable, suitable learning resource.
