## QA rules and result meanings

The audit checked the item-identity state, task/output claim, cited evidence and locator, independent explanation, separately stated Nigeria context, and free-learning link in each source-batch row. The cited URLs were opened in grouped checks where possible. A redirect was followed and judged at its destination; a 403, browser/crawler error, or metadata-only page is **inconclusive**, not a confirmed dead link. Only an explicit 404 is called a dead link here. Access date is the environment date, 2026-10-09.

- **Support/bounded:** source content or title and stated locator fit the narrow claim, with the record's limits retained.
- **Partial:** source is relevant but does not support the whole wording, exact task, jurisdiction, or claimed locational detail.
- **Unverified:** URL could not be read or the locator was not confirmed; keep the claim provisional.
- **Mismatch:** learning resource does not teach the task named in the pair; recommend explicit `none-found` rather than presenting it as task-specific.

All 72 records correctly keep the exact ESCO linked-item URI unresolved. An ESCO occupation URI and label occurrence are not an exact item identity or proof of a task. The cited ESCO API page documents URI identity and lists versions through v1.2.0, whereas the lane refers to an occupation snapshot v1.2.1. Preserve the snapshot claim as provenance but do not treat the API documentation as confirmation of that version or invent an item URI. Each task/output remains candidate use inferred from role profile context, not independently proven duty.
