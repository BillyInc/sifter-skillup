## Main findings

1. **Sports management (D071):** athletic director, sports coach, sports scout
   and sports official/referee have direct global task evidence. Nigeria’s
   sports ministry strategy confirms an institutional sports-development
   ecosystem, but it is not used as vacancy or title proof.
2. **Fashion/textiles (D075):** fashion designer, fabric/apparel patternmaker
   and tailor/dressmaker/custom sewer are established occupations. The Nigerian
   Fashion Council and FADAN provide local sector/professional context. The
   catalogue already has equivalent records in D106/D160, so these are route
   reuse candidates, not duplicates.
3. **Maritime/naval operations (D076):** IMO STCW capacities provide exact
   source-backed candidates for master, chief mate, deck officer, chief engineer
   officer, second engineer officer, assistant engineer officer,
   electro-technical officer and able seafarer deck. NIMASA separately supports
   Nigerian seafarer certification, labour and manning context.
4. **Customs/trade (D079):** WCO supports customs-broker functions globally;
   Nigeria Customs supports local customs-agent licensing and statutory context.
   “Customs agent” remains review-only because legal equivalence with customs
   broker is jurisdiction-dependent.
5. **Interior/spatial design (D080):** interior designer is an established
   occupation already present elsewhere and should be reused. Set/exhibit
   designer is source-backed but remains review-only until the D080 boundary
   confirms whether exhibition/set work belongs in that domain.
6. **Beauty and finishing trades:** O*NET and Nigeria’s NBTE/CORBON standards
   support skincare, makeup, nail-care, painting and wall-covering work. Existing
   D088/D101 specializations are reused where possible; no duplicate identity is
   proposed.

## Nigerian evidence boundary

professional bodies, occupational standards or facility requirements. They do
not automatically establish nationwide demand, employer vacancies or a globally
standardized role title. Those claims remain separate data fields for a later
market-evidence pass.
