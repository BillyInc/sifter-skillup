## Evidence newly checked

Access date for all URLs below: **2026-09-26**.

1. **JAMB identity and provider context.** The official JAMB Degree Agriculture brochure lists **Pasture and Range Management — FUNAAB** in the course/degree-awarding-institution table (printed p. 106). Its programme index separately lists Pasture & Range Management as item 53 (printed p. 75). This confirms the specific FUNAAB offering used for the bounded comparison; it does not establish a curriculum for other institutions or offerings.
   - [JAMB Degree Agriculture brochure](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/brochure-degree-agriculture.pdf), printed pp. 75, 106; search-indexed table text for the row reads “PASTURE AND RANGE MANAGEMENT — FUNAAB.”

2. **National CCMAS baseline.** NUC Agriculture CCMAS presents ANS 409, *Pasture and Range Production and Management*, in the Animal Science course table (printed p. 161) and gives its outcomes/content on printed p. 183. The outcomes explicitly cover distinguishing pasture/forage; identifying indigenous and introduced pasture/forage species; establishing and maintaining pastureland and fodder banks; germplasm collection/preservation/storage; and understanding range management. Contents also name grasses/legumes/shrubs, seed production, grazing systems, forage conservation, and dry-season feeds. The CCMAS therefore supports a substantial foundational learning block; the defect is not that ANS 409 is irrelevant.
   - [NUC Agriculture CCMAS](https://www.nuc.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Agriculture-CCMAS.pdf), printed p. 161 (course table), printed p. 183 (ANS 409 outcomes/content; PDF text lines 7115–7132).
   - The same national CCMAS has a separate ANS 506, *Pasture and Range Production and Management*, in a course table at printed p. 64, with outcomes/content at printed pp. 94–95. Its outcomes likewise cover species, pasture establishment, fodder banks, and range management. This corroborates the national curriculum’s treatment of these competencies, but it is not an additional distinct course to count automatically in the FUNAAB offering.

3. **FUNAAB’s provider-specific undergraduate course sequence.** FUNAAB’s official Pasture and Range Management course list identifies six courses: PRM 201 *Introduction to Forage Science*; PRM 501 *Forage and Seed Production Technologies*; PRM 502 *Forage Quality and Conservation*; PRM 503 *Techniques in Pasture Management*; PRM 504 *Management of Grasslands*; and PRM 505 *Principles of Pasture and Range Sciences*. This is provider-specific evidence of multiple named curriculum components beyond the single course currently selected in the JAMB scope.
   - [FUNAAB Pasture and Range Management courses](https://funaab.edu.ng/pasture-and-range-management-courses/), course list lines 149–155. Page says last updated 2011; treat as official but dated evidence, not proof of the current session’s complete requirements.
   - FUNAAB’s [PRM 201 course synopsis](https://funaab.edu.ng/prm-201-course-synopsis/), lines 145–170, identifies PRM 201 as a 2-unit undergraduate-area course coordinated from the Pasture & Range Management office, with topics including pasture’s role in animal/crop systems, grasses/legumes/browse plants, classification, soil nutrients, terminology, and production prospects.
   - The linked [PRM 201 course note](https://funaab.edu.ng/funaab-ocw/opencourseware/Introduction%20to%20Forage%20Science.pdf), PDF printed p. 1, repeats the code/title and describes the same subject matter. It is item-specific course evidence, but only for PRM 201.
   - FUNAAB’s [2019–2022 University Calendar](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/second.pdf) identifies Pasture and Range Science as an option and lists PRM 302 *Principles of Pasture and Range Management* and PRM 304 *Tropical Pasture Production* (printed pp. 136–137); it also lists FPY 413 *Pasture Production and Management Techniques* (printed p. 138). This independently supports a broader, option-specific sequence, although the calendar is dated and does not by itself prove current offering requirements.

## Finding and recommendation

**Newly established:** the row-414/FUNAAB case has an authoritative provider-specific basis for more than the one selected ANS 409 course. FUNAAB publishes a pasture/range undergraduate course list and course synopsis, and its calendar identifies option-specific pasture courses. The provider context is confirmed by JAMB’s course/awarder table. Thus “one course is all the curriculum evidence available” is false for this specific FUNAAB occurrence.

**Bounded fix recommendation (not applied):** repair the FUNAAB occurrence as a provider-specific curriculum scope, retaining NUC ANS 409 as the CCMAS-aligned baseline and adding only FUNAAB courses whose undergraduate status, current applicability, and course-level source material can be verified. At minimum, PRM 201 is supported by an item-specific synopsis/note; PRM 302/304 and FPY 413 are supported by the official historical calendar. The PRM 501–505 course list establishes titles, but not enough outcomes to write detailed student learning claims for each. Do not combine ANS 506 into this occurrence as an extra course solely because its title resembles ANS 409; the NUC course table places it in a distinct curriculum option, and no evidence checked here shows it is separately taken in the FUNAAB programme.

**Not yet established / remaining gap:**

- No current FUNAAB handbook or current-session programme specification was found in this focused pass to prove that the 2011 course list and 2019–2022 calendar remain the active required/elective sequence.
- Course-level outcomes or syllabi were located only for PRM 201 and NUC ANS 409; the official FUNAAB course list gives titles for PRM 501–505, not their current detailed outcomes.
- JAMB confirms FUNAAB as the named awarder, but this check does not establish that the same scope applies to any other institution or faculty occurrence.
- No shared mappings, source records, or generated scopes were changed. The scope remains pending until current provider-specific evidence is reconciled and a controlled data repair is authorized.
