- **Source status S:** prior row checkpoint records the identity/faculty/offering context; exact awarder-cell closure for this particular row is stated there. The range is not fully source-closed, and parsed institution arrays are not source verification.

- **Defect status D:** consult the cited checkpoint for this row’s explicit `confirmed defect` versus `lead / unresolved` classification. This index does not upgrade leads or re-adjudicate claims.
- **Open question Q:** row-specific unresolved source/curriculum questions remain in the cited checkpoint. Use its official URL, printed page/table/course locator and access date; this inventory adds no new internet research.
- `Full/0` means full single-component inheritance and zero row-specific selected-course rows, not no curriculum. `External/n` means current external-scope record with n courses, not an automatic claim that external research is exhaustive. `Combined/0` means no selected course rows currently establish the declared combination.
