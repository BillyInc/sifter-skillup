## Recommended learning path

Organize the path around learner choices and the ownership of each learning item:

1. **Programme context:** show the selected programme record as context, not an extra checklist.
2. **Required shared D078 foundation:** complete the three shared items in this recommended teaching order: frame a researchable social question and choose a fitting design; evaluate evidence and its limits; communicate findings for the intended audience. They are required for every D078 area and role route. This is a transferable evidence-literacy baseline; it does not mean every occupation conducts a standalone formal study or needs every method.
3. **Selectable domain items:** after the shared foundations, let the learner choose one or more useful domain-owned items. Show which areas, roles and task contexts each item supports, why it may give the learner an edge, and the next recommended item within that strand. Methods and tools remain conditional choices.
4. **Selected area items:** when a learner chooses one or more areas, inherit the domain layer and present area-owned items linked to the area's evidenced task families. Keep them separate from domain-owned items that merely support the area.
5. **Optional role items:** after area selection, a learner may add role-owned items tied to that role's task context. Role selection does not make unrelated domain or area items compulsory.

A task is evidence for why a learning item is useful; it is not a required student selection or a gate into the learning path. Learners select items, one or more at a time. For the items they choose, show an advisory order and the next item so they do not have to guess. Useful items remain skippable; only the three shared foundations are compulsory across D078.
