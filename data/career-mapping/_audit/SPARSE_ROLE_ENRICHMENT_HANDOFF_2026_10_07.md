## Candidate-level rule

## Product disclaimer

This is a source-backed research and review ledger, not a claim that these are the only occupations available, that every candidate is a Nigerian vacancy, or that every candidate has been canonically activated. More roles can be added after later labour-demand research and review; users should not interpret the ten-role planning floor as an exhaustive occupational universe.
