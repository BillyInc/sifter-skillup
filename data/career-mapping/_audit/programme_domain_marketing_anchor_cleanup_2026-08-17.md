# CCMAS programme → Marketing/Brand anchor cleanup

Audit date: 2026-08-17

The Marketing/Brand definition requires market research, brands, customers, campaigns, advertising, sales channels, promotion, or audience/communication strategy. Generic business, communication, or entrepreneurship wording is not enough for a Functional link.

Applied result:

- 31 unsupported D030 links removed.
- 5 media/business/creative links demoted to Adjacent where a bridge exists but marketing is not core.
- Explicit Marketing, Advertising, Strategic Communication, and other course-backed links were retained.

D030 now has 41 programme links. The current overall mapping has 236 programme records and 2,088 links:

| Tier | Links |
|---|---:|
| Direct | 226 |
| Functional | 1,743 |
| Adjacent | 119 |

The remaining D030 links are still subject to final semantic review, but each retained link has a stronger business/media/market anchor than the removed generic links.
