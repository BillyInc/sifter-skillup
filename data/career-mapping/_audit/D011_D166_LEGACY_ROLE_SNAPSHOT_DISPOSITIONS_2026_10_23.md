## Disposition rule

A title is not retained merely because the general occupation operates in finance, engineering, manufacturing, or autonomous technology. Keep it only where source-backed tasks fit the domain and at least one existing area. Use explicit task conditions for broad occupations. Do not turn product-adjacency or sector employment into an area route.
