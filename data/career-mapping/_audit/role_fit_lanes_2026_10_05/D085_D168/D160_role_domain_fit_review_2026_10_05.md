# D160 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

**Scope:** Fashion styling and image consulting shape how people, products and brands look. Personal and fashion stylists put outfits together for clients, make-up artists and hair designers create complete looks, and visual merchandisers design shop displays and negotiate with suppliers. They work with fashion brands, retail stores, media productions and private clients.
