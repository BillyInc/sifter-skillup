# D110 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Chemical and rubber processing involves running plants that convert raw materials into products such as soap, lime and other chemicals. Operators control distillation units, kilns, blenders and soap-making machines, adjust process settings, troubleshoot faults and follow strict safety rules with protective equipment. Common employers are chemical plants, soap factories and other process industries.
