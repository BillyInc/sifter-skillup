# D162 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

**Scope:** Immigration law and asylum practice deal with the legal side of people moving between countries. Immigration lawyers and human rights officers represent migrants and asylum seekers, while advisers, clerks and legal assistants compile documents, conduct legal research and manage casework. Law firms, government agencies and charities are the main employers.
