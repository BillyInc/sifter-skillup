# D140 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Robotics engineering designs machines that sense, move and act with little human control. Robotics, automation and controls engineers develop the mechanical parts, control systems and software that make robots work, relying on technical drawings and mathematics. They typically work for manufacturing companies, engineering firms and research organisations.
