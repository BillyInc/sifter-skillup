# D107 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Leather technology and shoemaking covers turning hides into leather and making footwear, bags and other leather goods. Tanners process hides; makers and machine operators cut, stitch, last and finish shoes, including bespoke and orthopaedic footwear; technicians test quality while designers and managers develop products and run production. Employers include tanneries, footwear factories, workshops and leather goods shops.
