## Audit notes

- All exact D128 role keys and task descriptions were checked against the lane queue.
- The parent scope is broad: it includes vulnerable adults, children/families, community work, elderly residential care, public housing, youth services and social-service management. Each reviewed task has a concrete connection to one of these elements.
- Hospital setting does not by itself negate social-work fit where the actual task is psychosocial support, advocacy and care coordination.
- This is a taxonomy-fit audit, not a professional licensing, qualification or service-quality determination.
- No role-to-specialization assignment, student-visibility decision or canonical taxonomy/mapping change is made.
