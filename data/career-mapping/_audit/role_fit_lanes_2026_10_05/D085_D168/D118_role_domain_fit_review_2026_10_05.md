# D118 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Crop production and horticulture is the growing of food crops, fruit, vegetables and garden plants. Managers and supervisors plan planting, fertilisation and pest control; workers pick produce, apply crop protection and operate vineyard machinery; inspectors check that farms follow agricultural and environmental rules. Farms, plantations, nurseries and agricultural agencies provide most of the work.
