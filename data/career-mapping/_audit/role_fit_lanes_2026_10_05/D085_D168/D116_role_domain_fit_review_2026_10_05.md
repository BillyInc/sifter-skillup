# D116 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Heavy equipment and earthmoving is the operation of cranes, graders, rollers, dredgers and similar machines. Operators lift loads with tower and mobile cranes, level and compact ground for roads, keep their machines in good condition and follow construction safety procedures. Construction firms, ports and plant hire companies are typical employers.
