# D103 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Jewellery making and goldsmithing is the craft of shaping precious metals and stones into rings, chains and other ornaments. Goldsmiths, jewellers, engravers, stone cutters and setters create new pieces, mount and adjust stones, and repair damaged items using specialist equipment. Most practitioners work in small workshops, jewellery shops or craft studios.
