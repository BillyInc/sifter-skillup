# D120 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Fisheries and aquaculture covers catching fish and farming aquatic stock. Managers run hatcheries and fish farms, technicians monitor fish health and mortality, and workers harvest stock, dive, moor cages and crew fishing boats, all within biosecurity and fisheries law. Fish farms, hatcheries and fishing companies are the main employers.
