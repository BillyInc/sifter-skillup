# D127 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Early childhood education and nursery work is the care and teaching of young children before primary school. Childcare workers and nannies play with children, help them develop personal skills and handle everyday problems, while coordinators and head teachers run nurseries and lead early-years teaching. Nursery schools, day-care centres and private homes are common workplaces.
