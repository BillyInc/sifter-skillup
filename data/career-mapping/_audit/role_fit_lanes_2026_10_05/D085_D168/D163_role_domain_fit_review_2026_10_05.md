# D163 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

**Scope:** Intellectual property law protects inventions, brand names and creative works. Patent and trademark attorneys prepare applications, present legal arguments and protect their clients' interests, while paralegals and administrators manage case files and paperwork. Most work is found in law firms, large companies and government registries, and practising as a lawyer requires being called to the Bar.
