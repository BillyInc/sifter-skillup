# D087 role-to-parent-domain fit review

Date: 2026-10-05  
Domain: Hairdressing, Barbering and Hair Technology  
Scope: role-to-parent-domain fit only. No subgroup/specialization fit, names, count, or assignments are assessed.

## Method and sources

Compared the exact task text attached to each stored ESCO occupation URI with the parent domain's explicit hair-service scope. ISCO/subgroup membership and title similarity were not used as fit evidence. ESCO's own documentation describes its occupation profiles as containing descriptions, scope notes, definitions, and skills/competences. The role URI pages could not be re-fetched by the web client in this pass, so the task text is taken from the checked-in ESCO profile cache and exact URIs are retained for direct verification.

Corroborating references: [ESCO occupation profiles](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/classification/occupation-main); [U.S. O*NET profile for hairdressers, hairstylists and cosmetologists](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/39-5012.00); [HABIA, the UK hair and beauty sector's government-recognised standards-setting organisation](https://habia.org/). O*NET and HABIA are national references, used only to corroborate that hair cutting, styling, scalp/hair treatments, and performance hair/wig work are recognized hair-sector tasks—not as global taxonomic authority.

**5 mappings reviewed: 5 supported, 0 conditional, 0 unsupported, 0 unresolved.** Decisions are based on explicit hair-service tasks, not the presence of an ISCO or subgroup link. This pass changes no canonical data and makes no claim that the five roles cover every hair-sector occupation.

External academic sign-off: not obtained. The O*NET and HABIA references are US- and UK-specific respectively; neither is presented as global authority. No role-to-specialization fit is included.
