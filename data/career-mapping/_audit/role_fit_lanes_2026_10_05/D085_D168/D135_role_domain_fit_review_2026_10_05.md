# D135 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Nuclear engineering and radiation science applies physics to nuclear energy and radiation. Nuclear engineers and reactor operators run and decommission reactors, health and medical physicists keep people safe from radiation and support radiotherapy, and radiochemists work in laboratories using mathematical modelling and spectroscopy. Power stations, hospitals, research institutes and regulators employ them.
