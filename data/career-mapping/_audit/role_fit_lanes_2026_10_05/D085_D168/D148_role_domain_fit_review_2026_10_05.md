Scope: Offshore and subsea work centres on producing oil and gas at sea. Drilling and equipment engineers design wells, subsea structures and production systems, while operators run refineries, gas plants and remotely operated underwater vehicles. Oil and gas companies and offshore platforms are the usual workplaces, and safety training is required before anyone works offshore.
