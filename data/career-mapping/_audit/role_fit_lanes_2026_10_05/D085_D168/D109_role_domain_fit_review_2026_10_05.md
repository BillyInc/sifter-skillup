# D109 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Metal processing and foundry work is about shaping and finishing metal in industrial plants. Operators run casting, coating, drawing, grinding and abrasive blasting machines, load and remove workpieces, reject faulty ones and keep equipment running to quality standards. They are employed mainly by foundries, steel plants and metal manufacturing factories.
