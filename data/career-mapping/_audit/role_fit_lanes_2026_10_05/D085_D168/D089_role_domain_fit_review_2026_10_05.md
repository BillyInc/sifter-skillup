This is a US reference, not global taxonomy or universal licensing guidance.

- [ESCO occupation profiles](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/classification/occupation-main): exact URI locators are shown per role. Task text is from the checked-in profile cache; ESCO defines occupation profiles but does not determine Sifter domain fit.
