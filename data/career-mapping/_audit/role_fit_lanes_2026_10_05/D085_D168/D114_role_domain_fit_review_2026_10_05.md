# D114 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Assembly and packaging is the factory work of putting products together and preparing them for sale. Assemblers build motorcycles, aircraft engines, medical devices and electronics by reading assembly drawings and aligning components, while other operators run cartoning, labelling and filling machines. This work happens in factories and assembly plants across many industries.

## Audit notes

- All 43 exact D114 role names were reconciled against the queue. The WEF trend record remains unresolved because it has no occupation tasks; no tasks were invented.
- The scope intentionally spans many industries; medical, aircraft, vehicle, electrical, electronic, furniture, rubber and wood-product roles fit when their exact task assembles components/products.
- Machining/programming, composite fabrication, production tooling and semiconductor fabrication are distinguished from assembly where the recorded task does not establish assembly work.
- No role-to-specialization assignment, student-visibility decision or canonical taxonomy/mapping change is made.
