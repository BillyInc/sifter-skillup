# D124 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Community health extension provides frontline basic health care close to where people live. Practitioners give first aid, assist nurses with patient care, collect blood samples, fit spectacles as opticians or optical technicians, sterilise equipment and support patients at home or as hospital porters. Health centres, hospitals and home-care services are the usual workplaces.
