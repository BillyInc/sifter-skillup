# D146 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Building services engineering deals with the mechanical and electrical systems that make buildings work, such as lighting, air conditioning, fire protection and automation. Engineers in this field design these systems from drawings, maintain them, and increasingly connect them to smart controls. Construction firms and engineering consultancies are the main employers.
