# D104 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Pottery, ceramics and glass covers the craft of forming clay, porcelain and glass into useful and decorative objects. Practitioners such as ceramic painters, glass-blowers, pottery casters, brick moulders and engravers mould, cast, blow, paint and engrave their pieces, often working from their own sketches. They typically work in potteries, glassworks, brickyards and craft studios.
