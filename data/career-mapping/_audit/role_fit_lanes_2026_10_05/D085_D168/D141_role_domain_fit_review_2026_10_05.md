## Audit notes

- All 19 exact D141 role keys and task descriptions were checked. Because each record describes quantum-specific work or a quantum-sector enabling function and the parent scope includes computing, hardware, sensors/devices and software, all fit the domain at the broad domain level.
- This is a fit audit, not occupational-title validation. Titles such as core innovator and practitioner are broad and should not be presented as globally standardized titles without a separate role-title evidence review.
- Quantum cryptography/QKD is distinguished from post-quantum cryptography; no claim is made that they are interchangeable.
- No role-to-specialization assignment, student-path recommendation or canonical mapping/taxonomy change is made.
