Museums, galleries, libraries and cultural institutions provide most of the work.

## Sources and limitations

- **QUEUE_TASK_PROFILE — Checked-in role/domain audit queue:** [D085_D168_role_fit_queue_2026_10_05.json](D085_D168_role_fit_queue_2026_10_05.json). Exact D152 role_key, parent scope and stored task descriptions. Supports: The specific recorded duties evaluated for each D152 mapping. Limit: This is the record under audit, not an independent verification of an occupation's complete scope.
- **ICOM_MUSEUM_DEFINITION — International Council of Museums (ICOM):** [https://icom.museum/en/news/icom-approves-a-new-museum-definition/](https://icom.museum/en/news/icom-approves-a-new-museum-definition/). ICOM-approved 2022 museum definition: museums research, collect, conserve, interpret and exhibit tangible and intangible heritage; they also support education and knowledge-sharing. Supports: The parent-domain boundary around museum collections, conservation, interpretation, exhibitions, research and public access. Limit: A global professional definition, not an occupational licensing rule or proof that every cultural venue is a museum.
- **UNESCO_MUSEUMS_RECOMMENDATION — UNESCO:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-concerning-protection-and-promotion-museums-and-collections-their-diversity-and-their](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-concerning-protection-and-promotion-museums-and-collections-their-diversity-and-their). 2015 Recommendation, especially functional policies §§24–26: preservation, research, education, communication, inventories and digitization of collections. Supports: Internationally agreed museum/collection functions and the distinction between heritage collection work and generic cultural programming. Limit: An international recommendation implemented through jurisdiction-specific law and policy; it does not define local job titles or authority.
- **ICA_ARCHIVIST_ROLE — International Council on Archives (ICA):** [https://www.ica.org/discover-archives/what-is-an-archivist/](https://www.ica.org/discover-archives/what-is-an-archivist/). What is an archivist?: collecting, managing, preserving records and archives, maintaining provenance/original order, and enabling long-term access. Supports: The direct archival-records portion of the domain and the archivist/cultural-archive-manager mappings. Limit: Global professional guidance; local archival laws, qualifications and title conventions vary.
- **ICOM_CONSERVATION_SCOPE — ICOM Committee for Conservation (ICOM-CC):** [https://icom.museum/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/1_ICOM-CC.pdf](https://icom.museum/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/1_ICOM-CC.pdf). Committee remit and working groups: conservation specialists safeguard cultural heritage and collections through conservation practice and research. Supports: Museum-object/cultural-heritage conservation as distinct from the queue's environmental-resource-management task profile for 'conservation scientist'. Limit: The committee describes conservation of cultural heritage; it does not establish that an identically named natural-resources occupation performs museum conservation.
- **ICOM_CODE_COLLECTIONS — International Council of Museums (ICOM):** [https://icom.museum/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/ICOM-code-En-web.pdf](https://icom.museum/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/ICOM-code-En-web.pdf). ICOM Code of Ethics for Museums, §§2.21–2.24: collections protection, preventive conservation, condition monitoring, conservation/restoration and documentation. Supports: Collection-care, registration and conservation duties within museums. Limit: Professional ethical standards, not a universal legal mandate or a substitute for task evidence.
- **ICOMOS_ARCHAEOLOGICAL_HERITAGE — International Council on Monuments and Sites (ICOMOS), ICAHM:** [https://icahm.icomos.org/](https://icahm.icomos.org/). ICOMOS International Scientific Committee on Archaeological Heritage Management: standards and best practices for archaeological research and cultural-resource management; links to the 1990 Charter for Protection and Management of Archaeological Heritage. Supports: The archaeology portion of the parent scope and a separately recorded potential role-coverage gap. Limit: International guidance on archaeological heritage management; it does not validate any absent role mapping or one country's permitting rules.
