# D112 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Wood processing and pulp work converts logs into sawn timber, veneer, pellets, pulp and paper. Machine operators run saws, debarkers, planers, bleachers and paper machines, perform test runs, troubleshoot faults and work safely in protective gear, while supervisors oversee production lines. Sawmills, paper mills and wood products factories are the main employers.
