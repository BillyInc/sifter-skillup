# D102 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Carpentry and wood technology is the craft of shaping wood into buildings, furniture and everyday objects. Carpenters build frames and install doors, windows and staircases, machine operators shape and dry timber, furniture makers craft tables and chairs, and artisans handcraft baskets, carvings and toys. Construction firms, furniture workshops and joinery factories are the usual employers.
