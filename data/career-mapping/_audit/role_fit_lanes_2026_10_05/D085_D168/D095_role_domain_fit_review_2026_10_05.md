# D095 role-to-parent-domain fit review

Audit date: 2026-10-05  
Scope: role tasks against **Motor Vehicle Mechanics and Repairs** only. No subgroup or specialization fit is assessed; no canonical mapping is changed.

## Result

Reviewed **20/20** mappings: **10 supported, 1 conditional, 9 unsupported for this parent domain, 0 unresolved**.

The parent describes servicing engines, vehicle electrical systems, vehicle restoration/bodywork, workshop maintenance, and expressly includes bicycles. Repair, maintenance, roadside-service, tyre-service, overhaul, and restoration tasks fit that scope.

The **nine unsupported** task links are motor vehicle aftersales manager (sales/contracts), motor vehicle assembler, assembly inspector, assembly supervisor, motor vehicle body assembler, engine assembler, engine inspector, engine tester, and parts assembler. Their queue descriptions concern sales or production/assembly and manufacturing quality checks, not servicing vehicles already in use. This is a scope decision for these mappings only; it does not delete those occupations or select another domain.

**Coachbuilder** is conditional: the stored profile combines body fabrication/manufacture with vehicle-body work, while the parent includes restoring/refurbishing bodywork but does not clearly delimit production coachbuilding.

## Sources and limits

- BLS describes automotive service technicians as inspecting, maintaining, diagnosing, and repairing vehicles. [BLS: Automotive Service Technicians and Mechanics](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/automotive-service-technicians-and-mechanics.htm)
- BLS describes body/glass repair as restoring, refinishing, and replacing vehicle bodies, frames, and glass. [BLS: Automotive Body and Glass Repairers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/automotive-body-and-glass-repairers.htm)
- BLS distinguishes production assembly and manufacturing quality checks from repair/service work. [BLS: Assemblers and Fabricators](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/assemblers-and-fabricators.htm)
- BLS describes diesel technicians as inspecting, repairing, or overhauling diesel engines. [BLS: Diesel Service Technicians and Mechanics](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/diesel-service-technicians-and-mechanics.htm)

These BLS sources are US-specific corroboration, not global occupational taxonomy. The JSON review preserves exact queued ESCO locators and task excerpts and notes that the individual ESCO pages were not live-refetched in this pass.
