## Sources and limits

- [BLS Animal Care and Service Workers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/animal-care-and-service-workers.htm): tasks and settings for animal care, grooming, training, kennels, shelters, stables, pet sitting, and zoos. US occupational reference; not global taxonomy.
- [BLS Animal Caretakers factsheet](https://www.bls.gov/ors/factsheet/animal-caretakers.htm): explicitly describes non-consumption animals and settings such as kennels, shelters, zoos, circuses, and aquariums. US-specific task boundary.
- [WOAH development of animal-welfare standards](https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/animal-health-and-welfare/animal-welfare/development-of-animal-welfare-standards/): international standards treat production systems (including beef/dairy cattle) separately from topics such as dog-population management and working equids. It informs scope caution but is not an occupation taxonomy.
- [ESCO occupation profiles](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/classification/occupation-main): exact URIs are shown per role. The task text is from the checked-in profile cache; individual URI pages were not independently fetched live in this pass.
