# D111 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Textile and garment manufacturing runs the machinery that spins fibre into yarn and turns yarn into fabric and clothing. Spinning and twisting operators, knitting and finishing technicians and pattern-printing workers run machines, measure yarn, test fabric quality and inspect finished goods, with supervisors managing operations. Textile mills and garment factories are the usual workplaces.
