# D134 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Naval architecture and marine engineering is the design of ships and the running of their machinery. Naval architects design and survey vessels and offshore energy installations, while marine engineers operate and maintain ship engines and systems at sea, from assistant to chief engineer. Shipyards, shipping companies and offshore energy firms are typical employers.
