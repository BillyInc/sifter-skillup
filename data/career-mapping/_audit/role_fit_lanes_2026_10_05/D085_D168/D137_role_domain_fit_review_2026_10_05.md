# D137 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Medical physics and radiation therapy apply the science of radiation to diagnosing and treating illness, particularly cancer. Radiation therapists deliver treatment to patients, radiographers capture medical images, and medical physicists plan doses and keep equipment safe and accurate. People in this field work mainly in hospitals and cancer treatment centres.
