# D147 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Process automation keeps factories and plants running through computer-controlled systems. Control room operators monitor automated machines in refineries, gas plants and production lines, troubleshoot faults and write reports, while automation and instrumentation engineers design the control systems themselves. Manufacturing companies and oil and gas facilities employ most of these professionals.
