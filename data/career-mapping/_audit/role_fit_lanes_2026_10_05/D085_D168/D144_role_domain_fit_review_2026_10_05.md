# D144 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Climate science and meteorology study the weather and the long-term behaviour of the atmosphere. Forecasters and meteorologists use instruments and specialised computer models to predict weather conditions, while climatologists, hydrologists and oceanographers analyse climate, water and ocean patterns. Typical employers are government weather agencies, aviation organisations and research institutes.
