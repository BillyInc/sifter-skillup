# D122 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Medical laboratory technology is the testing of blood and other body samples to help doctors diagnose and treat illness. Phlebotomists collect blood, while technologists and technicians run clinical chemistry and molecular tests, classify blood types, maintain laboratory equipment and keep patient records. Hospitals, clinics and diagnostic laboratories employ most practitioners.
