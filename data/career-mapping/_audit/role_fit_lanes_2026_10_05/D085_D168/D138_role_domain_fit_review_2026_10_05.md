# D138 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Biomedical engineering combines engineering with biology and medicine to design equipment and materials used in healthcare. Biomedical engineers and biomaterials scientists develop and test medical devices, while research and robotics engineers work on new technologies for the body. They are employed by hospitals, medical device manufacturers and research laboratories.
