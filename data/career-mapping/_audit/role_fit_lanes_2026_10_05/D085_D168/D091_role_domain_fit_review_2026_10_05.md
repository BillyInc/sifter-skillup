## Sources and limits

- [UNECE Consolidated Resolution on Road Traffic, Annex III](https://unece.org/DAM/trans/roadsafe/publications/docs/Consolidated_Resolution_on%20Road_Traffic_RE1_e.pdf): minimum requirements for professional driving instructors, including educational methods, vehicle-category knowledge, and category-specific ability. Regional recommendation/instrument, not a universal licensing rule and not a source for maritime training.
- [IMO Maritime Training Institutes](https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/humanelement/pages/maritimetraininginstitutes.aspx): STCW training/assessment is supervised and monitored, and trainers/assessors are to be appropriately qualified. IMO notes Member Governments are responsible for approval; this does not define road instruction or national licensing.
- [ESCO occupation profiles](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/classification/occupation-main): exact task URIs are shown per role. The individual ESCO pages were not live-fetched in this pass; task text is from the checked-in cache.
