Scope: Aeronautical engineering is the design, building and upkeep of aircraft. Aerodynamics engineers and engine designers develop new aircraft, flight test engineers prove them in the air, and avionics inspectors, maintenance engineers and assembly supervisors keep production and finished aircraft safe. Aircraft factories, airlines, maintenance organisations and air forces are the main employers.
