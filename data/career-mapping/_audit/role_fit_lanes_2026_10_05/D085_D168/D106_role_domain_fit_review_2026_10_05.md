# D106 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Garment making, tailoring and dressmaking turns fabric into clothing and covered furnishings. Dressmakers, sewing machinists, patternmakers and embroiderers cut and sew garments, grade clothing sizes and work to standard sizing systems, while upholsterers cover seats for boats and railway cars. Typical workplaces include tailoring shops, garment factories and fashion houses.
