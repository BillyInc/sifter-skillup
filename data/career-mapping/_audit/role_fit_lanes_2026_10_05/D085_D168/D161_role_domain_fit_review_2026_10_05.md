# D161 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

**Scope:** Humanitarian aid and emergency response deliver help to communities hit by disaster, conflict or hardship. Aid workers and project officers manage relief programmes, coordinators organise disaster response, logistics officers move supplies, and monitoring officers track whether help reaches the people who need it. Charities, international organisations and government agencies do this work.
