# D125 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Medical secretarial work is the administration that keeps clinics and hospitals organised. Medical secretaries and receptionists book appointments, greet patients, answer their questions and transcribe notes, keeping health records accurate and confidential, while practice managers run the office. Hospitals, clinics and veterinary practices employ people in this line of work.
