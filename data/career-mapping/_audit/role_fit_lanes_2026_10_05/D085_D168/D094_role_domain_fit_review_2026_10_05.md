# D094 role-to-parent-domain fit review

Audit date: 2026-10-05  
Scope: role tasks against **Electronics Servicing** only. No subgroup or specialization fit is assessed; no canonical mapping is changed.

## Result

Reviewed **26/26** queued roles: **18 supported, 8 conditional, 0 unsupported, 0 unresolved**.

The parent scope is repair/maintenance of electronic devices and communication equipment, including service of phones, computers, alarms, and telecoms equipment; smart-home installation; and hardware/microelectronics testing. Repair, maintenance, installation, testing, and communication-infrastructure roles fit that task boundary.

Conditional roles are computer hardware engineering technician, electronics engineering technician, instrumentation engineering technician, microelectronics engineering technician, microsystem engineering technician, optoelectronic engineering technician, sensor engineering technician, and tramways technician. The first seven have product-development or mixed development/testing profiles not clearly bounded by a parent description centered on servicing, maintenance, and testing. The tramways task excerpt identifies lift/aerial-tramway maintenance but does not say whether the relevant work is electronic servicing or primarily mechanical transport-equipment maintenance. These remain conditional pending more specific task evidence or a clarified domain boundary—not promoted or removed by title/code.

[BLS: Electrical and Electronic Engineering Technicians](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineering-technicians.htm)
