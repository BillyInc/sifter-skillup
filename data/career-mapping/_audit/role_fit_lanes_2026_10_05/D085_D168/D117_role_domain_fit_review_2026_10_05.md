# D117 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Railway work keeps trains and rail yards running. Train drivers operate services, dispatchers and signalpersons control movements from signal boxes and level crossings, shunters and train preparers couple wagons and follow switching instructions, and conductors look after passengers. Most roles are with railway corporations and rail freight operators.
