# D086 role-to-parent-domain fit review

Date: 2026-10-05  
Scope: role-to-parent-domain fit only. **No role-to-specialization assessment or assignment was made.**

## Method

For each D086 mapping in the saved unresolved lane queue, compare the role's task definition at its exact stored European Commission ESCO occupation URI with the parent domain, *Gaming, Betting and Lottery Operations*. ISCO group, subgroup membership, title resemblance, and role frequency are not evidence of fit. WLA and Gaming Laboratories International (GLI) sources corroborate the boundaries of gaming operations, products, systems, and supplier work; they do not decide an individual role's fit by themselves.

## Corroborating sources and limits

- [WLA-SCS:2024 Code of Practice](https://publications.world-lotteries.org/security-and-risk-management/wla-scs-2024-code-of-practice), Annex B (game-operation controls) addresses lottery draws, betting, video lottery, online games, and related operations; Annex C addresses gaming-system development and services. It corroborates industry scope, not an academic occupational taxonomy.
- [WLA-SCS:2024 standard overview](https://publications.world-lotteries.org/security-and-risk-management/wla-security-control-standard) describes security and integrity controls for lottery, video-lottery, sports/esports betting operators and suppliers. It does not imply every cybersecurity role belongs in D086.
- [GLI standards catalogue](https://gaminglabs.com/gli-standards/) lists gaming-device, bingo/keno, interactive-gaming, table-game, raffle, and event-wagering system standards. [GLI Gaming Security Framework](https://gaminglabs.com/gaming-security-framework-gli-gsf/) distinguishes land-based venue controls and online gaming controls. GLI is a testing/standards provider, not a regulator or occupation taxonomy; jurisdictions decide which standards apply.
