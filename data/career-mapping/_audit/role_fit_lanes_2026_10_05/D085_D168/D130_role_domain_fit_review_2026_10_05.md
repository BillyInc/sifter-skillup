# D130 role-to-parent-domain fit review — 2026-10-05

Scope: Recruitment and employment services match people to jobs. Recruitment consultants, talent acquisition specialists and employment agents advertise vacancies, interview and select candidates, follow labour legislation and keep candidate information confidential, while employer branding specialists present organisations attractively to job seekers. Recruitment agencies and company human resources departments are the typical employers.
