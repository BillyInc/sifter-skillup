## Evidence and limits

[WOAH international standards](https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/standards/) cover terrestrial animal health/welfare and veterinary public health, and establish separate aquatic-health codes. WOAH's [aquatic animal work](https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/animal-health-and-welfare/aquatic-animals/) describes the role of veterinarians and aquatic animal-health professionals in health, welfare and disease controls. The [WOAH PVS Pathway](https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-offer/improving-veterinary-services/pvs-pathway/) addresses veterinary-service capability. FAO's [aquatic health-management work](https://www.fao.org/in-action/aquatic-health-management-biosecurity/our-work/en) provides complementary context. The [AVMA complementary/alternative veterinary-medicine guidance](https://ebusiness.avma.org/files/productdownloads/VetTherapeutics.pdf) is a US professional source and does not determine authorization elsewhere.

Conditional is not confirmed: animal manual therapies require diagnosis/referral and legal scope; aquaculture and food biotechnology fit only on the stated health/public-health assignment. This review does not establish local registration or student eligibility.
