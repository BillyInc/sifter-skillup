## Evidence and limits

BLS's [protective-service occupation overview](https://www.bls.gov/ors/factsheet/protective-service-occupations.htm) spans firefighting, law enforcement, animal control, private investigation, security, lifeguards, transportation screeners and crossing guards. Its [security-guard profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/protective-service/security-guards.htm) describes property protection, access control, patrol, alarms and emergency response; [SOC definitions](https://www.bls.gov/soc/2018/soc_2018_definitions.pdf) include fire pump operators, marine firefighters and juvenile correctional officers. [IMO search-and-rescue guidance](https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/safety/pages/searchandrescue-default.aspx) supports the maritime rescue function. These are occupational boundary references, not universal demand or licensing evidence.

Aircraft marshaller and game keeper are excluded based on the supplied tasks: aircraft movement/parking and wildlife habitat/game-population management do not themselves establish security or protective services.
