## Evidence and limits

[ESA Operations Engineering](https://www.esa.int/About_Us/Careers_at_ESA/Operations_Engineering) describes the space-mission operations engineering job family, including mission operations and ground infrastructure. [NASA Systems Engineering, Integration & Testing](https://www.nasa.gov/ames-engineering/spaceflight-division/seit/) describes engineering work supporting spacecraft and nanosatellites. [O*NET Avionics Technicians](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/49-2091.00) explicitly includes space vehicles in its occupation task description.

Aerospace and telecom occupations often span non-space work. Their D059 links remain conditional until the actual role assignment is tied to spacecraft, satellite, or mission-system tasks. An aircraft-only task is not made space-related by ISCO or subgroup placement.
