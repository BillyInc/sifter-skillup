## Evidence and limits

The [ILO occupational-safety systems guidance](https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/safety-and-health-work/occupational-safety-and-health-management-systems) defines workplace hazard identification, risk assessment/control and worker protection; [ILO-OSH 2001](https://www.ilo.org/publications/guidelines-occupational-safety-and-health-management-systems-ilo-osh-2001) describes organizational OSH systems. [OSHA's hazard-control guidance](https://www.osha.gov/safeandsound/safety-and-health-programs/find-and-fix-hazards) and [inspection fact sheet](https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/FACTSHEET-INSPECTIONS.pdf) provide official examples of workplace hazard identification and compliance inspection. These sources support the general OHS boundary, not country-specific legal conclusions.

Cross-sector roles are conditional when their task could concern product, environmental, patient, transport-system or public safety rather than worker protection. The specified work assignment must establish the OHS link.
