## Evidence and limits

[WHO's mental-health overview](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-health-strengthening-our-response) frames mental health as a component of health and well-being, while occupation-task evidence is provided by [O*NET clinical psychologists](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-3033.00), [marriage and family therapists](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1013.00), [mental-health counsellors](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1014.00) and [substance-abuse counsellors](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1011.00), together with exact ESCO/O*NET locators retained per row.

Conditional is not confirmed: family-planning counselling is limited to a genuine person-centred counselling remit; life coaching is not psychological treatment; and mental-health support work must not imply independent clinical practice. Volunteer mentoring is not counselling merely because it supports personal development.
