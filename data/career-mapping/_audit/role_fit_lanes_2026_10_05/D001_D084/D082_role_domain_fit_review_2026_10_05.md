## Evidence and limits

BLS profiles define [receptionist](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/receptionists.htm) work (calls, visitors, information and appointments), [customer-service representative](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Office-and-Administrative-Support/Customer-service-representatives.htm) work (questions, complaints and service channels), [information clerk](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/information-clerks.htm) duties, [public-safety dispatch](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/police-fire-and-ambulance-dispatchers.htm), [concierge](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/concierges.htm) support, and [medical administrative support](https://www.bls.gov/ors/factsheet/medical-secretaries-and-administrative-assistants.htm). These are task-boundary references, not Nigerian demand or licensing claims.

Market research interviews and survey enumeration collect research data rather than provide service; medical transcription is record production; medical-practice management is conditional on actual frontline service duties.
