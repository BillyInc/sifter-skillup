## Evidence boundary

[NIST IR 8202](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8202/final) describes blockchain as a distributed, tamper-evident/tamper-resistant ledger and discusses cryptographic links, validation, and consensus. It is used only to define the technology boundary. Each role's exact ESCO task text, source locators, and limits are retained in the JSON report. Generic IT work is not treated as blockchain work just because the same skills could be used on a blockchain project.

Conditional mappings should not be rendered as confirmed domain roles or student requirements without evidence that the role assignment actually concerns blockchain/DLT. No alternative domain or canonical correction is inferred.
