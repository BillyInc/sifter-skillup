## Evidence and limits

[WHO's rehabilitation workforce overview](https://www.who.int/teams/noncommunicable-diseases/sensory-functions-disability-and-rehabilitation/rehabilitation/workforce) names audiologists, occupational therapists, physiotherapists, prosthetists/orthotists and speech/language therapists among rehabilitation occupations. The [WHO Rehabilitation Competency Framework](https://www.who.int/publications/b/54312) supplies a cross-professional competency reference. [ASHA's SLP overview](https://www.asha.org/Students/Speech-Language-Pathologists/) and [dysphagia practice portal](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/adult-dysphagia/) corroborate communication/swallowing task scope.

Conditional is not confirmed: massage, complementary/manual modalities, generic movement-science work and broad adjunctive therapies require the assignment and scope conditions stated. This fit review does not validate a treatment or credential.
