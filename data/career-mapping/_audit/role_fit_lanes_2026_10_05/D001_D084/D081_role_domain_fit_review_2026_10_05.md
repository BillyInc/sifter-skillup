## Evidence and limits

BLS's [general office clerk profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/general-office-clerks.htm) covers phones, correspondence, mail, scheduling, filing, document updates and data entry. Its [secretary/administrative assistant profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/secretaries-and-administrative-assistants.htm) describes routine clerical and organisational support, and its [office-administrative occupations overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Office-and-Administrative-Support/home.htm) distinguishes clerical/administrative tasks from postal delivery. The [BLS office-support factsheet](https://www.bls.gov/ors/factsheet/office-and-administrative-support-occupations.htm) provides a broader occupational-group cross-check. These are US references, not local demand evidence.

Postman/postwoman, proofreader and secretary general are excluded because their supplied work is postal delivery, editorial production or executive leadership—not office clerical administration.
