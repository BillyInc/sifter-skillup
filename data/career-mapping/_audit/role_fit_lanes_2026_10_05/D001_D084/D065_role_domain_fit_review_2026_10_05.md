## Exact decisions

| Role | Result | Task-based rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Interpreters and Translators | supported | The task is directly to interpret oral/sign language or translate written material between languages, matching the stored domain definition. |
| language engineer | supported | The task applies computing and natural-language processing to translation quality and mapping between human and machine translation; it directly supports translation and linguistic systems. |
| sign language teacher | conditional | The task teaches sign language, a language modality recognized by the World Federation of the Deaf. Teaching a language is not itself interpreting; the link is justified only when the assignment also serves sign-language linguistic documentation, interpreter training or closely connected language-service capability. **Condition:** a documented interpreting, translator-training or linguistic-service remit, not language instruction alone |
| translation agency manager | supported | The task manages delivery and quality of translation services and coordinates translators, directly supporting the domain's translation work. |

## Evidence and limits

The [ESCO framework](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/use-esco) is a common occupation/skill classification and its exact occupation locators are preserved per task-backed row. The [World Federation of the Deaf statement](https://wfdeaf.org/wfd-statement-on-standardized-sign-language/) discusses natural sign-language variation and resources used in teaching sign languages and training interpreters. It supports sign language as a language, not an inference that teaching alone is interpreting.

Conditional is not confirmed: the sign-language teacher link requires a documented interpreting, translator-training or linguistic-service assignment.
