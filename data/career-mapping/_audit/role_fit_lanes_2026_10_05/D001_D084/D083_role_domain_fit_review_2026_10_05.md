## Evidence and limits

BLS [travel-agent duties](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Sales/Travel-agents.htm) cover trip planning, packages, excursions, bookings and destination advice. Its [information-clerk profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/information-clerks.htm) includes passenger reservations, ticketing and itinerary questions; [concierge duties](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/concierges.htm) corroborate visitor support. [ICAO cabin-crew training guidance](https://www.icao.int/cabin-crew-competency-based-training-and-assessment) grounds the passenger safety/service assignment, and [UN Tourism's glossary](https://www.unwto.org/glossary-tourism-terms) provides tourism terminology. These sources support role boundaries, not local labour demand or licensure.

Environmental education officer is conditional only for guided visitor/nature-tour duties; education/outreach without a tourism or visitor-service assignment does not establish fit.
