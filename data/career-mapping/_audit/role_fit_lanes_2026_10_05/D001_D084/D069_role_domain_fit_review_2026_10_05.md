## Evidence and limits

[ICAO Standards and Recommended Practices](https://www.icao.int/safety/airnavigation/pages/standard-and-recommended-practices-(sarps).aspx) set international aviation-safety standards. [FAA pilot resources](https://www.faa.gov/pilots) and [air-traffic information](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic) provide US-system examples; [EASA airworthiness](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/aircraft-products/airworthiness) describes aircraft-safety regulation. The [NASA astronaut overview](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/astronauts/) concerns human spaceflight, which is distinct from aircraft operations.

Conditional is not confirmed: mixed spacecraft/aircraft work and general passenger service count only on the aircraft-specific assignment. Spaceflight is not included by aviation adjacency.
