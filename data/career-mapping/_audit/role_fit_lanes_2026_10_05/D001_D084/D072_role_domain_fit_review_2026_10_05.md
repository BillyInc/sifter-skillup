## Evidence and limits

The [USGS geologic-mapping program](https://www.usgs.gov/mission-areas/geology-energy-minerals/science/geologic-mapping-and-foundational-science) and [USGS Geology, Geophysics, and Geochemistry Science Center](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/gggsc/science/science-topics) cover mapping, geospatial analysis, hydrogeology, hydrology and Earth-material sciences; [USGS CEGIS](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/cegis/science) is its geospatial-science source. [NOAA hydrographic surveying](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/navigation/hydro/) and [NOAA hydrography overview](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/hydrography.html) define marine measurement/mapping and bathymetric survey work. BLS occupational descriptions corroborate [GIS-connected geography and mapping occupations](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/geographers.htm), [hydrology](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/hydrologists.htm), [geological/hydrologic technicians](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/geological-and-petroleum-technicians.htm) and [surveying/mapping technicians](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/surveying-and-mapping-technicians.htm). These sources support the domain boundary; they are not evidence of Nigerian demand or local licensure.

Mud logger is conditional: the current task text supports subsurface fluid, depth and lithology logging, but it does not establish that every such job performs GIS or produces spatial mapping. Keep the link only where the assignment includes geological-log or spatial-data work.
