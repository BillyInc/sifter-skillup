## Evidence and limits

[CFA Institute investment career paths](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/careers) distinguish research, portfolio management, trading, corporate finance and related roles. Its [sales and trading overview](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/careers/sales-and-trading) describes buying and selling financial products for investors, while its [portfolio-manager profile](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/careers/portfolio-manager) identifies investment decisions and portfolio construction. [CFTC futures-market basics](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/EducationCenter/FuturesMarketBasics/index2.htm) describes futures contracts and commodity trading; that US-market regulator source is used to define the instrument, not to infer global access or licensing.

Conditional is not confirmed: it marks occupations whose tasks span the investment market and other functions. Generic corporate risk, investor relations and broad financial planning do not become Trading roles by financial-sector proximity alone.
