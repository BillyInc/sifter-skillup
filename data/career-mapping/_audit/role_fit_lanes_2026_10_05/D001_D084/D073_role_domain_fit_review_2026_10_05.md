## Evidence and limits

The [International Council on Archives](https://www.ica.org/discover-archives/what-is-an-archivist/) describes archivists' work in managing and providing long-term access to records. [ICOM's museum definition](https://icom.museum/en/resources/standards-guidelines/museum-definition/) identifies research, collecting, conservation, interpretation and exhibition; [ICOM Documentation](https://icom-documentation.mini.icom.museum/standards/museum-documentation-definition/) covers collection documentation, management, accessibility and conservation. [IFLA library services](https://www.ifla.org/publications/riss-statement-on-libraries-and-development/) describes library access, reference, circulation and user support. The [Canadian Conservation Institute's collection documentation guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/conservation-institute/services/agents-deterioration/dissociation.html) identifies registrar/collection-manager duties in documenting and tracking collections, and its [object-care guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/conservation-institute/services/learning-activities/care-objects.html) addresses handling, condition reporting and preservation.

The conditions on information manager, taxidermist and zoo registrar preserve the boundary between general information/animal operations and the domain's library, archival or cultural-collection remit.
