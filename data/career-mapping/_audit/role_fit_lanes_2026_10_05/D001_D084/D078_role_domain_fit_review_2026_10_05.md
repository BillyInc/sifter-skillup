## Evidence and limits

[UNESCO UIS's social-sciences classification](https://uis.unesco.org/node/3080050) includes psychology, economics/business, sociology, law, political science, social/economic geography and media/communications. [UNESCO's ISCED-F field descriptions](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/in/documentViewer.xhtml?+v=2.1.196&ark=%2Fark%3A%2F48223%2Fpf0000235049%2FPDF%2F235049eng.pdf&file=%2Fin%2Frest%2FannotationSVC%2FDownloadWatermarkedAttachment%2Fattach_import_63478a00-96f0-4fee-aea7-1533d340d96f%3F_%3D235049eng.pdf&id=p%3A%3Ausmarcdef_0000235049&locale=en&multi=true) place social anthropology, sociology, demography, criminology and human/social geography in social-science fields, while history/archaeology and philosophy are in humanities and physical geography in Earth science. The UN [Population Division](https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/) conducts official demographic research. The introductory [UNESCO social-science explainer](https://www.unesco.org/uk/query-list/s/419467) is used only as a broad cross-check.

Archaeologist, genealogist, geographer, statistician and thanatology researcher remain conditional because the supplied tasks span adjacent fields; student-facing inclusion requires the specific social-science lens stated for each role.
