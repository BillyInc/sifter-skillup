## Evidence and limits

[UNESCO ICT Competency Framework for Teachers](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-ict-competency-framework-teachers?hub=785) treats using ICT in teaching as a defined professional competency and guides teacher training for technology integration. [O*NET Instructional Coordinators](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/25-9031.00) includes instructional design, researching instructional technology, and training instructors to use or integrate it.

These sources support the boundary: EdTech work involves technology-enabled learning, instructional design, or ICT-focused training—not simply working as a teacher or trainer. A conditional disposition is not a confirmed student path; general subject-teaching records should not appear as D058 roles solely because they teach.
