## Bounded findings and role reconciliation

- The four existing areas map to distinct operating/product outputs: venue operations, betting/wager lifecycle, lottery/draw administration, and product/system design/testing/assurance. Responsible product design, integrity/security and compliance cut across areas; they should be contextual methods/requirements rather than forced fifth areas without evidence.
- Alternatives and complements are contextual: fixed-odds vs pari-mutuel differ in pricing/settlement; RNG-based vs externally determined outcomes have different fairness evidence; land-based vs remote channels have different operational and testing needs; GLI/WLA/regulator requirements are not interchangeable. Product standards, testing, analytics, responsible design, payments and security may connect downstream to multiple areas.
- Role denominator mismatch: domain export lists 17 roles; fit review contains 13, all supported, plus four missing from the review: First-Line Supervisors of Gambling Services Workers, Gambling Cage Workers, Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers, and Gambling Surveillance Officers and Gambling Investigators. Preserve as a crosswalk/review queue; do not infer fit or change dispositions in this source pass.
- Nigerian regulatory evidence is jurisdictionally fragmented. FCT and Lagos sources show distinct local licensing/activity categories. This packet does not make a claim about the current constitutional allocation of national/state authority; regulator pages and controlling legislation must be separately reconciled before student-facing legal content.

## Counter-search scope added

- Venue: surveillance, cage, internal audit, KYC, staff training, exclusion and harm recognition are operational controls with jurisdiction-specific ownership.
- Betting: event selection, odds compilation, margin, result source and settlement form a linked sequence; price/risk work cannot be inferred solely from generic “sports betting” labels.
- Lottery/bingo: draw governance and physical/electronic draw integrity are distinct; RNG statistical checks are only one part of game integrity.
- Products/systems: software testing, RNG, game math/RTP, player interfaces, security, change control and responsible design connect to operator acceptance but are not interchangeable.
- Emerging: cryptographic provability is an optional, narrow design/audit pattern, not a general replacement for independent testing or regulation.
