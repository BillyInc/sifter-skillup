**Status: in progress; evidence assessment only.** This is a lane-specific, append-only checkpoint. It does not approve or implement taxonomy changes.

## Scope and guardrails

- Assigned roots: D057–D112 inclusive (**56 domain IDs**); D113 is out of scope.
- Audit each root’s specialization/area set for parent-domain fit, clear non-overlapping boundaries, naming, reasonable coverage/count, and whether a flat domain is preferable to artificial subdivision.
- D086’s user-specified label work and D108’s user-specified label fix are not to be repeated. D086 remains in scope for set/boundary/count assessment only. D113 is excluded entirely. D108 may be assessed for set structure without redoing its label fix.
- Do not edit canonical `domain_meta.json`, `domain_structure.json`, role-library JSON, role mappings, generator scripts, or shared registers. Do not infer role fit from ISCO or subgroup membership. No fabricated roles, requirements, or learner-item relationships.
- A specialization is an evidence-backed field/practice area, not merely an occupational-code bucket. Recommend adding one only when sources support a distinct area and its boundary; otherwise prefer a broader/flat path. This audit assesses taxonomy structure, not role-to-domain fit.
- Each decision must record authoritative source URL and exact locator (heading/section/page/table), supported claim and limits, alternatives, and uncertainty. External evidence supports an internal taxonomy recommendation; it is not formal accreditation or local employer validation.

## Method and progress accounting

Read-only inventory source: `domain_meta.json` and `domain_structure.json` as found at task start. Existing structures are unverified input. Source checks will prioritize official professional bodies, standards bodies, government/educational occupational or curriculum frameworks, and primary institutional material. Where an official source does not establish a boundary or count, record that uncertainty rather than guessing.

Progress is counted only when a domain has both (1) a recorded set-level disposition and (2) at least one relevant source with an exact locator and explicit limits. Inventory alone is not an audited domain.
