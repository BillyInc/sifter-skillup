# CCMAS programme → domain cluster correction audit

Audit date: 2026-08-17

## Digital Health (D057)

The D057 definition requires computers and data to run healthcare: health records, clinical coding, medical informatics, databases, health-information systems, or digital-health application development. A programme being in healthcare is not enough.

Removed the D057 Functional link from general pharmacology, physiotherapy, audiology, nursing, complementary/alternative medicine, dental therapy, medical laboratory science, nutrition, occupational therapy, prosthetics/orthotics, radiography, speech-language therapy, MBBS, BDS, Bachelor/Doctor of Pharmacy, and Medical Physics.

Promoted Health Information Management → D057 from Functional to Direct because its curriculum identity is health information/records management. The remaining D057 links are the dedicated Information Technology and Health Informatics programme (Direct) and Biomedical Engineering (Functional, retained for its medical-device/health-technology bridge).

## Early Childhood (D127)

The D127 definition is specifically early-childhood/nursery care and teaching. It cannot be inferred from a programme being child-facing, health-related, a general teaching degree, or a social-science degree.

Removed 25 unsupported D127 Functional links from unrelated health, arts, subject-teaching, and social-science programmes. Retained:

- Education :: Early Childhood Education → Direct
- Education :: Primary Education → Functional
- Education :: Special Needs Education → Functional
