### Area boundaries

- **Automated driving planning** produces a selected ego behavior and feasible path/trajectory under maps, perceived objects, vehicle state, ODD and rules. Other-agent prediction is an input/condition, not the ego plan. Tracking that target belongs to control.
- **Vehicle control and dynamics** tracks a target trajectory or motion request and generates steering/braking/acceleration commands under vehicle and actuator constraints. It does not generate the target route/path/trajectory.
- **Vehicle interface and system integration** converts normalized intent to platform-specific commands and returns actual vehicle status, actuation and mode; it also integrates and diagnoses vehicle, compute, network and ADS subsystems. This is distinct from application planning/control algorithms.
- **ADS safety, verification and validation** assembles safety requirements and evidence across lifecycle and ODD. ISO 26262 malfunction hazards, ISO 21448 SOTIF insufficiencies, ADS-level safety-case reasoning, simulation, closed-course/on-road evidence and approval remain distinct layers.
