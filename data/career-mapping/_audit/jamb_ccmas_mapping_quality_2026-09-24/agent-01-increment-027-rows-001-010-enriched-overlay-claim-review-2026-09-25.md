## Conflict-level conclusion

The enriched overlay improves traceability by naming courses and occupations, but its course locators are local JSON anchors rather than primary document locators; its external source records have missing exact locators and access dates. More importantly, several cited courses do not support the claimed destination tasks. The material therefore does **not** justify automatic restoration of any of the five affected missing IDs in this partition. Current fail-closed rows 3, 5, 9, and 10 remain linkless/review-required pending candidate-set reconciliation. The only independently plausible future candidate is narrow D041 quantitative research for row 10; this is not yet approved or written to the map. D045 may be separately re-evaluated for tightly scoped policy/manpower pathways from the full relevant CCMAS outcomes, but not from the overlay's broad role list.
