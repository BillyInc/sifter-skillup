## CCMAS component and outcome comparison

### Administration and Management CCMAS — Accounting (ACC)

The NUC course structure (printed pp.31–32) establishes distinct accounting progression. Relevant direct course evidence:

- **ACC 101, Introduction to Financial Accounting I** (p.31; learning outcomes/content pp.34–35): accounting purpose and branches, source documents, journals/ledgers, double-entry, trial balance, basic records and bank reconciliation.
- **ACC 102, Introduction to Financial Accounting II** (p.31; outcomes/content pp.35–36): financial-reporting framework, users/reports, error correction, control accounts and preparation of accounts.
- **ACC 201–202, Financial Accounting I–II** (p.31): continuation of core accounting/reporting.
- **ACC 203, Corporate Governance & Accounting Ethics** (p.31; outcomes/content p.37): company governance, audit/internal control, sustainability governance and professional-accounting ethics.
- **ACC 204, Cost Accounting** (p.31; outcomes/content pp.38–39): cost classification/behaviour, materials/labour/overheads, job/process costing, marginal/standard costing, variance analysis and budgets.
- **ACC 301–302, Financial Reporting I–II** (p.31; outcomes/content pp.42–43): preparation and interpretation of financial statements under accounting standards, ratios and reporting judgments.
- **ACC 307, Auditing and Assurance I** (p.31; outcomes/content pp.43–44): audit objectives, legal/regulatory framework, audit process, evidence/reporting, standards, errors versus fraud.

These are direct Accounting learning outcomes; they do not imply that every Accounting and Finance offering includes every Accounting CCMAS course or that the row is a professional certification/licensure path.

### Administration and Management CCMAS — Finance / Banking and Finance (FIN)

The NUC Finance/Banking and Finance course structure (printed pp.234–235; programme overview p.232) covers functional finance and banking. Relevant outcomes:

- **FIN 101, Introduction to Finance** (p.234; outcomes/content pp.237–238): finance functions, sources of business finance, financial statement ratios, time value of money and interest.
- **FIN 204, Quantitative Analysis** (p.234; outcomes/content p.240): decision analysis, simulation, linear programming, inventory/sequencing and financial/statistical/operational modelling.
- **FIN 303, Principles of Finance** (p.234; outcomes/content pp.244–245): sources/uses of funds, working capital, cash budgets, capital budgeting, risk/return and financial planning.
- **FIN 313, Financial Management** (p.234; outcomes/content p.246): financial forecasting, investment appraisal, valuation, capital structure and financial risk.
- **FIN 410, Investment Analysis and Portfolio Management** (p.234; outcomes/content pp.250–251): investment instruments, return/risk, appraisal and portfolio methods.

Bank-specific courses are explicit and should remain separate from general Finance: **FIN 209/210/217** (banking, banking methods/law), **FIN 308** (lending/credit administration), and **FIN 409** (financial technology). The JAMB title says “Finance,” not “Banking and Finance”; these bank-specific topics are optional/context-dependent candidates, not an automatically required component for either offering.

### Social Sciences CCMAS — limited adjacent evidence for MAP-004

The Social Sciences programme list (printed p.18) contains Economics, Political Science, PPE and other social-science degrees but no Accounting or Finance degree. It nevertheless offers narrow relevant courses:

- **ACC 201, Basic Accounting** (printed p.80): double-entry, journals, ledgers, trial balance, cash books and basic financial statements. Its outcomes include government accounting and introductory accounting-system concepts; it is a foundation, not the Admin CCMAS Accounting sequence.
- **ACC 202, Development Budget and Financing** (printed pp.80–81): public/development budget processes, sources of funds, public expenditure planning and control. This is development/public-budget content, not corporate finance or a standalone Finance curriculum.
- **ECO 101/102, Principles of Microeconomics/Macroeconomics** (printed pp.230–231): national-income accounting, savings/investment, taxation, government revenue/expenditure, domestic money/banking and foreign exchange. These provide economic context; they do not teach the ACC reporting/audit sequence or FIN corporate financial-management outcomes.

Thus, the Social Sciences CCMAS can contribute context-specific support, but it cannot by itself substantiate a complete Accounting and Finance student path.

## Unresolved evidence / what cannot be generalized

- **No identical programme curriculum inference:** BCU’s JAMB faculty/context and FEDOTUOKE’s JAMB faculty/context are distinct. Same label does not make their selected courses identical.
- **No whole-degree union inference:** a title with two facets does not justify copying every course from both CCMAS programmes. This checkpoint proposes bounded candidates, not complete degree plans.
- **No faculty-only inference:** the Social Sciences JAMB label does not establish that the programme follows the complete Social Sciences CCMAS, nor does it prove it adopts Administration’s Accounting and Finance CCMAS wholesale.
- **No historical-to-current identity inference:** FUOTUOKE’s official 2012 history and current 2025/26 admission material show a naming/faculty transition. The current separate degrees cannot be projected backward onto the JAMB p.577 occurrence; the old occurrence cannot be treated as current without a current JAMB source check.
- **No item-level promotion from local JSON:** material ACC code/title discrepancies make all locally extracted ACC rows suspect until aligned with the official PDF. FIN course titles also need the same source comparison before accepting source-derived payloads.
- The official NUC CCMAS provides national minimum/core curricula and outcomes, not BCU- or FEDOTUOKE-specific selections, electives, sequencing or delivery. That is sufficient to form bounded learning candidates; it is not evidence of the exact course schedule at either school.
- Targeted official FUOTUOKE site search (2026-09-25) located its institutional-history and current admission pages, but not a course-by-course outcome syllabus for the historic combined JAMB occurrence. No claim of exhaustive web absence is made; the mapping remains a CCMAS-supported candidate review, not “genuinely unavailable.”
