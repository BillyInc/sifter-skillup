## Downstream reassessment method and disposition

For every linked domain above, the active taxonomy’s subgroup labels and declared descendant role counts were inspected against `role_library_v3_cleaned.json`. That confirms the descendants exist structurally; it does not validate them for these programmes. Existing tiers are therefore **reassessed as unresolved candidates**, not retained as verified direct/functional/adjacent claims. The strongest bounded leads are bioscience/lab/research or marine-aquaculture tasks for the Biology/Marine rows; energy-related foundations for rows 189–190; and meteorological study for row 187. Each requires matching specific source outcomes to a specific subgroup and actual role tasks. Broad farm, trades, engineering, data, biomedical, forestry, archives, AI and space descendants must not appear as confirmed student requirements without that evidence.
