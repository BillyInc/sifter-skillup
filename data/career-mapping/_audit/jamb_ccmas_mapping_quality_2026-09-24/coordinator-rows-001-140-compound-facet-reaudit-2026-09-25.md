## Decision standard

- A **confirmed scope defect** means the saved row-specific scope omits a named curriculum facet or has zero selected course rows, and the corresponding official CCMAS package/course outcomes establish that the facet is substantively distinct. This confirms an omission in the current representation; it does **not** authorize a full CCMAS degree-course union or a domain-link promotion.
- A **source/context candidate** means a title or parsed token suggests a facet, but an exact JAMB institution/table occurrence, curriculum equivalence, or selected-course outcome still needs verification. Keep it held; do not call it a confirmed curriculum union or genuine unavailability.
- A missing scope for a combined title is a real audit defect even when the safe current state is fail-closed. “Review required” is not a completed source search and not student-facing evidence.
