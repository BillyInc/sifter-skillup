## Downstream review rule applied

For each stored link, I enumerated the active domain’s subgroup labels and role counts in `domain_structure.json`, then checked the role library for the corresponding domain descendants. This is a structural descendant audit, not a claim that each descendant role is source-validated. A route is only acceptable when a cited CCMAS course outcome or authoritative external source supports the specific learning item, the named subgroup and the actual role task; the direct/functional/adjacent tier must reflect that evidence. Generic domain-wide or role-frequency associations do not meet that bar. Unsupported links should ultimately be removed or explicitly marked unsupported, not presented as confirmed student requirements.
