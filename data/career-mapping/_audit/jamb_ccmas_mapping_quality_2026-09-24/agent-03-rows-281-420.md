These are listing/identity evidence, not curriculum evidence.

## Coverage and structural checks

- The selected file-order rows and scope rows align by JAMB name across all 420 rows; the tranche contains 140 rows.
- Mapping types: **82 B_granular, 29 A_exact, 25 C_reclassified, 4 D_custom**.
- Scope categories: **78 partial CCMAS scopes, 54 full single-CCMAS scopes, 4 combined CCMAS scopes, 4 external scopes**.
- All 140 names have at least one matching source-offering record (case-insensitive) in the nine faculty offering files. Those records contain **439 source faculty/programme occurrences**, not 140 one-to-one source rows.
- **13 identities** occur under multiple JAMB source faculties, while `programme_mapping.json` has one `jamb_faculty` field per identity (and that field is absent on **49/140** mapping rows). The raw offering files retain separate lists, but the derived programme/domain identity does not retain a faculty-scoped source-context key. See the context table below.
- The 78 partial scopes contain **1,587 selected course records**. Every selected course record resolves to a course code in the stated CCMAS source file/programme; every scope-domain anchor title checked is present among that row’s selected course titles. This checks referential consistency, not whether every anchor semantically supports its domain.
- The 78 partial scopes contain **372 domain links**: 70 direct, 302 functional, 22 adjacent. Each has one or more scope-course title anchors. Manual review found counterexamples where an anchor exists but does not substantiate the link (below).
- The 4 combined scopes have **zero selected courses** each; they name multiple CCMAS components, but their 70 combined-scope links have no JAMB-scope course list. This is an evidence gap for link-by-link status review, not by itself proof that the component pairing is wrong.
- Across the 54 full and 4 combined rows there are **428 domain links**. For 332, a matching programme-domain evidence row with CCMAS course anchors exists and all anchor titles resolve to the named CCMAS component. **96 links have no matching row** in `programme_domain_evidence_2026-08-17.json`; their inherited label is not independently justified by that evidence artifact. These remain review candidates until another item-specific source is identified.
- Across the full tranche, **835 domain links** are present in the scopes: 115 direct, 670 functional, and 50 adjacent. These counts describe current labels, not validated correctness.
- **18 selected records across 9 JAMB rows** use a course code that has multiple distinct titles in the corresponding CCMAS JSON. Some are benign title variants; others are materially different and need the original CCMAS source-page/PDF checked before course-level claims are trusted (rows 295, 327, 389, 406).

## External-enrichment checks and unresolved evidence

- The tranche has 4 external scope rows: French, Anthropology, Sport Science, and Sport Management (Education). French is not genuinely CCMAS-absent (row 284 above). The other three have external enrichment files: `enrichment/anthropology.json`, `enrichment/sport_science.json`, `enrichment/sport_management_education.json`. Their records retain source URLs; Sport Science and Sport Management currently cite UNN’s [Human Kinetics and Sports Studies course modules](https://humankinetics.unn.edu.ng/course-modules/), while Anthropology cites UNN/UNIZIK department pages and the Nigerian Anthropological and Sociological Association. Those sources are external evidence, but their coverage must remain item-specific and their institution-specific scope must not be silently generalized to every JAMB offering.
- `enrichment/french.json` has external curriculum sources and an external domain claim, but its “no French course in CCMAS” premise conflicts with the current CCMAS JSON. Reconcile the version/source discrepancy before using this file as the authoritative French path.
- For Portuguese/English, African and Asian Studies, Textile Engineering, and any other part absent from CCMAS, the checked-in tranche does not yet provide adequate source-grounded component evidence. Do not mark unavailable from a missing seed link; perform targeted authoritative search and retain URL, locator, access date, and supported claim.
- For 96 full/combined domain links without a programme-domain evidence row, and for partial links whose present anchors are semantically weak, the status remains unresolved. Keep these out of “confirmed student requirement” status until evidence is attached or the link is rejected.
