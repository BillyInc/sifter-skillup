### Row 14 summary

The current 19-link set is over-broad relative to the two declared CCMAS components. D063 is the only link directly established by subject-specific course outcomes. D045 and D009 have plausible, narrower policy/statistical-analysis relationships but their current domain-level reach must be restricted to the matching subgroups and tied to a newly explicit scope. The other 16 links have no adequate course/outcome evidence in the checked component curricula for the current functional status; absent additional item-specific evidence, they should not be treated as confirmed student routes. This is a confirmed **evidence/scope contract failure** for the 18 non-Economics links, not a claim that all are impossible career destinations after further learning.
