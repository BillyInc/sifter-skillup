# Whole-catalogue JAMB–CCMAS component/scope screen (2026-09-25)

## Purpose and limits

This is a distinct, read-only triage inventory across the 420 canonical JAMB identities and the 237 CCMAS programme packages. It screens named disciplines/facets against the saved CCMAS component references and course scopes, emphasizing substantive mixed-discipline titles. It does **not** edit or approve any mapping, scope, canonical/derived/active map, seed, or role record. A “confirmed omission” below means confirmed absent from the **saved scope/component assignment**; it does not, by itself, prove what an individual university teaches. Exact institution/faculty source context must still be checked where noted.

Read the base catalogue audit and partition checkpoints alongside this note. The audit records 420 rows in the mapping and scope artifacts and 237 CCMAS packages. It also finds 83 CCMAS packages without an exact component reference in the saved scope. Those 83 are a reverse-coverage queue—not 83 proven unmatched programmes. A name-only crosswalk is discovery evidence, not permission to attach a curriculum. In combined scopes, zero selected courses is a material evidence gap; in a single full-parent scope, zero selected courses may simply mean inheritance of the complete parent and must not be called an omission without checking that parent.

- A CCMAS package with no exact JAMB component reference is not automatically unmatched, and lexical similarity is not sufficient to attach it.
