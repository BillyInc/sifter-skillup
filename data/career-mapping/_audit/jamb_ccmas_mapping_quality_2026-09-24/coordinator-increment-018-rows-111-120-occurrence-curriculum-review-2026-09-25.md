## Downstream-domain, subgroup and role check

For the links listed above, the existing `domain_structure.json` and `role_library_v3_cleaned.json` were cross-checked read-only. This is a **descendant-boundary check**, not a claim that every individual role has been manually source-validated. Examples of why broad inheritance is unsafe:

- **Geology candidates:** D072 Geospatial Science has a Geospatial Data & Spatial Analysis subgroup; D022 Mining spans mine managers, mining/geology engineers, survey/safety technicians and operators; D156 includes cadastral/geospatial surveyors; D054 includes petroleum engineers/geologists and plant/rig operators; D144 contains meteorologists/forecasters. A geology course is not interchangeable with the full role family in any of these domains.
- **IT candidates:** D001 contains software analysts/testers; D002 data-platform/pipeline engineers; D004 cyber defence/governance; D005 cloud infrastructure/DevOps and support; D007 interaction/visual design; D008 network/telecom; D009 statistics/data-AI engineering; D140 robotics engineering/technicians; D056 blockchain software. General IT infrastructure does not establish each specialty.
- **Life-science candidates:** D041 research separates quantitative science from laboratory/field technicians; D142 is computational biology/bioinformatics; D027 contains life-science research and laboratory/health-associate roles; D047 spans agricultural/life scientists, farm technicians and production; D119 animal breeders/dairy farmers; D121 forestry/wildlife; D122 health-associate laboratory roles. Course-to-task and professional-boundary checks are required before any role is shown as a route.
- **Industrial Mathematics candidates:** D014 includes actuaries, risk analysts and claims specialists; D009 includes statisticians/data engineers; D001 software development; D012 FinTech software/operations; D003 ML/computational-intelligence engineers. Mathematical modelling alone does not establish actuarial practice, software engineering or AI development.
- **Plant/biotech candidates:** D138 biomedical systems engineers, D143 forensic investigators/technicians, D048 food scientists and operators, D025 licensed pharmacists and support staff, and D122 medical laboratory roles have different prerequisites and tasks. Biological coursework cannot be inherited across those occupational boundaries.
- **Industrial Chemistry and SLT candidates:** D055 contains process engineers, plant technicians and machine operators; D093 electrical trades; D016 electrical engineers; D017 mechanical engineers; D020 environmental engineering; D074 safety inspectors; D026 public-health/inspection roles. Laboratory exposure or chemical-process content alone does not demonstrate engineering licensure, electrical installation, environmental engineering or public-health practice.
- **Information Systems candidates:** D001 software lifecycle roles, D002 data pipelines, D003 ML/AI, D004 security, D007 interaction design, D009 data science, D010 BI/data systems, and D032 project/programme management are separate role families. INS 204/207 are useful foundational anchors but not enough to claim all of them.

**Disposition:** preserve only the current map’s explicit links as unverified candidates; do not restore quarantined candidates from the pre-fail-closed snapshot. Botany’s D152 (and other anchorless current links) and the unscoped SLT specializations are student-display concerns for the next correction pass, but this user-requested tranche is append-only and does not edit the canonical map.
