Relevant files: `programme_mapping.json` row 145; `jamb_curriculum_scopes_2026-08-17.json` row 145; `jamb_domain_map.json` key `COMPUTER SCIENCE WITH ECONOMICS`; `ccmas/computing.json`; `ccmas/social_sciences.json`.
