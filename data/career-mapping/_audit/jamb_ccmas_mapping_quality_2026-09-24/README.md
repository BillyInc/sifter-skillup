# JAMB–CCMAS mapping-quality audit checkpoint

## Scope and decision rules

This audit covers the JAMB-to-CCMAS curriculum bridge and the resulting programme-to-domain links. It does not include the Skills Matcher UI or general downstream item enrichment. Use CCMAS source course titles, course contents and learning outcomes as the curriculum evidence. University-level curricula are not required. Use authoritative external evidence only where CCMAS has no suitable programme or does not provide adequate evidence. Preserve JAMB source faculty and institution offering context; identical display names are not sufficient reason to merge contexts.

Do not accept `reviewed`, `ready_partial`, or a keyword-selected scope as proof by itself. For every partial or combined JAMB mapping, verify the course codes and outcomes against the source CCMAS records, account for every substantive part of the JAMB name, and retain evidence for each domain link and match label. Distinguish confirmed defects from candidates awaiting source review.

## Implementation mechanism risk

`_build_jamb_curriculum_scopes.py` selects partial CCMAS courses by name-derived terms matched against course titles/content summaries, and `supported_links()` copies the parent link's `match_type` into the scoped link rather than recalculating that classification from the selected curriculum. This is useful for candidate discovery but cannot, by itself, establish that the selected course set is complete or that inherited direct/functional labels remain correct. The audit must join the selected code/title back to its CCMAS source record and inspect outcomes before resolving a row.
