# Rows 001–140: unanchored non-combined link review increment 006

Audit date: 2026-09-24  
Scope: first 25 saved-scope links without a JAMB course-title locator outside the combined rows, ordered by one-based JAMB row then domain ID. Rows 3, 5, 9 and 10 are full-parent scopes. For those rows, I reviewed the complete matching CCMAS parent package (including elective records), rather than interpreting an absent per-link title locator as absent curriculum. No shared mapping, seed, UI, or source data was edited.

## Count reconciliation

The tranche audit states 727/1,383 saved-scope links lack a `scope_course_titles` locator. Recounting rows 1–140 from the saved scope gives 727 missing locators: **292 are in the 19 combined rows and 435 are outside them**. The 418 combined links refers to the combined rows across all 420 JAMB identities, not the 1–140 tranche. Thus `727 − 418 = 309` mixes the tranche numerator with the all-420 combined count; it does not produce the rows 1–140 remainder. I used the requested deterministic row/domain ordering and reviewed the first 25 outside-combined occurrences. The 309 figure should be reconciled before planning tranche-wide remaining counts.

## Scope evidence reviewed

- Row 3 Common and Islamic Law: full CCMAS `Law::Common and Islamic Law` package, `data/career-mapping/ccmas/law.json`, 75 course records. Relevant compulsory outcomes include PUL 101/102 legal method and research/material use; PUL 301/302 criminal law and criminal-justice administration; ICL 203 Islamic criminal law; and the clinical/moot-court practice courses. The course catalogue also contains elective records titled *Criminology* and *Law of Intellectual Property* (8 units each), but those records have no parsed learning outcomes. They are elective catalogue leads, not proof every student takes them or learns the complete specialist practice.
- Rows 5 and 9 Criminology and Security Studies: same full CCMAS CSS package, `data/career-mapping/ccmas/social_sciences.json`, 34 courses. It explicitly covers criminology, criminal justice, security studies, research/statistics, policing systems, criminal procedure/evidence, correctional services, forensic science, intelligence/investigation, security policy, internship, and humanitarian emergency/disaster management. Key course outcomes include CSS 203 comparative policing; CSS 204 applied statistics; CSS 301 crime theory/corrections; CSS 303 criminology/security research methods; CSS 304 intelligence/investigation management; CSS 305 forensic science; CSS 306 field internship; CSS 402 security-sector governance; CSS 404 humanitarian/disaster management. A shared CCMAS parent does not collapse the two JAMB source identities or their faculty/offering contexts.
- Row 10 Demography and Social Statistics: full CCMAS DSS package, `data/career-mapping/ccmas/social_sciences.json`, 38 courses. It has population/demographic data, social statistics, census methods, demographic-data evaluation, mapping/cartography, population rates and life tables, statistical software, research/data collection, estimation and projections, and advanced social statistics (DSS 303–306, 401–406). This is strong evidence for demographic/social analysis, not actuarial insurance or general data engineering.
