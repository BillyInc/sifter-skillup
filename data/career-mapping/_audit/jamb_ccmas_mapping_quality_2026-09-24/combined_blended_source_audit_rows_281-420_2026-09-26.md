## Scope and decision rule

The saved scope JSON has only four explicit `combined_ccmas_curriculum` records in this tranche (rows 290, 306, 337 and 338); it gives all four zero selected courses. The remaining identities include genuine combined facets, single CCMAS packages whose established title happens to contain a conjunction, variants/aliases, and offerings whose institution-specific composition is not established.

The review cross-checks the current JAMB identity/faculty and saved component/course scope against the prior tranche checkpoints, then uses NUC course titles, content/outcomes, and offering-specific official or authoritative curriculum evidence where available. Exact JAMB university cells not confirmed in the cited source remain explicitly unresolved. JAMB PDF identity listings alone do not prove what an individual university teaches. No unsupported component is promoted by title similarity.
