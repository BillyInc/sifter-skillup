## External learning evidence where CCMAS does not establish professional practice

The Chartered Institute of Linguists (CIOL) describes its Level 6 Certificate in Translation as evidence of practical working-level translation skills, with general and business/commercial or government/public-service translation units. CIOL separately explains interpreting as spoken, often two-way language transfer and says professional interpreters commonly have specific training or qualifications. These authoritative professional-body sources corroborate that translation and interpreting are distinct skills and that one literary translation course is not equivalent to interpreter preparation. They are international benchmark/learning leads, **not a Nigerian legal requirement or a recommendation that this user/programme must obtain a particular CIOL qualification**.
