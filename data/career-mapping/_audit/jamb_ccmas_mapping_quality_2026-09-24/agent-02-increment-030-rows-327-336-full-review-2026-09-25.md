## Evidence and candidate disposition

- **327 Building Technology.** The CCMAS Building course slice is substantive: it includes materials, building graphics/construction, measurement, building services, safety, construction planning, contracts, BIM and construction robotics. But the JAMB identity occurs in the Sciences parser output, with only one token; the required direct JAMB programme/institution table confirmation is absent. Candidate domains: D052 Construction Management (direct), D018 Civil, D021 Architecture, D063 Economics, D156 Surveying (functional). This curriculum contains construction-management courses and a surveying course, but that alone does not qualify a student for all civil-engineering, architecture, economics or licensed-surveyor roles. D063 is especially overbroad: construction finance/cost planning are not general Economics preparation. Do not treat the broad five-domain set as confirmed student routes.
- **328 Chemical Sciences.** The scope is the Sciences CCMAS Chemistry component with general, physical, organic, inorganic, analytical, environmental, statistical and research courses. JAMB source occurrence is Sciences, but its five parser tokens do not establish the exact offering institutions or which institutions use this curriculum. Candidate links are D041 Research, D155 Nanotechnology/Advanced Materials, D141 Quantum Computing, D020 Environmental Engineering, D055 Chemical Engineering and D025 Pharmacy (all functional). Chemistry is a prerequisite/foundation in many of these areas, not a complete engineering, pharmacy or quantum-computing learning path. None of those broad domains is accepted without relevant course outcomes and descendant-role evidence. Particular concern: one selected course is COS 101 Introduction to Computing Sciences, but it does not itself support the computing-adjacent paths implied by other mapped domains.
- **329 Electronics.** The mapped CCMAS component is Physics with Electronics, yet the selected scope contains only PHY 422 Digital Electronics. NUC states this course teaches logic families, flip-flops, and building/troubleshooting digital circuits. It is direct evidence for a limited electronics learning topic, not a full Electronics degree curriculum. Current functional links D019 Renewable Energy Engineering, D093 Electrical Installation/Wiring, D059 Space Technology, D050 Industrial Automation all require task-specific evidence; a single digital-electronics course does not establish energy systems, installation practice/licensing, space systems or industrial automation. The JAMB occurrence has three parsed tokens and lacks PDF-verified school associations.
