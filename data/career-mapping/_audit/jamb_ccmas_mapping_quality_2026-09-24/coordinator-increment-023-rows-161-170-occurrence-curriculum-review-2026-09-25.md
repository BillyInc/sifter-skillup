## Scope and category findings

- Rows 161 and 168 use one component reference for combined Marine Biology / Marine Science and Plant Biology / Biotechnology titles; component unions need validation rather than inference from a broad course sample.
- Rows 162–164 contain broad Physics courses with varying electronics/industrial/applied facets. The downstream role lists are much broader than the named course anchors. Row 165 is especially thin (11 courses and one saved Chemical Engineering link).
- Row 169’s six-course zoology selection and row 170’s single explicit Molecular Biology course do not establish complete curricula for the named programmes.
- Rows 163 and 167 show direct/adjacent statuses that need to be justified against exact domain tasks. No path is promoted based on title similarity or generic occupational association.
