## Outcome

### Confirmed

- For the University of Ibadan Russian Combined Honours route, the phrase “with one of French / German” means **Russian plus either French or German**, as alternatives. It does not establish a required three-language Russian+French+German combination.
- UI’s department-level material confirms Russian, French and German are all taught in the same department and that some course listings span the language units.

### Still unresolved

- No accessible official UI source found in this pass documents a complete, variant-specific Russian+French or Russian+German curriculum with required/elective status and learning outcomes. The department’s grouped course list is insufficient for that purpose.
- The row-287 JAMB title alone does not select which one-companion variant a particular student follows. Do not merge all three language CCMAS scopes, and do not treat the current French-only local scope as a confirmed complete row-287 curriculum.
- The existing JAMB table/cell and NUC course/outcome checks remain exactly as recorded in the linked prior checkpoints; this addendum makes no claim to close those separate evidence items.

**Disposition:** slash semantics are resolved for UI as a one-of alternative; the exact applicable course/outcome scope remains unresolved and must stay distinct by companion-language variant until an official UI programme handbook, curriculum, or equivalent course-outcome source is obtained.
