**No downstream remapping, taxonomy edits, seed/SQL generation, or shared JSON edits were made.** “Confirmed gap” means the checked-in scope fails to represent a source-established curriculum facet or uses a contradicted component; it does not authorize a particular downstream domain/role. “Candidate” means exact offering structure or course/outcome fit is still unresolved. Proposed student paths remain unpublished until scope and source gates close.

## Audit rules and result

- A conjunction, slash, “with,” or “and” is a discovery signal—not proof of a full second degree. The source may describe alternatives, options, a legacy title, a combined award, a specialization, or multiple institution-specific offerings.
- JAMB’s programme-name directory establishes an advertised identity, but not the identity’s offering institution or detailed curriculum. Institution lists copied from flattened/OCR extraction remain leads until the row-bound source table is checked.
- CCMAS course titles, contents and learning outcomes are the primary curriculum basis. When the right programme is not in CCMAS, use an authoritative, item-specific external curriculum; a related university’s curriculum is not transferable without an exact offering link.
- Each component’s owner and source locator must be kept distinct. A combined scope must identify which course/outcome rows support which title facet; a zero-course combined scope is not an evidenced blend.
- The existing row-332 reconciliation is respected: D_custom, zero CCMAS component, zero selected courses and zero student paths remain in place. NUC Environmental Management/Environmental Standards and NOUN Environmental Science & Resource Management are candidates only, not equivalents.

**Result:** The register separates confirmed scope/source defects from unresolved curriculum and exact-context candidates. It also includes all 13 repeated-faculty identity records identified in the tranche review. Entries overlap; row groups are not unique-identity totals.
