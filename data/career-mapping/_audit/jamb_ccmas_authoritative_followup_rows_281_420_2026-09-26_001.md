## Append-only source-detail addendum — foreign-language source and course cross-check

The following primary-source checks were completed after checkpoint 001 was first saved; they supplement rather than replace its open items.

Source: [JAMB IBASS Arts brochure](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/brochure-degree-arts.pdf), table header `COURSE/DEGREE AWARDING INSTITUTIONS`, printed p.167; query checked 2026-09-26. Row 281 remains an across-page cell whose full list still needs precise visual confirmation.
- The same official IBASS table text shows the foreign-language entries: `GERMAN — IBADAN, LAGOS, OAU, UNN`; `PORTUGUESE: OAU`; `RUSSIAN — IBADAN, LAGOS, UNN`; and `RUSSIAN WITH FRENCH/GERMAN — IBADAN` (printed page footer p.204). Thus the Russian-with-combination row is not `French and German/Russian`; the base is Russian, and French/German is the companion expression. The exact meaning of the slash (alternative combinations versus a combined variant) is not resolved by the extracted line alone. It does resolve the canonical name mismatch and contradicts the current French-only scope for that row.
- For row 284 French, the authoritative UNIZIK Modern European Languages page identifies itself as a four-year regular B.A. (Hons) French programme and enumerates actual course codes/titles. It supports the 59-row local list as a strong candidate source (e.g. FRE 111/112/121/131/141 and FRE 361/362/461/463; UNIZIK page lines 105–354, accessed 2026-09-26), but must not be assumed applicable to every institution in JAMB's nationwide French row. The University of Ibadan-hosted Modern European Languages (French) CCMAS segment describes the curriculum's phonetics/phonology, oral/written comprehension, grammar, literature and creative/practical writing (PDF printed pp.1–3); it explicitly names translation/interpreting, bilingual journalism, diplomacy and international tourism as graduate skill areas. This supports French translation as a direct learning-to-task path and diplomacy as a plausible functional use, but the local active link record needs explicit course-level anchors and cannot inherit the UI segment as an identical course schedule for every French offering. Sources: [UNIZIK B.A. French course page](https://unizik.edu.ng/faculties/arts/departments/modern-european-languages/4-years-programme/); [UI-hosted French CCMAS segment](https://ui.edu.ng/sites/default/files/100%20LEVEL%20CCMAS%20FRENCH.pdf), accessed 2026-09-26.
- The UNIZIK page and UI-hosted French segment do not prove all 59 course rows have item-specific outcomes stored locally. The current 59 selected rows lack `source_ccmas_file` and per-course `learning_outcomes` in the generated scope. Therefore row 284's local course evidence contract remains incomplete even though primary external curriculum sources were located.
