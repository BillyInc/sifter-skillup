# JAMB combined/partial curriculum scope audit — rows 001–140

Status: incremental checkpoint, started 2026-09-26. This is a new, row-indexed
audit report. It is deliberately separate from canonical maps, scope JSON,
overrides, generators, seeds and SQL; none of those are edited by this audit.
Rows are one-based positions in `data/career-mapping/programme_mapping.json`.

## Audit standard and evidence status

For each identity, retain separate JAMB catalogue/source-faculty occurrences
and verify an offering context against the exact JAMB programme/institution
table cell. Then compare the current selected CCMAS course codes, titles,
content and learning outcomes against every substantive programme facet.
Where CCMAS does not supply a named facet, use an authoritative external
curriculum only after recording its exact URL, locator, access date and scope.
Reassess downstream domain links only from the resulting course evidence;
subgroup/role descendants remain unconfirmed unless their tasks are evidenced.

Status meanings:

- **Confirmed current-scope defect**: the checked-in identity/scope records
  demonstrably omit, conflate, duplicate or misstate a required curriculum
  facet/source record. This does not by itself prescribe a full-component
  union or prove an offering's institution cell.
- **Evidence needed**: a plausible gap or context difference, but exact
  occurrence/course evidence is insufficient to call the mapping wrong.
- **No gap established**: current evidence does not establish an omitted
  curriculum facet; descendants may still need review.

Important source-integrity limitation: the occurrence inventory dated
2026-09-25 says most institution tokens are parser output and
`offering_row_verified_against_pdf=false`; therefore those school lists are
not used as verified award contexts. JAMB programme-index pages establish
catalogue names/faculty blocks, not the individual institution association.
Only explicit exact printed table-row checks below are called verified.
