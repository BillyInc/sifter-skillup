Welcome to ANAN

Welcome to ANAN

The Association of National Accountants of Nigeria (ANAN) was founded with a bold vision to redefine the accounting profession in Nigeria through integrity, innovation, and world-class professionalism. Established in 1979 and Chartered by Act 76 of 1993, ANAN has grown into a premier professional accountancy organization, shaping the future of financial expertise in Nigeria and beyond.

With a commitment to its Vision of making ANAN a premium brand of choice in professional accounting practice in Nigeria and to impact on accounting education and practice in a profound and comprehensive manner, and its Mission of advancing the science of Accountancy in Nigeria, pioneering a multi-disciplinary emphasis in the production of well-rounded, well-blended, and well-honed professionals, profound in knowledge, skillful in practice and ethical in conduct, ANAN operates a world-class Nigerian College of Accountancy (NCA), where aspiring professionals receive cutting-edge training, and , ANAN University, a specialized institution dedicated to accounting education and research. This two institutions provide advanced learning opportunities to develop future industry leaders. ANAN remains the only accountancy body with a residential college and university in the world.

ANAN has over 58,000 nominal members in 47 branches across the length and breadth of Nigeria, UK, Cameroon, and Cote d’Ivoire. Our members, known as Certified National Accountants (CNAs), play critical roles in government, corporate organizations, and global financial institutions, driving transparency and economic growth. ANAN has made astute strides both in the National and International fronts, ANAN is an active Board Member of Pan African Federation of Accountants (PAFA), Council Members of Accountancy Bodies in West Africa (ABWA) and Association of Professional Bodies of Nigeria (APBN).

At ANAN, we believe in continuous learning, ethical leadership, and a dynamic approach to accounting practice. Whether you are a student, professional, or industry leader, you are part of a thriving community that is shaping the future of accounting in Nigeria and Globally.

Welcome to ANAN – A Premium Brand of Choice: where the Science of Accountancy is Advanced!

Why become a member

Becoming a member of the Association of National Accountants of Nigeria (ANAN) offers you a pathway to professional excellence and career growth. As a globally recognized accountancy organization, ANAN provides:

  • Professional Recognition - Stand out as a certified accountant with a reputable qualification
  • Career Advancement - Access exclusive job opportunities, training, and mentorship programs
  • Continuous Learning – Stay updated with industry trends through workshops, webinars, seminars, and MCPD programs.
  • Networking & Community – ANAN creates platform to connect with a vast network of professionals and industry leaders.
  • Ethical & Standard Compliance – Uphold the highest standards of accounting ethics and practice.

Our Partners

ANAN collaborates with esteemed global organizations to uphold international accounting standards, enhance professional development, and foster knowledge exchange. Through these partnerships, we ensure global recognition, strategic collaborations, and access to international opportunities for our members.

  • CITN – Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria
  • CIFCFIN - Chartered Institute of Forensics and Certified Fraud Investigators of Nigeria
  • CPA – The Institute of Certified Public Accountants in Ireland
  • CIMA – Certified Institute of Management Accountants
  • IPA Australia – Institute of Public Accountants Australia
  • SAIPA – South African Institute of Professional Accountants
  • IFA UK – Institute of Financial Accountants, United Kingdom
  • CITM – Chartered Institute of Treasury Management
