WHO WE ARE

The Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (CILT) is the leading International Professional body for all sectors of the Transport and Logistics Industry. Founded in the United Kingdom in 1919 and granted a Royal Charter on the 26th of November, 1926, the CILT was established to promote knowledge of the science and art of Logistics and Transport and to provide a source of authoritative views for communication to government, industry and the community at large. The CILT International headquarters is in the United Kingdom charged with the function of finance and administration while CILT Hong Kong and CILT Republic of Ireland are in charge of membership and education respectively.

2026 CILT NATIONAL CONFERENCE/AGM

Join us Today!

Transport and logistics systems are the backbone of national economic development, trade competitiveness, and social integration. Efficient and resilient transport infrastructure enables the movement of goods, services, and people, thereby facilitating economic productivity and national prosperity. In Nigeria, however, challenges such as infrastructure deficits, congestion, fragmented logistics networks, policy inconsistencies, and limited multimodal integration continue to constrain the efficiency of the transport and logistics sector.
The conference marks an important milestone in the roadmap towards achieving sustainable innovations in global logistics and transport operations with Africa in focus. This year conference will hold at the Nicon Luxury Hotel, Abuja.
