Examination / Certifications

CIPM administers online examinations which hold four times a year. Candidates must have been admitted as student members at least one month before the examination date.

The examining process is in 5 stages, which are:
Foundation,
Intermediate I,
Intermediate II,
Professional I and
Professional II.
