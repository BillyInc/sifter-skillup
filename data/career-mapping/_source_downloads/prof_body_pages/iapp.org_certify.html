Certification

Protect your enterprise, advance your career and increase your earning potential with IAPP certification.

AI Governance Professional - AIGP

Executing responsible AI governance

Establish foundational knowledge of AI systems, their impacts and responsible principles and demonstrate an understanding how current and emerging laws apply to AI systems.

AIGP is for professionals in all industries who need to understand and execute responsible AI governance.

Learn more

Certified Information Privacy Professional - CIPP

Practicing privacy

The Certified Information Privacy Professional — or CIPP — is the premier global credential for privacy and data protection.

The IAPP offers five distinct CIPP designation concentrations, demonstrating mastery in privacy laws, regulations and frameworks for the specific regions of Asia, Canada, China, Europe and the U.S.

Learn more

Certified Information Privacy Manager - CIPM

Operationalizing privacy

Demonstrate leadership and expertise in privacy program administration and management. 

CIPM is for global professionals responsible for integrating privacy requirements into business operations.

Learn more

Certified Information Privacy Technologist - CIPT

Engineering privacy

Show your ability to use technology in building data protection practices into products and services.

CIPT is for professionals responsible for information technology, information security, software engineering and privacy by design.

Learn more

Dedicated designations

Advance your privacy profile with a dedicated designation from the IAPP.

Privacy Law Specialist

Instantly placing you among the elite in privacy law, the Privacy Law Specialist designation verifies that you have met the stringent experience and knowledge requirements in this crucial and rapidly growing area of the law.

PLS designation

Fellow of Information Privacy

Have you achieved a CIPP designation, as well as the CIPM or the CIPT? Apply now to become an FIP and signify your comprehensive knowledge of privacy laws, privacy program management and data protection practices.

FIP designation

ANAB accreditation

The gold standard in privacy certifications

CIPM, CIPP/E, CIPP/US and CIPT credentials are accredited by the ANSI National Accreditation Board under the International Organization for Standardization 17024: 2012.
