- Visible domains audited: 160

- Domains with a verified web anchor: 160
- Additional hidden trade/creative rows audited separately: 7
- Hidden rows with international-only local-body evidence: 1 (funeral)

## Evidence Rules

A regulator can support scope, registration, licensing or examination claims. A professional association can support professional terminology and development signals. A government or sector page can support sector context, but does not create a licence or credential. Global taxonomies remain separate from Nigerian evidence.

## Coverage Interpretation

All 160 visible domains have at least one Nigerian body or sector-context entry in the existing body audit. This does not mean all 160 have a Nigerian statutory regulator, or that every body publishes a role dictionary. It means the local evidence layer has a source category that can be shown with the correct limitation. The seven hidden trade/creative rows are now also represented in `supplemental_hidden_domain_coverage`; funeral services remains explicitly international-only for this search.

The existing source log contained 28 entries. The focused audit adds 10 direct records, bringing the verified anchor set to 42. The original 15-anchor checkpoint is therefore historical source-work context, not the total source count. A linked source is an evidence anchor, not proof that every role under the linked domain has been fully extracted.
