# Programme → domain → subgroup → role guidance contract

Prepared 2026-09-12 from the original CCMAS document set and the parsed
repository records.

## Source boundary

The original files are stored locally at:

`C:\Users\USER\Downloads\ccmas original documents`

The authoritative public source page is the [NUC CCMAS page](https://www.nuc.edu.ng/ccmas/).
The repository keeps the parsed course records under
`data/career-mapping/ccmas/`. Every generated programme record retains the
parsed source filename and course locators so a reviewer can return to the
original document.

## The same shape at every layer

### Programme

The programme explains:

- what the curriculum covers;
- what the student may practise or produce;
- the order in which the recorded capabilities make sense;
- the meaning and use of any explicitly named tool;
- programme-owned reading or learning references;
- what is Nigerian curriculum evidence and what is only an optional global
  learning aid.

The programme does not claim that a student is already competent, that an open
reference is prescribed by every university, or that a programme-level
certification is required when the CCMAS record does not say that.

### Domain

The domain owns its foundation. It is synthesized from domain-owned evidence
and curriculum evidence, not from the frequency of role skills. Each item says
what it means in that domain, why it appears in the learning sequence, what it
connects to, and which public resource explains it.

### Subgroup

The subgroup adds a narrower method or area of work. It inherits the useful
domain foundation but does not repeat it as if it were subgroup-specific. Its
skills, tools, competencies, certifications and resources explain what the
area adds and how they are used together.

### Role

The role explains the work, outputs, methods, tools, competencies and any
qualification or professional pathway recorded in role evidence. A role tool
is not silently promoted to a domain requirement. Where Nigeria-specific and
global evidence differ, the display says so.

## Item explanation contract

Every visible skill, tool, competency or certification row can carry:

- plain meaning;
- contextual use;
- expected output;
- priority and why it has that priority;
- prerequisites and related items;
- source references and source locators;
- geographic scope: Nigeria, global, or Nigeria-to-global;
- evidence limitations;
- whether the row is an option, a reference, a professional pathway, or a
  demonstrated requirement.

The API reads these rows through `career_context_items`. The UI renders them
with `ContextualItemList`, while clickable links remain in the scoped
`career_learning_resources` collection.

## Reading and free-learning policy

Each of the 237 parsed programmes now receives:

1. an official NUC CCMAS source-page link with the original parsed filename
   and course locators; and
2. a discipline-appropriate open foundation reference.

These are not presented as prescribed Nigerian university books. Sociology is
the only programme that currently has a separately reviewed reading set:
Haralambos/Holborn and Giddens are publisher/library references, while OpenStax
is the free open-text alternative. Other programme-specific books require a
manual reading-list review against the relevant original CCMAS document or an
institution’s published reading list before they can be labelled as books used
for that programme.

The all-programme migration is generated by
`data/career-mapping/_build_programme_guidance_catalog.py` and recorded in
`data/career-mapping/PROGRAMME_GUIDANCE_CATALOG_MANIFEST.json`.
