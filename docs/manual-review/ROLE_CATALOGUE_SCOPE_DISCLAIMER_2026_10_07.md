# Role catalogue scope disclaimer

The roles shown are source-backed examples identified in the current catalogue;
they are not a complete list of every occupation available in a domain or area
of specialization. More roles may be added as occupational evidence and local
labour-market evidence are reviewed.

Global role evidence and Nigerian context are shown separately. A Nigerian
context note may describe local availability, institutions, regulation, or
employer practice; it does not replace the broader occupational definition.
Job boards such as Adzuna or Jobberman may provide demand context, but a job
advertisement alone does not establish a globally recognized occupation or
prove that a role belongs to a domain or area of specialization.

Students should use the displayed sources as a starting point, check current
employer and professional requirements, and carry out their own research
before choosing a learning or career path.
