# Next Six Domains: Bounded Source-Enrichment Plan

**Prepared:** 2026-10-29  
**Scope:** D006 IoT and Embedded Systems; D086 Gambling, Betting and Lottery Operations (currently cataloged as “Gaming, Betting and Lottery Operations”); D139 Video Game Design and Development (currently cataloged as “Game Design, Development”); D141 Quantum Computing and Quantum Information; D167 XR and Immersive Technology; D168 Esports and Creator Economy.  
**Purpose:** Produce source-backed learning-content evidence for the already reconciled domain/area/role structure, at D078-like depth, without reopening taxonomy or allowing source searches to expand indefinitely.

## Decisions this plan locks

- Work from the six canonical domains and 25 reconciled areas listed in the domain briefs. The current area catalog and role routes are the fixed boundary for this pass.
- Treat the 24 family-by-horizon cells per area as **disposition destinations**, not 24 independent searches. Across the 25 areas there are 600 cells to reconcile, but evidence packets should answer multiple named cells and task tracks at once.
- Do not impose a source-count quota. Source volume is not a quality measure. A claim is sufficiently supported when the source is fit for that claim, its context and limitations are recorded, and the needed independent corroboration is present for consequential or contested claims.
- Reuse existing source cards first. D006 (3 areas), D086 (4), D141 (4), and D167 (4) already have bounded area source indexes marked complete within their scopes. Do not repeat their general area searches. Their main remaining work is reconciliation of evidence into item/context/role-task records and any specifically named unresolved gaps. D139 (6 areas) and D168 (4) need their initial bounded area evidence packets.
- A source-backed finding may propose a correction to an area boundary or role route, but it does not authorize changing taxonomy, ownership, role fit, routes, student visibility, or graph status in this enrichment pass. Send such a finding to the coordinator as a scoped proposal with evidence.
- Keep `conditional` routes active and explain the condition/task where the occupation is useful. Conditional means useful in a specific context; it does not mean hidden, rejected, or universally required.

## Work model for two agents plus coordinator

For each domain, give the matching domain brief to two agents. They own disjoint areas so they do not repeat one another's source searches:

1. **Agent A** owns the odd-numbered areas in that domain brief.
2. **Agent B** owns the even-numbered areas.
3. **Coordinator (me)** owns the shared domain foundation/prerequisite synthesis, the cross-area connections, the merge, and the final quality/closure review. The coordinator does not ask agents to re-search a source family already indexed; shared domain claims are crosswalked from agent packets after area work.

For D006's three areas, Agent A owns area 1 and Agent B owns areas 2–3. For every other domain, the odd/even split is balanced. If agents work on several domains concurrently, keep the same division and separate outputs by domain and area ID. Do not have both agents edit the same canonical index or merge one another's files directly. Each returns an append-only evidence packet and a gap/disposition table; the coordinator integrates after both are complete.

## Evidence units and finite process

### Fixed area matrix

For each area, disposition these six item families at four maturity horizons: foundational/current, established, emerging, and research-frontier/future-facing.

1. Knowledge and principles
2. Methods and processes
3. Techniques, models and analytical approaches
4. Competencies and skills
5. Tools, platforms and infrastructure
6. Credentials, standards and regulatory practice

Each of the 24 cells ends as one of:

- `mapped`: one or more evidenced learning items, with source locators, context, inputs/outputs or use, assumptions/limits, level placement and supported relationships.
- `not_applicable`: a short boundary-based reason showing why that family/horizon does not represent a meaningful learning item in this area.
- `bounded_no_hit`: only after a dated, logged search of the appropriate source families and a clear statement of the search limits. A failed fetch or paywall is an access limit, never a no-hit result.

Do not add extra cells, horizons, task tracks or areas. A named source-driven discovery outside this frame is recorded as a proposal for a later decision, not silently used to enlarge the frame.

### Six task tracks per area

Disposition the six tracks from the same area evidence packet: (1) foundations/prerequisites; (2) methods, techniques and alternatives; (3) tools/data/infrastructure; (4) emerging/frontier practice; (5) standards, credentials and learning resources; (6) outputs and downstream role tasks. Each track ends `complete`, `not_applicable` with reason, or `bounded_no_hit` with its search log. Tracks are not six extra searches.

### Three-step bounded area packet

1. **Reconcile before browsing.** Inventory the existing index and route records. List only unresolved cell scopes, task tracks, source-context gaps, and routed role-task claims. Attach existing source/query IDs. Mark questions already answered as covered and remove them from the search list.
2. **One evidence pass, grouped by shared source.** For a previously indexed area, search only its named missing claims. For an area with no bounded index, prepare one packet from official/primary, academic/curriculum, professional/standards/regulatory, and credible industry/practitioner source families as relevant. Group queries by source and question; inspect a source once and map it only to the claims it supports. Allow one narrowly targeted correction batch only where the evidence pass exposes a specific contradiction or material unfilled claim.
3. **One counter-search and freeze.** Run one dated counter-search per area against the actual boundary, likely competing/omitted practices, established alternatives, prerequisites, combinations, and relevant role outputs. Record queries, source families, access limits and result. A material finding reopens only the affected cell, task, or route-context claim. Do not restart the area search.

The searches are therefore driven by named gaps, not by 600-cell arithmetic. Existing indexed evidence that fits the claim counts. A new source counts only after its claim-level evidence has been recorded and mapped. No broad "search more" task is allowed.

## Source quality and claim recording

Use the source family that can actually establish the claim:

- Official standards bodies, regulators, maintained technical documentation and primary datasets for standards, legal/regulatory practice, technical interfaces, tools and data access.
- Peer-reviewed research, systematic/scoping reviews, textbooks or university curricula for methods, theory, evidence strength, assumptions and prerequisites.
- Professional associations and qualification bodies for professional competencies and credentials.
- Employer/practitioner material and credible industry sources for actual workflows, role tasks, production constraints and current practice; treat vendor marketing as a claim to verify, not neutral evidence.
- Grey literature is allowed when relevant and credible. Record author/publisher, expertise or institutional standing, publication date, method/transparency, conflicts or commercial interest, and limits. A source being open or popular does not make it reliable.

Every evidence record must include: stable source ID; title; author/publisher; URL; exact locator (section/page/table/API endpoint); publication/version and access date; source family and geography/context; the specific claim supported; the learning item's meaning and where it is used; inputs, process and output where relevant; assumptions, maturity and limits; target domain/area/role-task IDs; relationships to prerequisites, complements or alternatives; and confidence/remaining gap. Separate the item's identity from a software/tool used to perform it. Do not promote a method because a tool supports it, or a tool because a method mentions it.

For meaningful learning items, capture enough context that a later graph designer can explain: what the item is; why it belongs at domain or area level; what it builds on; how it is used in this programme/area/task; what output it produces; where that output is used downstream; whether it is broadly useful or conditional; and what can be learned alongside it versus what is a true alternative or prerequisite. Explicit dependency edges require evidence or a structural reason intrinsic to the method/workflow. Mere association or shared use does not establish a blocker.

## Domain-to-role placement checks

- Keep genuinely shared foundations at domain level only when evidence shows they apply across multiple canonical areas; otherwise keep them in their specific area.
- Place broad knowledge, competencies, processes and tools separately where their learning purpose differs. Include foundational, current, modern, emerging and future-facing candidates when supported; label maturity honestly.
- For each routed role, inspect the canonical area route and document the actual task(s) supported by the area items. Apply conditions to conditional routes. One role may link to several areas when its tasks warrant it; do not flatten those routes into one universal role profile.
- Identify complements, alternatives and downstream uses based on workflow evidence. Never imply every alternative must be learned, or that a compatible combination is a prerequisite.
- Role evidence may explain application and route usefulness; it must not be used alone to define an area's boundary or create a new specialization.
- Source-backed evidence that challenges a role/area route is returned as a separately labeled proposal. Do not change fit, area ownership, route count, hidden/visible state or role inventory in the source packet.

## Closure gate: all conditions required

An area is closed for this bounded source-enrichment pass only when:

1. Its canonical boundary and expected outputs have appropriate source support independent of role-frequency aggregation.
2. All 24 matrix cells have terminal dispositions, with locators, context and limits for mapped claims.
3. All six task tracks have terminal dispositions; all source and query references resolve.
4. Applicable prerequisites, alternatives, complements, combinations, outputs and downstream task contexts are documented, with unsupported dependencies left out.
5. Every routed role has an evidence disposition for its area-specific tasks; conditional task contexts remain explicit and conditional roles remain usable.
6. One dated, bounded counter-search is recorded with no material unaddressed gap, or the affected record is repaired and only that bounded check repeated.

"Closed" means complete within the logged search scope, not universally exhaustive. Do not close an evidence gap by hiding an item, excluding a role, broadening its scope, or using `not_applicable` without a boundary reason. Do not keep searching after the gate passes. Reopen only for a new material source, a changed standard/tool, a concrete contradiction, or a counterexample to a mapped claim.

## Merge and coordinator verification

Each agent returns one packet per assigned area with: (a) pre-search gap inventory; (b) evidence/source additions with claim limits; (c) all 24 cell dispositions; (d) six task-track dispositions; (e) role-task/context map; (f) prerequisite/alternative/complement/output relationships; (g) counter-search log; (h) unresolved/proposed taxonomy or route issues kept separate; and (i) exact files and validation performed.

Coordinator merge is a deduplicating integration, not another discovery phase:

1. Deduplicate by canonical URL/version and source ID; retain separate locators/claims where one source supports different contexts.
2. Check all evidence-to-cell/task/role references, no unsupported claims, no role or area changes, and consistency of conditional routes.
3. Check that method/tool/knowledge/skill/credential types remain distinct and that prerequisites are not inferred from simple relationship mentions.
4. Reconcile shared domain items only after comparing their use across the fixed areas. Items useful in one or a few areas are useful edges, not compulsory domain foundations.
5. Run the existing focused validators and add a finite packet-integrity validator only if an existing one does not cover references/counts; do not create a new search framework.
6. Produce one integrated checkpoint with terminal counts and exact remaining blockers. No graph, sequence, UI, SQL or migrations are changed by these source prompts.
