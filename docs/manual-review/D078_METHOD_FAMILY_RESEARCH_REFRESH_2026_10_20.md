## How to read the route and relationships

- **Traditional** names the established design, analytic procedure, or model family relevant to that method.
- **Modern/ML** records a verified contemporary extension where one exists. It does not imply that every method needs ML or that ML replaces the traditional method.
- **Placement and role use** preserves ownership: broadly reusable methods/techniques remain domain-owned and can be projected into supported areas and roles; a specialist application stays area-owned. Role routes expose a relevant path through their area's method route; they do not make a domain or area item role-owned.
- All newly added learning items and edges are `resolved_ready_for_use`, selectable/clickable when projected, optional and skippable, with `blocking_policy: never`, no automatic selection and no automatic order. Relationship types describe a supported connection; they do not impose prerequisites. Where an edge says `alternative_to`, its explanation records the specific outcome, design, or analytic goal that makes it a conditional choice.
- Tool cards remain separate from method cards. Tool links say what software can implement the method; they do not make software a prerequisite.

## Relationship decisions

- Pearson, Spearman and Kendall are conditional alternatives for related association questions; the data scale, association target, ties and assumptions determine the choice. They do not estimate causal effects.
- Binary logit and probit are conditional link alternatives for binary outcomes. Ordinal and multinomial regression are separate because their outcome structures differ.
- OLS is a continuous-outcome linear model. Penalized ridge/LASSO/elastic net extend it for regularization/high-dimensional prediction and can be conditionally compared for prediction; their coefficients are not ordinary OLS estimates. Tree ensembles can also compete for predictive performance, not causal interpretation.
- Poisson/negative-binomial selection depends on count distribution/dispersion; the card records the distinction rather than making the pair a fixed sequence.
- EFA can inform a theory-specified CFA, but CFA is only a later test when the sample/design supports independent or held-out evidence. Reliability complements factor/model evidence; it is not validity evidence. IRT and CFA are alternative measurement frameworks only for bounded questions such as comparability, not interchangeable analyses.
- CA and CDA complement one another when a study has both interactional sequence and discourse/power questions; they remain separate analytic traditions. NLP/LLM methods support retrieval or tested measurement but do not replace close interpretation.
- Process tracing explains within-case mechanisms; experiments and quasi-experiments estimate effects under different assumptions. Their relationship is complementary.
- ERGM is a model-based extension of social network analysis. Moran/LISA describes spatial association; spatial regression models spatial dependence. They are linked, but a diagnostic does not automatically select a regression.
- Spatially explicit random forest is area-owned and predictive. The Economics-area time-series and causal-ML records remain area-owned. No area method was duplicated as a domain item and no role fit was changed.
- All method, alternative and tool edges are nonblocking, skippable and have no auto-order or auto-selection behavior.
