## Source-review notes

- For A05, the checked materials distinguish spatial data/software tasks from human-geography interpretation. The QGIS manual has direct exercises for vector analysis, reprojection, spatial statistics and map production. NBS Wave 5 metadata supplies a Nigerian example with sampling, geographic-area identifiers, migration modules and stated coverage. UN migration guidance and UN WPP methodology support migration measurement and projection limits.
- For A06, the open social-science methods text provides pinpoint chapters for design, measurement, sampling, survey and interpretive methods, analysis and ethics. CDC and the Magenta Book connect evaluation questions, programme theory, design, evidence and use. Official Kobo material confirms form logic, offline constraints and export formats. OpenLearn provides a free SPSS introduction; Taguette provides the free qualitative-coding route.
- Free software learning and free software use are distinguished. The SPSS course can be completed without installing SPSS, but real SPSS use may require a licence. NVivo remains a commercial alternative; Taguette is the free route. Data Carpentry's R-for-social-scientists lesson is explicitly marked alpha.
- Nigeria's Data Protection Act is a legal source, not an ethics approval or research-design manual. Study-specific institutional and legal review remains necessary.
