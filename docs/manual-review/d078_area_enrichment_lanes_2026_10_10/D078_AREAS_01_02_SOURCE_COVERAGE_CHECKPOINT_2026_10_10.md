## New actionable source leads

The most useful leads from official and professional sources are:

- **Nigeria data governance:** the NDPC’s Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and FAQs, plus the NBS microdata privacy policy. Use these to ground personal-data, confidentiality and access claims; they do not by themselves settle study-specific ethics approval.
- **Nigeria research ethics:** NHREC’s National Code of Health Research Ethics v7. It is directly applicable to qualifying health research involving people, not a blanket code for all social-science studies. Determine the relevant institutional review path for each project.
- **Qualitative field practice:** OpenLearn’s free course on conducting qualitative interviews, with preparation, consent, interview conduct, fieldnotes and data handling sections.
- **Local economic data:** NBS’s Nigeria Labour Force Survey Q2 2024–Q2 2025 catalogue and documentation as a more recent data-appraisal exercise than the 2018/19 NLSS.
- **Forecasting/nowcasting:** IMF Nowcasting course description and developing-country working paper directly address high-frequency indicators and forecast evaluation. The course has advanced prerequisites. A Nigeria-specific IMF nowcasting paper surfaced, but its exact title/locator was not verified, so it remains a research lead only.
- **Survey AI:** AAPOR’s reports directory exposes an interactive edition of its 2026 responsible-AI report as well as the PDF; the underlying guidance remains professional and U.S.-based.

These are source leads only. No new candidate item or student-facing link was created.
