## Area-specific judgment and boundaries

### Sociology and Social Anthropology

The task set spans theory-informed question framing, ethical field inquiry, interviews/observation, qualitative and mixed analysis, secondary/documentary work, social-survey design and design-aware analysis, and computational/digital research. It includes KoBoToolbox as a field-form option; Taguette and NVivo as distinct qualitative-data organization options; Gephi and R/quanteda routes for network or text tasks; and Python/R only where coding is useful to a particular task. These tools do not decide what a social concept means, produce representative samples, or prove competence.

AI-supported coding and question drafting are labelled **emerging**; synthetic respondent material and LLM-agent simulation are **experimental**. Their output must be tested against human-reviewed evidence and reported with limitations. A simulated response is not a respondent; model-generated themes are not findings without human interpretation and validation. AAPOR guidance supports bounded human-supervised survey uses, not a claim that AI translation performs accurately in Nigerian languages.

### Economics and Applied Economic Analysis

The task set covers economic reasoning, measurement/source appraisal, descriptive and econometric analysis, reproducible R/Python workflows, causal evaluation, inequality/poverty/distribution, forecasting/nowcasting, official macro/labour/fiscal statistics, and computational simulation. R and Python are alternative analysis routes; Mesa is an optional agent-based-modeling framework; Casevo is an **experimental** LLM-agent example, not a validated economic forecasting or policy model.

The **2025 System of National Accounts** is the updated international national-accounts standard, but implementation is transitional. Nigeria’s currently published NBS GDP methodology page identifies 2008 SNA and ISIC Rev. 4. The learner must follow the version and definitions of the actual dataset, not assume that Nigeria has already implemented the updated SNA. The ILO’s labour standards have changed across the 19th, 20th and 21st ICLS; a statistic must be read with the relevant survey round’s definitions and implementation status. The IMF identifies GFSM 2014 as under update, with a target completion date in 2027. These are statistical frameworks and guidance—not individual qualifications or a claim that every economics learner compiles official statistics.

## Nigerian/global balance

Nigeria context is additive and task-relevant: NISER applied work and methods training, NBS microdata and official statistical methods, Afrobarometer Nigerian survey materials, and the Nigerian professional associations. Global methods remain eligible without a Nigerian-branded counterpart. A Nigerian source is not a gate for a globally used method; equally, a global framework does not establish Nigerian law, dataset implementation, local-language accuracy or institutional approval. Nigerian learners can learn global methods/tools and apply them to Nigerian questions/data when access, context, method and data documentation support it.

## Explicit no-hit and unresolved record

- No broadly applicable compulsory certification for either area was found in the checked association, standards, framework and research sources. **No certificate requirement is invented.** This is a bounded source finding, not proof no country or employer ever asks for one.
- No independently verified Nigerian statute establishing NASA as a statutory licensing regulator was found in this pass. The association’s own description is attributed to it; do not present that claim as independently certified legal status.
- No single Nigerian professional competency/task standard covering the full Sociology and Social Anthropology or Economics and Applied Economic Analysis areas was found. NASA and NES provide professional context, not a comprehensive framework.
- No Nigerian version of every global method/tool is expected. The remaining local requirement is to check Nigerian dataset documentation and local language/ethics conditions when a specific task uses them.
- No validated evidence was found to treat Casevo or synthetic LLM respondents as standard economic/social-science practice or as an empirically representative population model. They remain experimental and hidden.
- Source pages, software versions, learning-link availability, licence/access, and publication vintages can change; recheck them before student-facing implementation. Some exact tool paths in the JSON are vendor/developer documentation, not free instruction.
