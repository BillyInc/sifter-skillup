## Curriculum-evidence and offering-context rule (clarified 2026-09-28)

- Use the NUC CCMAS national curriculum as the baseline evidence for curriculum components and programme-to-domain analysis. The fact that CCMAS specifies a 70% national core and institutions may add local content does **not**, by itself, block verification of the national baseline or require obtaining each university's syllabus.
- Keep that national baseline distinct from an institution-specific timetable or curriculum claim. Do not say a particular university teaches every course in a combined CCMAS reference union unless its own source proves that.
- Continue extracting and validating distinct JAMB source offerings: exact programme/title variant, faculty/source context, institution-awarder association, and separate occurrence. JAMB offering evidence establishes those identity/provider facts, not an institution-specific curriculum.
- Use authoritative external curriculum evidence only where CCMAS is absent or materially insufficient; retain item-specific URL, locator, access date, and what it supports. Do not hold a CCMAS-supported national mapping solely for unavailable institution-specific 30% material.
