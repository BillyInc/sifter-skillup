# Role coverage enrichment plan — 2026-10-05

## Purpose

After and alongside the catalogue-wide domain, area-of-specialization and role-fit audit, find domains and specializations whose role coverage is missing or too thin to explain the real work available in that field. Enrich the role catalogue from **global evidence first**, then add **Nigeria-specific role titles or practice variants only where Nigerian evidence directly supports them**. This is separate from proving whether an already-linked role belongs to its current domain.

The current specialization inventory marks D037 Legal Practice, D038 Compliance, Regulation and Governance, D039 Higher Education and Academic Research, and D040 Vocational and Technical Training as having zero active role mappings despite existing subgroup records. These are confirmed coverage-gap candidates, not proof that those domains have no occupations. The full catalogue scan must find all such cases and other branches where the existing role set is implausibly narrow for the documented scope.

## Workflow and evidence standard

1. **Freeze the audited structure first.** Use the reviewed domain definition and the corrected areas of specialization as the frame for checking role coverage. Do not use raw ISCO membership or existing subgroup contents as proof that a role belongs.
2. **Build a deterministic gap queue across the entire catalogue.** Include every domain/specialization with zero active roles; very small role sets; repeated generic roles with no field-specific tasks; and scope themes with no plausible role route. Report role counts and coverage signals separately. Do not impose a universal minimum number of roles: a niche field may legitimately have few established occupations.
3. **Search for globally established occupations.** Use authoritative occupational classifications and primary sources from global professional bodies, regulators, standards organizations, international organizations, and direct employer/professional role frameworks. Match the occupation's actual tasks and required professional context to the parent domain and specialization.
4. **Check for Nigerian-specific roles or variants separately.** Use Nigerian government, regulator, professional-body, official employer, and dated vacancy evidence. Add a Nigeria-specific title, statutory function or practice variant only when sources directly support it. Label its geography and jurisdiction; do not make local titles the global default.
5. **Keep role existence, taxonomy fit and labour demand distinct.** A source may establish that an occupation exists without proving that it fits a specific domain, is common in Nigeria, or is in demand. Global job evidence is not Nigerian demand evidence; dated Nigerian vacancies can establish occurrence, not by themselves a national shortage or demand level.
6. **Do not fabricate or over-split.** Do not promote a specialization, method, competency, tool, seniority level, work setting or generic task into a role title unless a credible source recognizes that occupation or role family. Do not duplicate one role under several domains without distinct, task-supported contexts.
7. **Assign roles to areas only after the area set is approved.** A role must fit the parent domain by its core tasks and fit its proposed area of specialization independently. If a role legitimately spans multiple specializations, record each supported route and its added learning; otherwise use the single best-supported route or a direct domain-to-role path.

## Required record for each proposed addition or disposition

- Stable role ID or explicitly marked new-role candidate; canonical title and sourced aliases.
- Parent domain and approved area of specialization, with task-level fit rationale.
- Status: globally established, Nigeria-specific variant, global with Nigerian counterpart, or evidence insufficient.
- Core tasks, occupation-vs-specialization determination, and any jurisdiction/licensure boundary.
- Exact source URL, publisher, title, date/access date, and locator or quoted section identifier.
- Whether the evidence establishes existence, domain fit, specialization fit, Nigerian occurrence, or demand—state each separately.
- Duplicate/alias check and disposition for any existing near-match.

## Completion and verification gates

- Reconcile every current domain and every approved specialization against the deterministic sparse-coverage queue; record zero-role and low-coverage cases, including those where the correct disposition is “no distinct role evidenced.”
- Independently verify each new occupation candidate against its source and task description; no auto-promotion from title similarity, keyword match, ISCO group or subgroup count.
- Test exact role-key coverage, stable identifiers, no accidental duplicate roles, parent/area references resolve, and global/Nigerian scope labels do not bleed together.
- Re-run the full role-domain fit and specialization-link audit after additions. New roles do not bypass the existing requirement for defensible role→domain and role→specialization relationships.
- Keep evidence/review artifacts separate from canonical role-library edits until the candidate has passed the source, fit, alias and jurisdiction checks. Then apply the accepted additions locally, regenerate derived outputs and run all relevant audits/tests.
