## Decision rule

A shared/versatile foundation must be evidenced across the relevant downstream paths; broad usefulness, role frequency, a source name, or mere technical possibility is insufficient. Items useful on a subset of paths are additional edges or conditional options. Unresolved source or checkpoint support blocks promotion; it is not a negative finding.
