## Evidence findings and boundaries

The role-list entry and occupation profile establish where the pair was inventoried, not that the item is a task, qualification, or universally required capability. For this lane, the role source is the role's ESCO occupation profile and its associated label. ESCO's concept system identifies concepts with URIs, but the exact linked skill/knowledge URI, concept type and item version were not independently resolved for these pairs. The JSON records preserve that uncertainty instead of substituting a URI based on label similarity. The occupation-profile URI and its ESCO v1.2.1 snapshot are recorded separately from the unresolved item identity.

The records distinguish global evidence from Nigeria-context evidence. Local sources are narrowly described: for example, NBS material is a historical explanation of population data sources, NOUN material is institution-specific course/policy evidence, and Nigerian laws are linked only where a primary official source was located. These sources do not establish national job prevalence or a requirement for every listed role.

Several pairs have an important scope boundary:

- `conduct psychological research` and `experimental psychology` are limited to social-science inquiry where supported. They do not imply clinical assessment, counselling, diagnosis or treatment, and preserve the D078/D064 boundary.
- `forensic psychiatry` is a medical specialty; its presence in the criminologist list does not authorize or establish a D078 task. `forensic anthropology` is treated as specialist human-remains work, not as a synonym for social anthropology or thanatology.
- `human physiology`, `geology`, and `geological time scale` are treated as conditional or out-of-scope unless an independently supported interdisciplinary question needs them. They are not domain foundations.
- `deliver social services in diverse cultural communities` and `create criminal profiles` have material task/authority mismatches or unresolved evidence. Their records explicitly state the missing support and safer research boundary.
- Legal, accounting, finance and regulatory items are jurisdiction-specific. A research item does not confer legal, clinical, investment, enforcement, excavation or professional authority.

Free learning URLs are recorded with the exact destination and locator, and the entry states whether the material directly teaches the task, is introductory/adjacent, or remains a candidate pending access/content confirmation. A course or code is not treated as a credential. Nigeria examples and international/US/UK materials are not silently substituted for one another.
