## Two initial domain-wide evidence leads

1. **Academic teaching, scholarship/research, and educational service are recognized higher-education work functions.** UNESCO defines higher-education teaching personnel to include people engaged to teach, undertake scholarship and/or research, or provide educational services to students or the wider community. This supports investigating these as distinct but connected domain-wide task areas; it does not mean every academic job performs all of them.
   - Source: UNESCO, *Recommendation concerning the Status of Higher-Education Teaching Personnel (1997)* overview.
   - Exact URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/higher-education/1997recommendation
   - Locator: “About the Recommendation”; definition quoting Article I.1.f; sections describing professional responsibilities and scope.
   - Search evidence: web search `site:unesco.org Recommendation status higher education teaching personnel research teaching duties academic freedom` (2026-10-12).
   - Claim limit: international normative framework; not a job description, universal workload allocation, or Nigerian appointment rule.

2. **University academic work is not exhausted by classroom teaching; research, dissemination, and academic freedom are part of its documented scope.** UNESCO’s account of the 1997 Recommendation describes academic freedom in teaching and research, including carrying out research and disseminating/publishing results. This supports a domain-wide research-work candidate requiring more precise task/output decomposition in the full pass.
   - Source: UNESCO, *Protecting academic freedom is as relevant as ever*.
   - Exact URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/protecting-academic-freedom-relevant-ever
   - Locator: discussion of the 1997 Recommendation; paragraphs on freedom in teaching, pursuing research, disseminating results, and choosing current research-informed teaching material.
   - Search evidence: web search `site:unesco.org higher education teaching research academic freedom research duties` (2026-10-12).
   - Claim limit: supports the recognized function and professional conditions, not a complete task inventory or a claim that all staff publish research.
