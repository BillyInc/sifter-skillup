### Sampling and knowing what evidence represents (`sampling_recruitment_and_representativeness`)

**Category:** `useful_additional_edge`. Sampling and case-selection judgement strengthens many empirical routes, but its form depends on the question and evidence source; some roles do not sample people or make population claims.
**Breadth:** Applicable across survey, evaluation, quantitative and qualitative case-selection workflows, but not every D078 role or design.
**Boundary:** Keep probability-sample representativeness distinct from purposive case selection. A case or convenience sample must not be described as representing a wider population without design support.
**Evidence limit:** AAPOR and ESRC support the research-method use; they do not establish a universal task for every occupation. NUC is Nigerian curriculum context only.
**Display/canonical state:** hidden pending full evidence and downstream checkpoint review; not promoted.

### Turning ideas into observable measures (`measurement_and_operationalisation`)

**Category:** `useful_additional_edge`. Turning an abstract construct into observable indicators is valuable when a project measures concepts, especially structured quantitative or instrument-based work, but is not required in the same form by every interpretive or non-empirical route.
**Breadth:** Crosses multiple empirical routes, yet the appropriate form differs by method and construct; not established as an all-path foundation.
**Boundary:** Do not imply that every social concept must be reduced to a numeric measure or that operationalisation replaces qualitative interpretation.
**Evidence limit:** GESIS and the cited research-design teaching support measurement as a methodological concern; route-specific and local curriculum evidence needs exact claim review.
**Display/canonical state:** hidden pending full evidence and downstream checkpoint review; not promoted.

### Checking reliability, validity and uncertainty (`reliability_validity_and_uncertainty`)

**Category:** `useful_additional_edge`. Checking consistency, whether an interpretation is supported, and what uncertainty remains is useful across many evidence-producing routes, but checks differ by method and evidence type.
**Breadth:** Broadly transferable within research/evidence work, not one task or statistic that every D078 role performs.
**Boundary:** Separate measurement reliability, validity of an interpretation, design uncertainty and qualitative quality; no single coefficient certifies evidence quality.
**Evidence limit:** GESIS and the cited methods curricula support these as methodological concerns, not identical requirements across all routes.
**Display/canonical state:** hidden pending full evidence and downstream checkpoint review; not promoted.

### Piloting questions before fieldwork (`pilot_and_cognitive_pretesting`)

**Category:** `conditional_route_choice`. Cognitive pretesting is useful when developing questions or instruments; a pilot tests broader procedures. Neither is a generic requirement for work that does not design or administer an instrument.
**Breadth:** Applies to instrument-building and fieldwork routes; narrower than a shared domain foundation.
**Boundary:** Do not collapse cognitive interviewing into an operational pilot. They answer different questions and can be used separately or together when justified.
**Evidence limit:** GESIS/AAPOR support the distinction; NUC curriculum is a named Nigerian example, not a national or global mandate.
**Display/canonical state:** hidden pending full evidence and downstream checkpoint review; not promoted.

### Focus groups and workshops (`focus_groups_and_workshops`)

**Boundary:** Not a universal interview substitute; a group discussion does not produce representative views by default.
