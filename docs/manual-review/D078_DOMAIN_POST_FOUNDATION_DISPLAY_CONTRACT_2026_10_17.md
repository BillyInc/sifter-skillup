# D078 domain sequence and display contract ? 2026-10-17

This contract has been implemented in the runtime and graph.

## Display

After the programme, the learner completes three compulsory shared foundations:

1. Research question design
2. Evaluate evidence and limits
3. Communicate evidence

The domain then shows separate cards in this order:

- Broad domain sequence: social-world orientation, sources, existing evidence, sampling, measurement, reliability and uncertainty.
- Cross-cutting safeguards and reproducibility.
- Method routes and alternatives.
- Advanced and specialist extensions.
- Supporting tools and tool alternatives.

The learner can stop at the domain. Area selection is not required to discover or study domain knowledge.

## Requirement and dependency rule

The three foundations are needed, have scope_kind shared_domain_foundation, are skippable false and have blocksProgression true. They are displayed in order.

All 49 post-foundation domain records are skippable and nonblocking. Area additions and role additions are also skippable and nonblocking. builds_on, supports, complements and works_with explain the path or co-learning; they do not create blockers. Alternatives are choose-one routes and are not limited to tools.
