# Programme-to-career mapping: Nigeria pricing and revenue scenarios

**Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** market-research memo; not a financial forecast or commitment.

## Product scope

This pricing applies only to the evidence-backed guidance journey:

`programme → domain → area of specialization/practice → role`

It excludes recruitment, jobs, implementation services, entrepreneurship/build
features and school-admin seats.

## PenHills comparison

No current, verifiable public PenHills per-student rate card was found. The
indexed entity appears to be a Lagos board-games organisation, but its identity
may not match the intended comparator:

- [Pen Hills LinkedIn profile](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/pen-hills-56bb1415)

No official programme page, school package, currency, billing period or

PenHills as a pricing benchmark.

## Recommended price

- **Reference institutional price:** ₦10,000 per eligible student per academic
  session.
- **Launch/institutional price:** ₦7,500 per student per academic session.
- **Large-volume price:** ₦6,500 per student at 1,000+ seats.
- **Admin/counsellor accounts:** free or unmetered.

The strongest public Nigerian comparator found was
[CareerPointa](https://www.careerpointa.app/pricing), which lists individual
and school packages from ₦5,500 to ₦12,500 per student, with its premium tier
including university recommendations, learning pathways, employability/salary
insights and a roadmap. This supports ₦7,500 as a realistic launch price and
₦10,000 as a defensible reference price, subject to customer testing.

Other anchors were [Eden Academii](https://www.edenacademii.com/programs) at
₦25,000 for a workshop and [SkulCare](https://skulcare.com/pricing) at
₦500–₦1,000 per student/month for generic school software. Neither is a direct
equivalent.

## Private-school student pool

The latest detailed official state-level source located was NEMIS 2021/2022.
Private senior-secondary enrolment was reported as:

| Market | Private SSS students |
| --- | ---: |
| Lagos | 152,629 |
| Ogun | 206,975 |
| Ondo | 50,106 |
| Osun | 11,736 |
| Oyo | 65,548 |
| Ekiti | 5,916 |
| **South-West total** | **492,910** |

Source: [NEMIS Nigeria Education Digest 2022, Table 4.6](https://nemis.education.gov.ng/NIGERIA%20EDUCATION%20DIGEST%202022%20current.pdf).
NEMIS also reports [4,649 private SSS schools in the South-West](https://nemis.education.gov.ng/visuals/display_school_zone?zone=+South+West)
for that period. These are historical enrolments, not a 2026 forecast.

At ₦7,500 per student, the theoretical pool is ₦1.145bn for Lagos and
₦3.697bn for the South-West. More realistic adoption scenarios are:

| Segment | 5% adoption | 10% adoption |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Lagos private SSS | ₦57.2m | ₦114.5m |
| South-West private SSS | ₦184.8m | ₦369.7m |

These are addressable-seat scenarios, not expected sales.

For current context, the [Lagos 2024/2025 DNEMIS statistics](https://emis.education.gov.ng/portal/asc/state-H2ZhSMudlMI/)
report 11,922 private schools and 1,170,918 private learners across all levels;
that figure must not replace the SSS-only base above.

UNILAG, LASU and OAU together report 111,136 students. At ₦7,500, the
theoretical pool is ₦833.5m; at 5% and 10% adoption, this is approximately
₦41.7m and ₦83.4m. Use an explicitly eligible undergraduate cohort before
quoting a university revenue forecast because these figures may include mixed
undergraduate, postgraduate, distance, sandwich or other categories.

## Interpretation and safeguards

- Count billable SSS students for private-school offers, not all learners.
- Count an agreed eligible university cohort, not total institutional
  enrolment.
- Do not charge additional seats for admins or counsellors unless a separate
  admin product is introduced.
- Validate willingness to pay with pilots, conversion data and institutional
  quotes before treating these as a forecast.
- Recheck prices, enrolments, links and access dates before commercial launch.
