## Research and integration focus

Respect the distinctions among experience/content design; software and real-time applications; devices, tracking and platform engineering; and human factors, accessibility, safety and evaluation. Check learning items across the actual workflow: user/experience goals; interaction and spatial design; content/asset pipeline; runtime, rendering and input; tracking/calibration and device/platform constraints; deployment/performance; comfort, accessibility and safety; and testing/evaluation. These are gap prompts, not a compulsory list.

Describe platform- and device-specific tools in context, including their supported inputs, outputs and limitations. Distinguish XR interaction techniques from SDKs and engines, tracking algorithms from sensor hardware, spatial audio from content production, and accessibility/evaluation protocols from compliance labels. Emerging devices or interfaces must be clearly labeled by maturity and evidence type. A prototype or lab paper does not establish widespread production practice.

For safety and human factors, record the population, exposure/task, evaluation design, setting and limitations; do not universalize a small usability study. Include accessibility and inclusive design where supported by standards and research. For cross-area handoffs, document how design/content outputs feed implementation, how tracking/device outputs constrain interaction, and how evaluation results feed design or release decisions. Only mark dependency where the workflow requires it.
