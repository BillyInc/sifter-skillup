### Keep D086 distinct from video games and esports

D086 is the **gambling industry**: casino/venue operations, betting and wagering, lotteries/bingo/draws, and gambling products or systems. It is not the video-game creation domain (D139) and is not esports competition, events, teams, broadcasts or creator work (D168). Use “gambling” or the specific activity in student-facing wording; do not use the bare word “gaming” where it could mean video games. Shared software, design, payments, media or analytics can be linked only when a source establishes the actual cross-domain task.

### Student-suitable content rule

Keep occupational learning realistic, lawful, non-vulgar, non-exploitative and appropriate for students. Exclude instructions or promotion for illegal gambling, fraud, match/draw manipulation, evading age checks or regulation, targeting minors, predatory customer acquisition, or manipulating people into gambling; exclude vulgar or sexually explicit examples. Do not provide betting picks, gambling strategies for personal profit, or encouragement to gamble. Do include factual, age-appropriate learning about the industry's real operations and risks: responsible-gambling controls, player protection, age safeguards, integrity, fraud/AML controls, regulatory duties, product mathematics, testing and system operations where sources support them. Explain harms and limits accurately; do not sanitize the industry's risks or imply gambling is safe or profitable. If a source contains unsuitable material but supports a legitimate occupational claim, cite only the relevant factual portion and do not reproduce the unsuitable content.

## Research and integration focus

Build a precise separation among venue/casino operations, wagering and sportsbook operations, lottery/bingo/draw operations, and gaming product/system design or operation. Where an activity overlaps, preserve its context and avoid treating the areas as synonyms. Check evidence for the operational lifecycle, customer/player protection, integrity and fraud controls, responsible gambling, payments, data/security, event/draw integrity, odds or game mathematics, product testing, platform/system operations, and relevant jurisdictional regulation. These are prompts for gap checking, not a requirement to add every subject to every area.

This domain is jurisdiction-sensitive. For each legal or regulatory claim, state the jurisdiction, regulator, instrument/version/date, activity covered, and whether the cited material is binding, guidance, proposed, or industry practice. Do not generalize one jurisdiction's rules to the domain as a whole. Separate legal compliance from technical controls and professional competencies. Distinguish probability/odds models, gambling-product design, risk management, and tools that implement or monitor them. Preserve the distinction between game-of-chance product design in D086 and video-game design in D139.

For role context, map source-supported tasks to the appropriate area items and describe when a cross-area role route applies. A title mention alone is insufficient: record the concrete task and operating context. Credentials should be listed only when the source establishes their relevance, jurisdiction and issuer; do not imply universal licensing.
