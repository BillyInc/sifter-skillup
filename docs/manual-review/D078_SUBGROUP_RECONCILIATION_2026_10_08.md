# D078 area-of-specialization/subgroup reconciliation — 2026-10-08

## Decision

The saved D078 evidence supports a provisional seven-area structure. The two legacy subgroup records should not remain as two additional visible areas:

- **Computational and Quantitative Social Research** is absorbed into **Computational and Digital Social Science**. The older label is retained only as provenance because “quantitative” and “computational” describe approaches, while the newer area has the clearer social-inquiry and digital-society boundary.
- **Social, Cultural & Economic Research** is retired as a standalone area. It combines several different scopes and overlaps the discipline areas and the cross-cutting research/evaluation area. It remains a historical alias; this review does not redistribute its roles.

This is a structural recommendation, not a canonical taxonomy edit or a student-facing approval.

## Reconciliation criteria

Each proposed area was checked against D078’s current definition: the study of behaviour, societies and institutions, and the effects of social, economic, political and environmental conditions, using evidence and social-science methods to understand issues and inform policy.

An area is retained only when it:

1. fits that definition;
2. has a distinguishable boundary from sibling areas;
3. is supported by authoritative global, academic or professional evidence;
4. is an area of specialization/practice rather than a method, tool, skill or role family; and
5. keeps Nigerian context as a separately labelled enrichment layer rather than narrowing the global definition.

## Legacy subgroup decisions

### Computational and Quantitative Social Research

**Decision: merge and rename into Computational and Digital Social Science.** The legacy record is too narrow if treated as a separate area because quantitative and computational approaches can support economics, psychology, evaluation, population studies and other routes. It is also too broad if interpreted as a universal requirement. The merged area keeps the social question, evidence, theory and ethics at the centre and treats Python, R, SPSS, Stata, NVivo, Mesa, NetworkX and similar items as conditional tools or methods.

### Social, Cultural & Economic Research

**Decision: retire as a standalone visible area and replace it with the synthesized seven-area set.** Its label has no single stable boundary: “social,” “cultural” and “economic” work spans multiple disciplines and the applied research/evaluation practice. Keeping it would duplicate the proposed areas and make the learner-facing structure unclear. Preserve it as historical provenance only. No role is moved, removed or approved by this document.

## Evidence basis

- UNESCO’s ISCED field descriptions distinguish social and behavioural sciences, economics, political science, psychology, sociology/cultural studies, demography and social geography, while recording boundaries with physical geography, law, journalism and social work.
- UNESCO’s social-science R&D definition supports an interdisciplinary umbrella containing distinct disciplinary fields and qualitative/quantitative inquiry.
- The International Sociological Association’s Digital Sociology working group supports digital social inquiry as a recognized research focus spanning theoretical, empirical, methodological and socioethical work.
- ESRC research-training guidance supports treating research design, method choice, sampling, analysis and interpretation as research practice with specialist depth varying by subject; it does not require turning each method into a separate area.
