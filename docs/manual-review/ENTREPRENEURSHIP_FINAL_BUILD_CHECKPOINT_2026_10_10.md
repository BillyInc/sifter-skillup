## Reality decisions

- Nigerian evidence is added when it changes local viability, regulation, cost,
  demand, trust, funding or adaptation. Its absence does not invalidate a
  genuinely global pattern.
- Bounded search results use not_found_in_scope rather than absolute absence.
- NUC CCMAS supports programme and discipline context, not proof of business
  viability.
- CAC supports registration and registry claims, not total market absence.
- BOI supports funding-route information, not guaranteed eligibility or award.
- NDPC requirements constrain personal, cohort and behavioural data.
- Raw percentages, counts, market claims and “nothing exists” statements stay
  hypotheses until independently verified and dated.
