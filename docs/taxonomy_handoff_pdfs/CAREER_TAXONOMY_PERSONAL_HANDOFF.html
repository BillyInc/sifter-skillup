2. The three things that must stay separate

Layer key Definition Example Evidence location
LAYER-REFERENCE A pointer to a source URL, file, page, section, or row locator Source registry and source traceability
LAYER-CONTENT What the source actually says Course outcome, task, occupation title, rule, or extracted competency CCMAS and entity audit JSON
LAYER-DECISION Why Sifter uses the content for a relationship Direct, functional, adjacent, subgroup, role, or explicit limitation Mapping and disposition artifacts

Never describe a URL alone as proof of a mapping. Never use global content as Nigerian demand evidence. Never turn an unresolved or unavailable state into an invented local title, licence, credential, or market claim.

Subject matcher handoff

The complete source and behavior inventory is in CAREER_TAXONOMY_SOURCE_INVENTORY_2026-09-01.md. The executable matcher has three separate modes:

Choose mode→JAMB-only, O-level-only, or combined?→Exactly four UTME subjects→Five to allowed O-level maximum→Four UTME plus five to allowed O-level maximum→Typed UTME rules only→Typed O-level rules only→Match both layers independently→Intersect same canonical JAMB programme ID→Attach university offerings→Show matches, gaps, source wording, and limitation
View diagram definition
flowchart TD
  A[Choose mode] --> B{JAMB-only, O-level-only, or combined?}
  B -->|JAMB-only| C[Exactly four UTME subjects]
  B -->|O-level-only| D[Five to allowed O-level maximum]
  B -->|Combined| E[Four UTME plus five to allowed O-level maximum]
  C --> F[Typed UTME rules only]
  D --> G[Typed O-level rules only]
  E --> H[Match both layers independently]
  H --> I[Intersect same canonical JAMB programme ID]
  F --> J[Attach university offerings]
  G --> J
  I --> J
  J --> K[Show matches, gaps, source wording, and limitation]
  • JAMB-only: Use of English plus three other UTME subjects; match only utme rules and their required/one-of groups.
  • O-level-only: At least five O-level subjects; match only olevel rules and enforce the source's named credits and minimum where structured.
  • Combined: Match UTME and O-level independently, intersect by the same canonical JAMB programme ID, and report O-level gaps instead of flattening both exams into one score.

The matcher fails closed if the subject catalogue, structured options, typed rule records, programme ID, requirement ID, exam type, or source text do not agree. It does not turn an unparsed brochure sentence into an exact match. University rows are attached after the canonical programme match and add address, requirements, and notes; they do not create a new programme version.

Worked example: with UTME ENG, CHEM, BIO, PHY, a rule requiring CHEM and BIO plus one of MATH/PHY matches. With O-level ENG, BIO, MATH, CHEM, GEO plus another subject, a rule requiring the four named credits plus one of GEO/HSC/PHY/STAT matches. In combined mode, the programme is retained only when its same JAMB ID appears in both sets; missing O-level credits remain visible as a gap. See the academic worked examples and matcher audit.
